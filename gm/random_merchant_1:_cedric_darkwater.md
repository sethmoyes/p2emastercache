*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This curious half-elf healer grew up among peasants, working the land. Their parents were yeomen. In their youth, they grew up during wartime. They was influenced by a holy person who greatly influenced them. At one point, they lied to further their goals, motivated by pressured. Now they are traveling to Otari for work.*

**Proprietor:** Cedric Darkwater (Half-Elf Healer)

**Specialties:** Weapons and armor
