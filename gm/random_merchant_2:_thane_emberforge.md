*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This confident gnome priest was born to privilege among the nobility. Their parents were entertainers. In their youth, they was imprisoned for a crime. They knew an athletic or competitive person who greatly influenced them. At one point, they stole something valuable, motivated by religion. Now they are looking for employment.*

**Proprietor:** Thane Emberforge (Gnome Priest)

**Specialties:** General goods and supplies
