# Random Merchant 2: Brenna Nightshade

<div align="center">

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/62/Brenna.png" alt="Proprietor" width="250">

</div>

*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This confident gnome scribe was born to privilege among the nobility. Their parents were serfs/peasants. In their youth, they grew up during wartime. They had a significant romantic relationship who greatly influenced them. At one point, they publicly embarrassed someone, motivated by pressured. Now they are searching for a lost relative.*

**Proprietor:** Brenna Nightshade (Gnome Scribe)

**Specialties:** Alchemical items and potions
