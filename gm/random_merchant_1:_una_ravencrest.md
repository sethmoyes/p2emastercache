*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This proud half-elf healer raised by another family. Their parents were clergy. In their youth, they was mentored by a skilled teacher. They knew an athletic or competitive person who greatly influenced them. At one point, they lied to further their goals, motivated by money. Now they are running from the law.*

**Proprietor:** Una Ravencrest (Half-Elf Healer)

**Specialties:** Alchemical items and potions
