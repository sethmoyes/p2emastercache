*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This cowardly dwarf innkeeper was born to privilege among the nobility. Their parents were thieves. In their youth, they was betrayed by a trusted friend or family member. They befriended a traveling merchant or minstrel who greatly influenced them. At one point, they lied to further their goals, motivated by family. Now they are running from the law.*
