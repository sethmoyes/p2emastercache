# Adding Otari Shop Items to Pathbuilder

Pathbuilder 2e's item list is missing many official items, so something you
buy in Otari may not be there when you go to add it to your character. Every
shop page has an **Add to Pathbuilder** button that fixes this: it downloads
the items you pick as a Pathbuilder **custom pack**, a small file Pathbuilder
can import.

## You need Pathbuilder's paid version

Importing a custom pack is a paid Pathbuilder feature. The free version can't
open the file. The web app and the phone app are unlocked separately, so you
need the paid version on whichever one you import with.

## 1. Download the items

1. Open the shop you bought from and tap **Add to Pathbuilder**.
2. Everything in the shop is ticked. Untick what you didn't buy, or untick
   **All items** and tick just your purchases.
3. Tap **Download**. You get a file named like `otari-wrins_wonders-pathbuilder.json`.

## 2. Import it into Pathbuilder

**Web app (pathbuilder2e.com) and iPhone/iPad:**

1. Open the menu (top left) and choose **Custom Pack**.
2. Tap **Import Custom Pack** at the bottom of the window.
3. Pick the file you downloaded.
4. Check the list of items and tap **Accept**, then let Pathbuilder refresh.

**Android:**

1. On the home screen, tap **App Options**, then **Custom Packs**.
2. Tap **Import Custom Pack** at the top.
3. Pick the file from your Downloads folder and tap **Continue**, then **Finished**.

## 3. Add the items to your character

Open your character's inventory and add the items as you would any other
gear. Search by name: imported items sit alongside Pathbuilder's own.

## Good to know

- **One pack for all of Otari.** Every shop's download goes into the same
  pack, "Otari Shops (Abomination Vaults)". Importing a newer download adds its
  items to that pack and updates the ones already there, so you don't end up
  with duplicate packs.
- **Weapons, armor and shields come with their numbers.** Damage, group,
  traits, AC, Dex cap, check penalty, Hardness and HP are filled in, so
  Pathbuilder can do the math. Runes are not built in: add potency, striking
  and property runes with Pathbuilder's own rune options.
- **Everything else comes with its rules text** from the Archives of Nethys,
  plus a link to the full entry.
- **Prices are what the shop charged** when you downloaded the file.

## How spell scrolls work

A scroll in your inventory is for casting. Learning its spell is a separate
step.

- **Casting it:** if the spell is on your spell list, you can cast it from the
  scroll once, using your own spell attack modifier and spell DC. The scroll is
  then used up. If the spell isn't on your list, you need Trick Magic Item.
- **Learning it:** a caster of the spell's tradition can use Learn a Spell on
  the scroll (1 hour, plus materials by the spell's rank), and the scroll isn't
  used up.
  - **Wizards and magi** write it into their spellbook.
  - **Witches** teach it to their familiar.
  - **Bards, oracles, psychics and sorcerers** can't add it to their
    repertoire on the spot. Learning it makes it a choice the next time they
    add or swap a repertoire spell, at a new level or by retraining.
  - **Clerics and druids** can already prepare any common spell of their
    tradition.

If you learn a spell, add it to your spellbook or repertoire in Pathbuilder
yourself; the scroll stays in your inventory until you cast it.
