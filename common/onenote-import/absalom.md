ABSALOM: THE MASTER GAZETTEER & VISCERAL COMPENDIUM


I. THE OUTER DISTRICTS & SUBURBS


1. The Suburbs (Shoreline, Westerhold, Copperwood, & Dawnfoot)

- The Visceral Feel: Heavy with woodsmoke, fresh sawdust, tanneries, and water-mill pulp. Rhythmic pounding of smithies and axes; dust kicked up by endless trade wagons. Grounded, industrious, and perpetually living in the literal shadow of the outer walls.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Shoreline: Industrial town outside the walls with paper mills, tanneries, and breweries.
    - Pyramid of the Dog: Dilapidated siege castle serving as headquarters for the Warhounders gang led by Nessian.
  - Westerhold: 2,000-year-old Dwarven-founded roadside settlement.
    - Crystal Creations: Glassblowing shop serving as the secret surface entrance to Cowsworn Thieves Guild tunnels.
  - Copperwood: Ancient laborer encampment turned trades town; residents ("Copperheads") work as guides/porters.
  - Dawnfoot: Military family town built around Starwatch Keep.
    - Dawn Castle: Converted 1298 AR siege castle used as town hall and military graduation hall.
- Key NPCs: Nessian (Warhounder boss); Deepkeg Family descendants.
- Local Drama: Smuggling through subterranean Dwarven tunnels beneath Crystal Creations; Warhounder banditry creeping in from the Puddles.

2. Starwatch Keep & Pilot Island

- The Visceral Feel: Freezing salt spray, ozone, and roaring waves. Shouted naval commands and screeching military griffons overhead. Warmth from a blinding 655-foot beam sweeping across the harbor.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Pilot Island & Absalom Lighthouse: Tallest structure in Absalom (655 ft), forged by Aroden.
  - Starwatch Keep: Massive naval base and investigation hub isolated during peacetime.
  - Walled Docks & Aviaries: Houses Wave-Riders (hippocampus cavalry) and military griffon squadrons.
- Key NPCs: Harbor Master Adriel Nepri (High Council member).
- Local Drama: Anti-piracy operations, harbor pilot escort laws, and naval defense during sieges.

3. The Flotsam Graveyard

- The Visceral Feel: Gag-inducing rot of kelp, waterlogged oak, and stagnant brine. Groaning timbers and black water slapping against thousands of sunken warships.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Shifting Labyrinth: Shifting underwater maze of 4,000 years of sunken siege fleets.
  - Azarketi Enclaves & Reefs: Coral-covered wrecks inhabited by Azarketi, grindylows, and sea monsters.
- Key NPCs: Salvagers Guild divers, Harbor Pilots.
- Local Drama: Illegal crossings carry total forfeiture of ship and cargo to the Primarch. Salvagers Guild constantly clears pathways while scuttling confiscated ships.

4. Fort Tempest & Azlanti Keep

- The Visceral Feel: Dry ozone, stone dust, and old blood. Cavernous white stone walls forged by Aroden that swallow sound and intimidate mortals.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Fort Tempest: Converted siege castle of Carass the Angel Binder with reinforced Dwarven cliff-foundations.
  - Azlanti Keep: Ancient fortress with radiant crystal Arches built by Aroden; features 12 legendary magical siege defenses.
- Key NPCs: Sergeant Salif Deepkeg (Fort Tempest); First Gear Simo (Azlanti Keep); The Varlock (magical defenders).
- Local Drama: Unlocking forgotten Arodenite siege defenses like the Crucible Directive.

II. THE CITY DISTRICTS


1. The Wise Quarter (Academic & Arcane Hub)

- The Visceral Feel: Old parchment, myrrh, and ozone. Scratching quills, hushed debates in marble halls, and looming pyramid libraries.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Forae Logos: World’s largest library with columns depicting the 12 Guises of Aroden.
  - Protectorate Anthology: Learned Guard HQ at the base of Forae Logos stairs.
  - Arcanamirium: Arcane university founded by Nex.
  - Absalom Mint: Fortress producing all official coinage (Penny, Weight, Measure, Sphinx).
  - Conclave Dome: Grand Council meeting hall with 49 stone thrones for the Low Council.
  - Absalom Menagerie: Public park with magically climate-controlled Wall-of-Force animal enclosures.
  - Black Rose Museum: Dimensional-folding museum run by Nigel Aldain housing the Shadow Obelisk.
  - Primarch’s Residence: Ancient 2,000-year-old tyrant's estate currently locked down after Primarch Gyr's disappearance.
  - Palace of 13 Spires: Foreign dignitaries' luxury rental estate with Chelish, Taldan, Osiriani, and Minkai wings.
  - Silk Castle: Ancient kite shop run by Vitar Kusek; hosts the Festival of Ribbons.
  - Learned Pig: Taproom stuffed with thousands of student books and scrolls.
  - Scrivener’s Square & The Inkwell: Print industry hub centered on a public fountain.
  - Temple of Sivanah: Illusionist temple whose colors and architecture subtly shift weekly.
  - Salhar Estate: Osiriani ambassador estate.
  - Withun House: Drumac Kalistrade business academy secret indoctrination center.
  - Other Institutions: Absalom Academy of Law, Athanaeum Museum, Dotus Academy, Gallery Rousta.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Dawan Tour; Archmage Lady Dariana; Nigel Aldain; Guard Captain Menier "The Colossus"; Lord Kirkus of House Deak (Exchequer); Drem Salhar.
- Local Drama: High taxes on students; Druid protests at the Menagerie; the search for missing Primarch Gyr inside his sealed manor.

2. The Petal District (Elite Nobility)

- The Visceral Feel: Intoxicating perfumes of rare orchids, string music, marble fountains, and gilded gates. Suffocating judgment from generational wealth.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Hot House: Glasshouse serving as the district council seat (Conclave of Flowers).
  - Ankhamon Estate: Lush Osiriani villa; conceals the sub-basement sanctum of ancient ghoul Mother Jackal and the Eaters of Knowledge cult.
  - Castle Blackrose: Imposing black fortress housing house treasures.
  - College of Mysteries: Oldest magical academy with a giant glass dome ("The Lens").
  - Silken Court: High-end Calistrian temple/brothel run by Lady Dariana.
  - Mirel Palace & The Tally Wall: Death-trap estate of House Mirel; outer wall features a tally of dead thieves.
  - Court of Black Paper: Norgorberite public temple and elite law school.
  - Palace Ormuz: Sprawling estate housing Alchemical labs and a reactivated Jhasine War Machine.
  - Spiral Cross Cemetery: Ancient cemetery guarded by Pharasmins and Mother Jackal.
  - Statue of Primarch Harvey: Notorious, awkward statue depicting a constipated hero.
  - Hillview Station: Lotus Guard HQ and high-profile courthouse with balcony galleries.
  - Madani Manor: Dean of the Arcanamirium's 3-story garden mansion.
  - Other Estates: Deak Palace, Erastil Hunting Club, Fiddlemourne Manor, Lantern Lodge (Tian-Xia PS Lodge), Magpie Manor, Yury Manor Museum (Unsolved Murder Manor), Crescent Manor (Grandmaster Torch's vacant estate).
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Lord Uran Ormuz; Lady Hamaria Blackrose; Captain Folend Mirel (werewolf guard chief); Lord Kalo Mirel; Lord Yamathar Ormuz.
- Local Drama: The political war over replacing the Harvey statue; Mother Jackal’s secret cult operating beneath noble galas; mysterious lights in missing Grandmaster Torch’s Crescent Manor.

3. The Ivy District (Arts, Bards & Counterculture)

- The Visceral Feel: Oil paint, woodsmoke, blooming ivy, street lutes, and theatrical soliloquies. Restless, romantic, and rebellious energy.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Chamber Grove: Converted theater serving as district council HQ.
  - The Ivy Playhouse: Grand theater & Actors Guild HQ.
  - The White Grotto: Premier Bardic College with an outdoor amphitheater.
  - Bloom Cabaret: Chic cafe hosting the secret "Order of Good Standing".
  - Sundown Street: Row of hyper-exclusive tailors (Ambrose Black, Alamir Kai, Madame Theodore).
  - Flower Street Market: Vibrant market patrolled by Abadaran paladins.
  - Ivy District Park & Topiary Menagerie: Vast woods with a 9ft topiary hedge mage and Sacred Oak Grove shrine to Shelyn.
  - Wandering Monster Park: Leshy-run independent park and counterculture amphitheater.
  - Thistle Guard Station: District watch HQ focused only on violent crime.
  - Vault of Abadar: Private bank and temple hit by a recent major heist.
  - Tempest: Illusory-shifting nightclub housing a Zon-Kuthonite priest upstairs.
  - Other Venues: Alysia’s Tea House, Embre’s Armory, Gold Serpent Restaurant, Gutless Griffin Tavern, House of Healing (Sarenrae), Ravounel Consulate, Starpine Manor, Daelan Manor, It Sparkles Jewelry, Kyonin Embassy, Northman’s Woodworks.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Alan Allseasons (Alan Alwayss); High Priestess Jocelyn Fier; Engleton Embre; Hans the Northman; Jambar Dusky Shankle.
- Local Drama: The "Brattle Bunch" leshy gang vandalizing theaters with moss graffiti; an alien entity possessing craftsmen via stolen rubies from the Racked Rock.

4. Westgate (Conservative Middle Class & Trade)

- The Visceral Feel: Fresh bread, cobblestones, and iron gates. Orderly, traditional, quiet, and fiercely middle-class.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Misthyll Hall: Honeycombed council hall with a central assembly amphitheater.
  - Sally Port: Massive western gatehouse managing trade with Otari and the hinterlands.
  - Guild of Wonders: House Mirel's taxidermy shop fronting an elite assassin & espionage academy.
  - House of Seven Faces: Desna's temple featuring 7 mysterious ancient faces.
  - Statue Street: Site of the cruel historical "Charge of the Gorgon" games; now a sanctuary for the destitute.
  - Tomb of the Living: Secret, dark dungeon prison disguised as a noble crypt.
  - Father’s Forge: Torag’s primary Smithy temple featuring the "Founders Wall".
  - Fierce Stripe: Shop catering to Westgate's bizarre obsession with pet Badgers.
  - Groggy Froggy: Tavern famous for the petrified statue of its former owner.
  - Metic Ringer Sanitarium: Psychiatric facility holding secret sin-organ brain experiments.
  - Other POIs: Churren Dispatch (Mobile doctor service), Anandar Block (Vudrani Enclave), Cavel Detective Agency, To Eat the World (Spectacle restaurant), Westgate Heritage Museum, Windarium (Clockwork & Badger shop), Menames Manor, Caza Manor, Westgate Bathhouse.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Lady Selene of Deak; Venla Caza ("Battling Belle"); Erdan Sanl (Detective); Lord Rogen Menames.
- Local Drama: Statue heads being vandalized/stolen off Statue Street; the petrified owner of Groggy Froggy going missing; unspeakable dungeon experiments inside the Sanitarium basement.

5. The Foreign Quarter (Global Enclaves & Gladiators)

- The Visceral Feel: Roasting garlic, foreign spices, sweat, and cheap ale. A dozen languages blaring against the distant roar of 50,000 arena fans.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Irorium: The largest gladiatorial arena in the world with 200ft walls.
  - Grand Lodge: World headquarters of the Pathfinder Society.
  - Utterhome: HQ of the Sleepless Suns (District Watch).
  - New Thassilon Embassy: Bipartisan embassy with Karzoug-loyal and Nocticula-loyal wings.
  - Five Fire Pavilion: Official Goblin Embassy founded by Skira Toadlicker.
  - Tiantown: Massive cultural enclave for refugees from Imperial Lung Wa.
  - Wounded Wisp: Historic dive bar where the Pathfinder Society was founded.
  - Crimson Coin: Bare-knuckle brawl tavern and illegal arena betting house.
  - Black Blades Academy: Open-yard swordplay school run by Benal Blackblade.
  - School of the North Song Wind: Open-air Magaambya outdoor magic school.
  - Other Enclaves: Devil’s Garden (Chelish Enclave), Green Star Market (Lirgeni/Yamasan Enclave), House of Shade and Grace (Thuvian Inn), Lion Square (Taldan Saronite Enclave), Seal of Cazal (Arcadian Shrine), Flying Alderman (Pathfinder Safehouse), Fenworth Game Emporium.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Torman Ahtate (Obese ex-gladiator carried by 8 half-orc women); Ganen of House Calthlen (Corrupt Arena Master); Benal Blackblade.
- Local Drama: Rigged arena matches controlled by Ganen; cultural clashes between Chelish Exiles and new Thassilonian agents.

6. The Ascendant Court (Religious Capital)

- The Visceral Feel: Choking clouds of frankincense and myrrh. Ringing bells, crying pilgrims, and the terrifying divine weight of the Starstone Chasm.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Starstone Cathedral: The god-forged cathedral sitting over a lethal chasm.
  - Avenue of the Hopeful: Thoroughfare where aspirants perform street-miracles before attempting the test.
  - Gods’ Market: Ancient plaza selling genuine holy relics and religious frauds.
  - Shrine of the Failed: Crypt honoring dead aspirants who almost reached godhood.
  - Caden’s Hall: Tavern-temple built on the exact spot Caden Cailean got drunk and ascended.
  - Bank of Absalom: Global banking house controlling regional interest rates.
  - Seventh Church of Iomedae & Tempering Hall: White marble cathedral and Paladin training ground.
  - Blackfinger Temple & The Black Mask: Public Norgorberite poison-lab temple and front costume shop.
  - Stoneshield House: Agnostic Gray Cloaks Watch HQ.
  - Tower of Twin Truths: 250ft Nethysian tower celebrating non-duality.
  - Garrick’s Workshop: Maker of Garrick’s Liquid Courage (sold to doomed god-aspirants).
  - Other Temples: Asari Palace (Arodenite relic house), Chelish Grand Embassy / Church of Asmodeus, Gorameric (Gorum Blood-Arena), Pleasure Salon of Calistria, Temple of the Shining Star (Sarenrae).
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Siyoy of the Thousand Poems; Arc-Banker Vclaw of Brevoy; Jonas Flackfatter (Norgorber High Priest); High Priest Tear Falbane; Watch-Overlord Ulun II.
- Local Drama: Fraudulent "Catacomb Coins" sold to collectors; betting rings at Pit View Pub pricing the deaths of starstone jumpers.

7. The Coins (Mercantile Capital & Underworld)

- The Visceral Feel: Copper, sweat, haggling, clinking scales, and aggressive mercenaries. Crammed market stalls blocking out the sun.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Grand Bazaar: Sprawling market containing Jokori’s Sensory Emporium, Lost & Found, and the illegal Red Silk Route.
  - Hall of Commerce & Foreign Coin Exchange: Bureaucratic trade HQ.
  - Clockwork Cathedral: Gothic mechanical research academy created overnight from an iron ship by "The Assembler".
  - Gallery of Innovation: Brigh’s temple-workshop run by a gnome freezing her own bleaching inside mechanical armor.
  - Band of the Palm: Secret HQ of the Bloody Barbers thieves guild led by a 9ft experimental troll.
  - Bailer House: Token Guard HQ known for lenient bail systems.
  - Saucy Wench: Relaxed dive bar owned off-duty by Guard Captain Kaies Finch.
  - Humbolt Outfitters: Massive 2-story fortress general store.
  - Javelin Gallery: Mercenary HQ for the Guild of Spears.
  - Mother Sphinx: Commodity front for Norgorber’s Smoke Knights guild.
  - Toran Embassy: Minotaur embassy promoting district improvements.
  - Other Venues: Grand Dance Hall of Kortos, Eternal Shackle, The Kennel (Protection racket front), Prophet's Academy, Rhett’s Home Orphanage, Second Labyrinth Gambling Den, Temple of Lost Coins, Free Union HQ, Mudhaven / Guiding Hand, Weyll Estate.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Lady Milina Arnson; Captain Kaies Finch; Dr. Benci (Troll thief-lord); High Priestess Finish Tanir; Prince Noir Spirit-Skin (Minotaur).
- Local Drama: Minotaurs from the hinterlands seeking to reclaim stones from the Eternal Shackle; child thieves operating out of the abandoned Crystal Palace.

8. Eastgate (Druids, Parks & Old Lore)

- The Visceral Feel: Pine needles, wet earth, and ancient silence. Massive trees crushing stone manors, with birds chirping and fey eyes watching from shadows.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Grand Halt: Ancient 17-trunk fig tree where Aroden rested; home to Dryad Queen Iann.
  - Gilltown: Run-down surface shacks hiding massive Azarketi underwater crystal/smuggling caverns.
  - Watchtower: Tallest defensive spire; HQ of the Eagle Garrison.
  - Wonderveil / The Vampire Towers: Abandoned world's fair grounds with floating towers swarming with dusk-bats.
  - Talevan School: Moral civics academy run by a reincarnated Chelish Thrun agent disguised as an elf.
  - Broken Bastion: Spell-Lord Maw-Goth Fellow's containment vault for dangerous artifacts.
  - Blue Tower: Communication tower for the Winged Sandal Messengers.
  - Evergreen Park: Shrine of Gozreh run by the fanatic "Children of Spring" cult.
  - Water Cleft: Cliffside aqueduct fountain serving as a major neighborhood social hub.
  - Other POIs: Eastgate Hall, Antler Lodge, Bagel’s Fur & Feathers, Black Hills Meat House, Andoran School, Yargos Gill Estate, Mama Shog’s Solutions (Lashunta witchcraft), Postern Gate, Turning Leaf Restaurant, House of Guile, Mersine Manor.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Lord Aunga of House Ofes; Maw-Goth Fellow; Dryad Queen Iann; Madame Vevrus (Corsava Thrun); Captain Maren Kinsgate.
- Local Drama: The "Children of Spring" cult driving urbanites crazy with primitivist proselytizing; lawyers fraudulently transferring villain cases to Eastgate’s lenient courthouses.

9. The Puddles (The Sunken Slum)

- The Visceral Feel: Ankle-deep brackish sewage, rotting fish, and mould. Sloshing water, coughing beggars, precariously leaning buildings, and desperate faces.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Stilt House: Raised HQ for the Salvagers Guild and the ruling Muckruckers crime gang.
  - The Siphons: Dangerous, flooded subterranean tunnel network controlled by the Sewer Rats gang.
  - Razor Hall: Former courthouse converted into a Skinsaw Cult execution trial house.
  - Asepia’s Astounded Abyss: Freakshow carnival funded by the Fishhead Queen (cursed mermaid).
  - Goblin King’s Court: Elevated rooftop canopy kingdom ruled by King Zusgut.
  - Chapel of Shadow: Flooded nethysian temple converted into an active Onyx Alliance shadow-rift portal.
  - Healing Raft: Floating soup kitchen/medical barge pushed into the mire every other day by Sarenites.
  - Brine Prison: Submerged, cruel lockup run as a front for the Salt Cartel.
  - Other POIs: Catwalk Corridors (Rooftop message runners), Bark Bazaar, Consulate of the Platinum Band (Apsu Temple), Little Inner Sea, Pure Water Home (Firebrand Orphanage), Soggy Piper Tavern, Yargo’s Mission, Metro Cathedral.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Hagen Topkick; King Zusgut; Father Elar Kelm; Yelmo the Despondent.
- Local Drama: Father Elar Kelm hiding a strange, leathery humanoid cocoon that washed in with the tide; gang wars between the Muckruckers and Warhounders.

10. The Docks (Maritime Trade & Pirates)

- The Visceral Feel: Biting tar, brine, and unwashed sailors. Creaking rigging, roaring stevedores, and the grinding iron gears of "The Beast."
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - The Beast: 5-story rotating crane tower lifting 60-ton loads off ships.
  - Crestwatch: Administrative hub housing Harbor Guard and Doc Council.
  - Black Whale Prison: 6 galleons lashed to a reef holding political prisoners.
  - Devil’s Own Shipyard: War-galleon shipyard housing the Chelish Guio Sisters gang.
  - Grog Pit: Notorious flop-house featuring an open barrel of free-for-all Grog.
  - Basara’s Boardwalk: Carnival boardwalk featuring Impalement Arts.
  - Ogrekin Hall & Red Drake Warehouse: Heavy hauling business employing giants and ogres.
  - Temple Row: Maritime shrines (Gozreh, Besmara, Hei Feng) featuring the Thirsty Giant Tavern.
  - Other Shipyards & POIs: Seafoam Conclave (Underwater cave community), Broadfoot Marshals (Dwarven firemen), Concurrent Currency, Pickled Imp Curio Shop, Lifter’s Mooring Union, Medlar Chef (Firebrand HQ), Sea King’s Shipyard, Bare Barges, Marge’s Masts / Meishin & Shen / Pride of Air / Cortos Watercraft / Princely Shipyards.
- Key NPCs: Nomarch Lord Aric of House Durian; Captain Elmoira "Tackle Queen" Taggart; Chief Engineer Amazia Manaha; Warden Gearen of Gix; Tarik Diaran ("Second Harbor Master").
- Local Drama: Tarik Diaran attempting to buy out the entire docks to form a corporate trade empire; ghost ship Ambrosia returning to port with its entire crew missing.

11. The Precipice Quarter (The Earthquake Ruins)

- The Visceral Feel: Acrid ash, dry decay, howling winds through shattered mansion frames, and cold haunted dread over crumbling cliffs.
- Locations & Landmarks:
  - Vigil’s Hope: Fortified former armory serving as HQ for Watcher-Lord Ulun II and the Knights of Lastwall.
  - Beldrin’s Towers: Three wizard spires (Broken Shield standing, Candelabra submerged, Horn half-sunken).
  - Bone Glutton Pit: Emergency rubble/corpse dump turned Ghoul and Urgathoa cult shrine.
  - The Dark Gate: Fissure to the Darklands defended by Turgoff’s Nailfists gang.
  - The Drownyard: Necromantic flooded schoolyard undergoing reclamation.
  - Arborium Arcanis: Blighted, mutated crystal-greenhouse ruled by corrupted Goran druid Kaia.
  - Spiral Shrine: Rebuilt Pharasmin temple run by the mute High Priest Travos Clim.
  - The Racked Rock: Remnant stone jaw of a defeated rack-worm attracting sleepwalkers and Rovagug cultists.
  - Gulgmoth: Damaged 30ft ancient war golem sitting dormant in a ruined square.
  - Haunted & Blighted Sites: Barterfell (Squalid trade slum), Grizzly Fair (Haunted carnival), Stinger Scar (Insect-infested park), Morant (Void-tainted morotic community), Rope Cliffs (Smuggler cliff-rigs), Haunted District Courthouse (Ghost of Jaran Mord), Rat-Taker's Palace.
- Key NPCs: Watch-Lord Ulun II (Acting Nomarch); High Priest Travos Clim; Turgoff (Nailfist leader); Corrupted Druid Kaia.
- Local Drama: Ulun II leading tactical urban clearing operations block-by-block against Urgathoan cultists and ghoul packs; Rovagug cultists conducting sacrifices inside the Racked Rock fissure.
