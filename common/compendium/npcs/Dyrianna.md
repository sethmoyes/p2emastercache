Lady Dyrianna, [[Calistria]]'s high hetaera in [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] and mistress of the Courtesans' Guild, runs the [[locations/absalom#2. The Petal District (Elite Nobility)|Silken Court]] in the Petal District. The Mythkeeper transcript gives her house as Avenstar. Canon lists her among [[Darchana Madinani|Darchana]]'s enemies, and the two names are easy to blur.
