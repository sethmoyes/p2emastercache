Boss of the [[Mud Lickers|Mud Licker]] mitflits on [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog|Floor 1]], enthroned in [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A10. Mud Licker Throne Room · Severe 1|A10]]. He dreams of giant-fly cavalry to conquer [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] and speaks Common. His pet is a giant solifugid, Bite-Bite. He will trade a map and his help if the heroes kill [[Graulgust]]'s [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]] below.
