Scion Lord Goodman Hugen, First Harbormaster of [[locations/absalom|Absalom]], High Councilor, head of [[House Candren]] and owner of the Sea King Shipyards. Publicly loyal to the Primarch; secretly anti-noble and Andoran-sympathizing. His escort monopoly feeds the Primarch's treasury, and [[Darchana Madinani|Darchana]]'s teleportation circles undercut it. In canon his ship "mysteriously sinks" in 4719.
