Occultist king of the [[Ghost Queen morlocks]], enthroned in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B12. Graulgust's Throne Room · Severe 2|B12]]. He is building [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] a temple to win her favor and never speaks her name: say "Belcorra" near him and he shrieks, then attacks. His pet grothlut ([[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B13. Grothlut Den · Low 2|B13]]) adores him. [[Augrael]] on [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker|Floor 3]] is his rival.

- **Book:** B12, B13, [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B22. Workshop · Severe 2|B22]].
