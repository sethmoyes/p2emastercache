A female river drake who has lived in the flooded cavern [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B19. Flooded Cavern · Low 2|B19]] for nearly two years, hunting only in the [[Gauntlight#The Fogfen|Fogfen]] so [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] never learns she exists. She cut off the [[crooks_nook|Crook's Nook]] thieves' escape. Her kill, a giant frog, lies in [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A5. Maggot Stable · Low 1|A5]]; at night she may hunt near [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A17. Ruined Pier · side quest|A17]].

- **Book:** A5, A17, B19.
