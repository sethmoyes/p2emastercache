Human wizard, Archdean of the [[Arcanamirium]] and Second Spell Lord of [[locations/absalom|Absalom]], holding a lesser Grand Council seat and running for Primarch. Her teleportation circles link other cities straight to the Arcanamirium, skipping the harbor's pilots and tariffs, which made Harbormaster [[Goodman Hugen]] her enemy. Canon per [PathfinderWiki](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Darchana); earlier notes garbled her as "Dariana of House Madani," and [[Morlibint]]'s letter named her "Quandary."

- **Don't confuse with:** [[Dyrianna]], [[Calistria]]'s high hetaera (also [[locations/absalom#2. The Petal District (Elite Nobility)|Petal District]], also her enemy).
