Red-bearded dwarf, former owner of the [[the_rowdy_rockfish|Rowdy Rockfish]], who left [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] with a hooded stranger carrying a lantern and vanished. He is chained in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D9. Lasda's Lament · Moderate 4|D9]] on [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead|Floor 4]], the living battery for the [[Gauntlight]]'s power ribbon. Rescuing him makes the ribbon wink out on every floor ([[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B35. Gauntlight Basement · hazard|B35]], [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C15. Gauntlight Reading Room · Moderate 3|C15]]).

- **Book:** Floor 4 D9; the ribbon on [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon|Floor 2]] B35 and [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker|Floor 3]] C15; Area 17 (The Rowdy Rockfish).
