Misanthropic owner of [[blades_for_glades|Blades for Glades]] and the last descendant of the [[Roseguard]] fighter [[Vol Rajani]]. He feels the town owes him better than his surly manner earns, keeps losing mayoral elections to [[Oseph Menhemes]], and once ran with the [[Osprey Club]] as its best knife-thrower, the "Ace of Blades." On [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]] he steals [[Roseguard Relics#Rajani's Cooperative Blade|Rajani's Cooperative Blade]]. His soul is what [[Urevian]] wants on [[floor-07-soul-keepers|Floor 7]], because of an old clause in [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s contract.

- **Book:** Area 6 (Blades for Glades); Floor 5 "Carman's Background," "Smuggler's Refuge," "Carman's Fate"; Floor 7 "Urevian's Offer."
- **Shop stock:** Blades for Glades.
