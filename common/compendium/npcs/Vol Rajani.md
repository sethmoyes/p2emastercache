The [[Roseguard]]'s fighter, a no-nonsense swordswoman who claimed to be exiled royalty from Nidal. Her icon is her longsword, [[Roseguard Relics#Rajani's Cooperative Blade|Rajani's Cooperative Blade]]. [[Urevian]]'s contract with [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] was for her soul, a debt that now falls on her last descendant, [[Carman Rajani]].
