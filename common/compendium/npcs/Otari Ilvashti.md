The [[Roseguard]]'s rogue, a charming optimist who kept the others hopeful. [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s last-ditch explosion buried him in the [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A8. Sinkhole · Moderate 1|A8]] collapse. He sheltered in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B28. Haunted Washroom (Otari's Spirit Echoes) · no encounter|B28]] and [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C3. Haunted Reading Room · no encounter|C3]] in his last days, where his ghost-echoes linger, and his ghost waits with his bones in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D18. Otari's Room · Severe 4|D18]]. He tells the heroes that only the icons of all four Roseguard can pierce the seal to [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]]. The town of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] is named for him.

- **Book:** A8; B28; C3; [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D16. Haunted Stairs · no encounter|D16]], D18 ("What Otari Knows").
- **Spelling:** the Player's Guide has "Ilvashti"; the floor docs follow the captions' "Ilvashi". All the same ghost.
