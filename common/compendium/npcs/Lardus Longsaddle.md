Foul-mouthed, short-tempered captain of the Otari Guard, an elderly but hale veteran of the [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] city guard who retired to [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] a decade ago. Famous for curse-laden tirades and short jail sentences, but heroes who catch criminals earn a rare spot on his good side. He turns a blind eye to [[Yinyasmera]]'s smuggling.
