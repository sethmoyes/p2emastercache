[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s vain drow apprentice, who loved her. He kept a home on the islet ([[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A18. Apprentice Island · no encounter|A18]]) and a workshop on [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon|Floor 2]] ([[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B2. Volluk's Workshop · no encounter|B2]]–[[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B5. Volluk's Machine · Moderate 2|B5]]), and became a worm that walks, a leech-mass, now lairing in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D8. Lounge · Severe 4|D8]] on [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead|Floor 4]]. His soulbound doll is [[Mr. Beak]], holding the soul of his goblin servant [[Borbo]]. The only surviving image of him is [[Chandriu Invisar]]'s portrait in [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C36. Scriptorium · Severe 3|C36]], which can be used against him in D8. He also made the stand-in lens that lets the [[Gauntlight]] run at reduced power ([[floor-09-on-the-hunt#I58. Vault of Secrets · no encounter|I58]]).
