Shrewd, lanky owner of [[crooks_nook|Crook's Nook]] and secret head of the [[Osprey Club]], [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s thieves' guild. Loyal to her town; she rarely lets her thieves target locals, and [[Lardus Longsaddle|Captain Longsaddle]] turns a blind eye. In the book she offers 50 gp for four missing employees who went into the [[Gauntlight]] (one dies in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B14. Shrine of the Ghost Queen · no encounter|B14]]; the [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]] hold three in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B24. Prison · no encounter|B24]]).

- **Book:** Area 11 (Crook's Nook); the missing-thieves side quest starts at [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A17. Ruined Pier · side quest|A17]].
- **Shop stock:** Crook's Nook.
