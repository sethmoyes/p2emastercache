Easygoing, multi-term mayor of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]], owner of the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 1. The Giant's Wheel (Otari Mill) · Lumber mill · level 5|Giant's Wheel]] and one of the town's three lumber companies, and a descendant of the [[Roseguard]] cleric [[Aesephna Menhemes]]. A devoted family man; his eldest daughter is [[Dorianna Menhemes]]. He hires the heroes after Deadtide for Otari (50 gp each) and keeps [[Roseguard Relics#Aesephna's Hunter's Brooch|Aesephna's Hunter's Brooch]] in a locked shadow box at [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 3. Menhemes Manor · Manor · level 4|Menhemes Manor]].

- **Book:** Area 3 (Menhemes Manor) and its Roseguard museum; [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon|Floor 2]] "Dying Light"; [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]] "Retrieving the Icons" and Dorianna's subplot.
