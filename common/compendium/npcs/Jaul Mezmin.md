Grizzled, feral werewolf wanted in [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] for murders thirty years ago, the last of them Ayla Lathenar, wife of [[Keeleno Lathenar]]. He hid among the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 19. Stone Ring Pond · Temple · level 4|Stone Ring Pond]] druids until found out, leapt from the Otari cliffs into the sea, and washed up in the Vaults. He lairs in [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s meditation pavilion, [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D14. Pavilion · Severe 4|D14]].

- **Book:** D14, [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D15. Twisting Tunnel · no encounter|D15]]; Area 12 ([[otari_market|Otari Market]]) and Area 19 (Stone Ring Pond).
