[[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s eccentric oddities merchant and the heroes' mutual friend: pupilless eyes, small ram horns curled around elven ears, fingertips that sparkle with motes of light. A scholar of the stars who follows the Cosmic Caravan, and paralyzingly claustrophobic, which is why her shop is an open dome. She is the first to notice the faint blue glow atop the [[Gauntlight]], and the book uses her to brief the heroes and identify [[Nhimbaloth]].

- **Book:** Area 4 of the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari|Otari directory]] ([[wrins_wonders|Wrin's Wonders]]); briefs the party at the start of [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog|Floor 1]]; occultism expert on [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]]; opposes trading [[Carman Rajani|Carman]]'s soul on [[floor-07-soul-keepers|Floor 7]].
- **Shop stock:** Wrin's Wonders.
