Red-haired, perpetually scowling wizard who owns [[odd_stories|Odd Stories]] with his husband, and warms quickly to fellow scholars. He buys rare books the heroes bring back (5 gp per Bulk from [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s library on [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker|Floor 3]]) and, in the book, holds [[Zarmavdian]]'s [[Roseguard Relics#Thresholds of Truth|Thresholds of Truth]] in his own collection.

- **Book:** Area 7 (Odd Stories); Floor 3 [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C11. Main Library · Moderate 3|C11]] book side quest; [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]] "Retrieving the Icons."
- **Shop stock:** Odd Stories.
