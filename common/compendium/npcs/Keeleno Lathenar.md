Dour, humorless owner of [[otari_market|Otari Market]]. Thirty years ago the werewolf druid [[Jaul Mezmin]] murdered his wife, Ayla, and Keeleno is sure the killer still lives. He pays well for wolf pelts, distrusts the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 19. Stone Ring Pond · Temple · level 4|Stone Ring Pond]] druids, and closes the market every Gozran 5th in Ayla's memory.
