Exiled Chelish noblewoman and sorceress who built [[Gauntlight|Gauntlight Keep]] and the Abomination Vaults to raise an army of aberrations and undead against [[locations/absalom|Absalom]], the city that exiled her family. A worshipper of [[Nhimbaloth]]. The [[Roseguard]] killed her, and she returned as a ghost on the 500th anniversary of her family's exile (4719 AR). She cannot leave the bottom three floors, so she works through will-o'-wisps and agents. The [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]] worship her as the Ghost Queen. She is the campaign's final boss on [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Floor 10]], destroyed by Nhimbaloth's gaze.
