Matronly, stoic dwarf who runs the [[the_rowdy_rockfish|Rowdy Rockfish]]. She took over the bar when the previous owner, [[Lasda Venkervale]], vanished: in the book he is her **son**. She is certain he still lives, and if the heroes bring him home from [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D9. Lasda's Lament · Moderate 4|D9]] she gives them a family heirloom, a minor sturdy shield bearing an angry rockfish.

- **Book:** Area 17 (The Rowdy Rockfish); [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead|Floor 4]] D9.
- ⚠ **Divergence:** both tables have played the Rockfish's keeper as Lasda's **wife**, not his mother. Table history stands; this is the book's version.
