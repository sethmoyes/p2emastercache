Chatty halfling high priestess of [[Sarenrae]] who runs the [[dawnflower_library|Dawnflower Library]], [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s largest temple, and has an uncanny knack for recommending exactly the right book. Open-minded about almost everything except thieves: her own sister stole valuable books from the temple and fled. [[Roseguard Relics#Rajani's Cooperative Blade|Rajani's Cooperative Blade]] is stolen from her display on [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]]'s Otari chapter.

- **Book:** Area 18 (Dawnflower Library); Floor 5 "Investigating the Fire".
- **Shop stock:** Dawnflower Library.
