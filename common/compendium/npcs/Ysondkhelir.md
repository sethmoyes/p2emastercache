A denizen of Leng in [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G18. War Room · Moderate 7|G18]] on [[floor-07-soul-keepers|Floor 7]], once [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s strategist, who torments dreamers. Before [[Dorianna Menhemes]] he preyed on the skulk [[Jedzeli]] ([[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F12. Kitchen · no encounter|F12]]). To Dorianna he is "the Motley Man," the man in colored rags who takes her hand. He never comes near her in person; his schemes with her are close to complete.
