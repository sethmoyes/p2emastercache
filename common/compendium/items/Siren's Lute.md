A lute that plays a faint, sweet melody on its own and never stops, so whoever carries it can't sneak. Not from the book; drawn from the GM's cursed-item card deck. GM ruling: it gives +1 to Performance to a bard who keeps it.

- **Book:** none. Found on [[Graulgust]]'s body at [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B14. Shrine of the Ghost Queen · no encounter|B14]]; thrown down the [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B16. Pantry · no encounter|B16]] stairs to [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C1. Visitors' Reading Room · Trivial 3|C1]] and returned by [[Augrael]].
