The icons of the four [[Roseguard]]. Together they pierce the spectral seal between Floors 4 and 5 (the [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D13. Upper Temple of Nhimbaloth · Severe 4|D13]] altar), as [[Otari Ilvashti]]'s ghost explains in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D18. Otari's Room · Severe 4|D18]]. All were found in [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] in the book ([[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]] "Retrieving the Icons").

## Otari's Thieves' Tools
[[Otari Ilvashti]]'s icon, with his bones in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D18. Otari's Room · Severe 4|D18]].

## Aesephna's Hunter's Brooch
[[Aesephna Menhemes]]'s icon, bearing [[Erastil]]'s symbol, in a locked shadow box in the music room of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 3. Menhemes Manor · Manor · level 4|Menhemes Manor]]. [[Oseph Menhemes|Oseph]] lends it if told the truth.

## Rajani's Cooperative Blade
[[Vol Rajani]]'s longsword, displayed at the [[dawnflower_library|Dawnflower Library]] until [[Carman Rajani]] steals it.

## Thresholds of Truth
[[Zarmavdian]]'s spellbook. In the book it sits in [[Morlibint]]'s collection at [[odd_stories|Odd Stories]].
