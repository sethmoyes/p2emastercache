Morlocks who climbed up from the depths to "prepare the way" for [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]], whom they worship as the Ghost Queen. They fought the [[Cult of the Canker]] on the way, drove the [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] to the surface, and hold [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon|Floor 2]] under [[Graulgust|King Graulgust]].
