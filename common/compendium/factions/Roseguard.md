The four-person adventuring party who made their name in [[locations/absalom|Absalom]], killed [[Belcorra Haruvex]] in 4244 AR, and founded the logging town of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]. Otari honors them every Founder's Day, 3 Desnus, and the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 3. Menhemes Manor · Manor · level 4|Menhemes Manor]] holds a public Roseguard museum.

- **Members:** [[Aesephna Menhemes]] (cleric of [[Erastil]]) · [[Otari Ilvashti]] (rogue) · [[Vol Rajani]] (fighter) · [[Zarmavdian]] (wizard).
- **Their icons** break the seal between Floors 4 and 5: see [[Roseguard Relics]].
