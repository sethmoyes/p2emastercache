A pale, indestructible lighthouse rising from the ruins of Gauntlight Keep in the middle of the Fogfen, half an hour's walk north of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]. Beneath it lie the Abomination Vaults, [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s ten-level megadungeon. The lighthouse shaft runs the full height of the dungeon, and a ribbon of necrotic light inside it powers the beam. Belcorra meant to fire it at [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].

## The Fogfen
The sulfurous swamp around the keep: frogs, mosquitoes and ever-present mist. Few in [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] go there, and the ruins are thought picked clean. See [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog|Floor 1]] "Getting Started."
