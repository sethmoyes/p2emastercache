[[locations/absalom|Absalom]]'s great school of practical magic, in the [[locations/absalom#1. The Wise Quarter (Academic & Arcane Hub)|Wise Quarter]]. Nex built it as an apology for an earlier Nexian siege of the city. Its ranks run apprentice, journeyman, maven; the Learned Guard keeps the district's peace. [[Darchana Madinani|Lady Darchana]] is its Dean, and her teleportation circles link it directly to other cities.
