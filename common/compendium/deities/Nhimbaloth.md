The Empty Death, an Outer God of ghosts and lost souls, seen only as mist with seven-fingered tentacles. [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] worshipped her, and the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]] on Floor 10 is an ancient serpentfolk shrine to her. In the finale her gaze destroys Belcorra.

- **Book:** [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A15. Nhimbaloth Shrine · Severe 1|A15]] (Nhimbaloth Shrine); Floor 10 "Nhimbaloth's Entrance," [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare#J20. The Empty Vault · Extreme 10|J20]].
