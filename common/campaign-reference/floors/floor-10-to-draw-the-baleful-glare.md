## Chapter Tools

### Chapter 10 Synopsis
▶ [1:45](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=105)

The heroes have **their final confrontation with [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].** On the way to **the Empty Vault, where she can be found**, they pass **challenges that aspirants of [[Nhimbaloth]] faced when this was an active temple to the Empty Death.**

- ***Ironically, this lowest level is one of the safest*** — **its denizens generally stay put**, and **the heroes can rest as long as they need in its empty chambers.** **They'll need to be in top form to face Belcorra.** **If they haven't collected all three [[fulcrum lenses]] by now, they may have to retreat, recover them, and face her again.**
- **Few inhabitants know the temple's true story** — **notably the [[Lady's Whisper]], and maybe a dread wisp** — **and none will tell it.** *Was it a cult hub cast deep into the earth by an ancient enemy, or the headquarters of a Darklands cabal?* **Whatever its origin, the initiation trials are the last obstacles before Belcorra.**
- **The three trials:**
  1. **Challenge of Souls** — **unworthy initiates were poisoned, tormented and had their souls extracted.**
  2. **Challenge of Despair** — **initiates face their greatest fear.**
  3. **Challenge of the Swamp** — **initiates show mastery over swamp dangers.**
- The heroes **face them in that order only if guided by the Lady's Whisper**; **otherwise they may tackle them in any order.** **Beyond lies the Empty Vault — Belcorra's domain and the source of the [[Gauntlight]]'s power.**

**Black stone** · **carvings of bones or mist** · **clammy air** · **eerie lights** · **fog** · **swamp plants.**

▶ [3:47](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=227)

## The Trials

### J1. First Challenge · no encounter — *Challenge of Souls begins*
▶ [4:25](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=265)

- **Sensory:** **a basin of reeds and moss in an alcove between doors** ⚠F9. **Two metal tubes jut into the room from the east, each sealed by an iris-like mechanism a foot inside — no visible way to open them.** **Black stone doors lead south; a single gray stone door carved with swirling mists leads west.** **None has a handle or latch.**
- **The tubes:** **slant downward and drain into the swamp in J15** — **just wide enough for a Medium creature to slide down.**
- **With the [[Lady's Whisper]]:** the skeleton **silently points at the gray door.** **That door opens with a push, or swings aside if someone stands before it for a round.**
- **Sealed:** **the south doors (hardness 15, 60 HP) and the tubes' iris openings (hardness 18, 72 HP)** **stay shut unless the Lady's Whisper wills otherwise.**
- **Dread wisps:** if any follow the heroes, **one hovers above the basin, silently reporting everything to [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].**
- **Passing the Challenge of Souls:** when the heroes return **having fed or defeated the soul feeders in J8**, **the Lady's Whisper opens the south doors and leaves them open.** **DC 27 Perception (Sense Motive)** reveals **it's offended that they passed but is suppressing the irritation.**

#### The Proctor
▶ [6:28](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=388)

The heroes may **go along with the Lady's Whisper at first**, wondering what the enigmatic undead wants — **but after one or two tests they may decide they *don't* want to be judged worthy of [[Nhimbaloth]]'s attention.** **That's especially true once they realize each passed test *increases its displeasure* and that it plans to kill them.** **It answers any pre-emptive attack as though expecting it:** **it levitates out of melee reach and casts spells to eliminate the heroes; the pair of dread wisps that may have followed from [[floor-09-on-the-hunt#I64. Initiates' Chamber · Severe 9|I64]] join in.**

### J2. Poisoning Room · Moderate 10
▶ [7:20](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=440)

- **Sensory:** **a long room; at the east end stands a giant statue of a cracked, chipped skeleton on a burial shroud, its skull clenching a bundle of swamp weeds in its teeth.** **Narrow slits and stone doors are built into the walls** (shooting galleries).
- **The statue:** **DC 25 Religion** recognizes **a rare physical depiction of [[Nhimbaloth]].**
- **History:** initiates **were made to kneel before it while hidden cultists shot poison blowgun darts at them.** **Those who didn't succumb were deemed worthy and led south; those who did were taken north to have their souls harvested.**
- **Creatures — six cultist specters:** they **manifest in the shooting galleries with ghostly blowguns** (in addition to their vile-touch strikes), **firing ethereal darts of their own substance.** **The slits give anyone in a gallery greater cover — +4 circumstance bonus to AC, Reflex saves vs. area effects, and Stealth.** **If the heroes enter a gallery, the specters pass through the walls to keep their distance.** **They fight until destroyed.**

### J3. Cleansing Chamber · no encounter
▶ [8:57](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=537)

- **Two grime-crusted copper tubs, dry, stand against the south wall.** Once **initiates who survived the poison were stripped, bathed in swamp water, and escorted to the Worthies' Cave.**
- **Secret door:** **DC 25 Perception** — **a false stone on the east wall must be rotated to pivot open one of the tubs and the wall beside it.**

### J4. Worthies' Cave · Low 10 (trap)
▶ [9:34](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=574)

- **Sensory:** **a natural chamber whose ceiling has seven finger-wide holes.** Before **the Challenge of Despair, surviving initiates were subjected to *mind-numbing gas* to make it harder.**
- **Secret doors:** **both open the same way — rotate a section of the smooth flat wall to pivot the door open.** **They sit at the ends of two short halls, so they're easy to find** (**searching either hall reveals the door and the method**).
- **Hazard — Befuddling Gas Trap (level 11, complex mechanical):** **spews mind-numbing gas from the seven holes.** **While it's active the doors *won't* open — they must be battered down (hardness 18, 60 HP).**

### J5. Level 10 Portal Chamber · Moderate 10
▶ [10:44](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=644)

- **Door:** **locked; keys long lost.** **DC 25 Athletics to force, or four successes at DC 25 Thievery.**
- **Sensory:** **swirling silver-filled runes on the rounded walls; sheets of reflective metal on the floor and ceiling.** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **added this room to connect her teleportation network.** **Reactivate it with *awaken portal* (as at [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B17. Level Two Portal Chamber (Teleport Circle Summary) · no encounter|B17]]).**
- **Creature — a powerful stone golem** built into the wall between the runes: **as the lowest portal chamber, Belcorra made sure it was well guarded.** **It steps out and attacks anyone not accompanying Belcorra.**
- **XP: 30** for reactivating the circle.

### J6. Crematorium · Moderate 10
▶ [12:02](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=722)

- **Sensory:** **two pools of bubbling magma side by side in a large chamber.**
- **Hazard:** **extremely hot — 2d6 fire damage every 10 minutes** (the resident creatures are immune ⚠F5). **Immersion in the 25-ft-deep magma pools: 20d6 fire damage per round.**
- **Creatures:** **bound fire elementals** have **kept the room hot for centuries.** **They climb out of the pools to attack intruders — aggressively — and fight until destroyed.**

### J7. Soul Extractors · Moderate 10
▶ [12:45](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=765)

- **Sensory:** **a strange, irregular hall with several wide alcoves, each holding a standing rack of crystals and levers "like bizarre portrait devices."**
- **History:** **failed initiates were strapped to the racks; the occult machines extracted their souls into crystal globes, and special glass golems sliced their bodies apart.** **The golems then carried the soul-filled globes — too delicate for their blade hands — to the dread wisps in J8.** **Only three machines still work, each holding a globe with the soul of an initiate killed centuries ago.**
- **Knowledge:** **DC 30 Occultism, Religion or relevant Lore (e.g. Spirit Lore)** on a working machine reveals **what the globes are and how they were made.**
- **Creatures — four glass golems:** they **try to slice intruders to ribbons.** **Their greenish glass bodies glow unsettlingly — giving them *grim glittering* instead of *dazzling brilliance*.**
- **Treasure:**
  - **Three crystal globes, ~1 ft across — 200 gp each to unsavory soul merchants such as night hags.** **If smashed the fragments are worthless, but the trapped soul travels to the Boneyard and its ultimate reward.** *(Smashing them in J8 is how the heroes satisfy the soul feeders.)*
  - **Machine parts:** **several hours and DC 30 Crafting** strips a working machine of **rare metals and delicate gears worth 80 gp — 160 gp on a critical success; on a critical failure the parts are ruined.**

### J8. Devouring Chamber · Severe 10
▶ [14:58](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=898)

- **Sensory:** **a large chamber with a spiny, dust-covered floor; a large stone basin of swamp plants on the far west edge.** **Thick dust covers shattered crystal shards — evidence of hundreds of initiates' extracted souls.** **The crystals make the floor difficult terrain and impose a −5 circumstance penalty to Stealth** ⚠F6. **Harmless to anyone wearing footwear — barefoot creatures take 5 piercing damage at the end of each Stride.**
- **Creatures — three dread wisps ("soul feeders")**, **extensions of the Empty Vault's evil energies (J20), funneling the souls released here into that chamber.** **They can't return to the Empty Vault — or even leave this room — without more souls.**
  - **What they want:** they **demand the heroes fetch the crystal globes from J7 and smash them here.** **They don't admit that this frees them to return to the Empty Vault — but they truthfully say it will make them vanish from here.** **One soul feeder winks out per sphere smashed** — **and the heroes will face them again in the Empty Vault.**
  - **If the heroes refuse:** they **attack to kill and use the heroes' souls as fuel.** **They gang up on one hero at a time.** **Each hero killed here immediately returns one soul feeder to the Empty Vault.**
  - **Stolen Soul:** **a hero who dies here has part of their soul stolen** — **they can be raised normally but gain *doomed* and can't reduce it below 1 for a year.**
- **XP: 30** for **dealing with the soul feeders and passing the Challenge of Souls.**

### J9. Second Challenge · no encounter — *Challenge of Despair begins*
▶ [17:27](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1047)

- **Like J1, but** **the west gray door is carved with frightened faces** and **there are three metal tubes**; **the basin of reeds and swamp water is to the south.** **The south doors and tube irises work as in J1.** **The west door is unlocked and leads to the second test.**
- **With the [[Lady's Whisper]]:** it **silently points to the west door**; **the second dread wisp shadowing it hovers over the basin and reports to [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].** **Once the heroes are through, it opens the doors to J14, waits there, and closes the doors behind itself.**

### J10. Path of Pleasant Memories · hazard
▶ [18:35](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1115)

- **Sensory:** **the walls of this long, narrow hall flash stark white, then swirl with colorful images.** **A door at each end.** **Cult magic powers the illusions here and along the Path of Despair (J11–J12); each viewer sees different images — "a hodgepodge of memories" drawn from their own mind.** *(GM: draw on the party's victories, friendships and funny moments from your campaign.)*
- **Effect:** **a hero approaching the west door makes a DC 27 Will save.** **Failure: entranced by the images for 24 hours — −10 ft to all Speeds in J10–J12 and can't avert their gaze in those areas.**
- **Secret doors (cultist shortcuts):** **north — DC 25 Perception; south — DC 30 Perception.** **Each opens by manipulating a slightly darker section of wall.** **The south door is one-way: the heroes can use it to reach J12 but can't come back.**

### J11. Path of Failure · hazard (level 12)
▶ [20:15](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1215)

- **Sensory:** **the path widens and turns south, opposite a single narrow door.** **Walls swirl with personalized memories of *failure*** — **mentors scolding, authorities punishing, lost friends crying.** *(GM: pull in the party's real failures — fleeing a significant foe, triggering a damaging trap.)*
- **Hazard — Images of Failure (level 12, magical trap):** **the images worm into the victim's mind.** **The heroes can avert their gaze and gain that action's bonus to the hazard's saves — these illusions are more than visual.**

### J12. Path of Powerlessness · hazard (level 12)
▶ [21:21](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1281)

- **Sensory:** **walls, floor and ceiling swirl with overlapping, shifting images that *predict failure in the future*** — **viewers see themselves growing infirm, succumbing to disease, or falling to [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].**
- **Exit:** **the north secret door doesn't open from this side.** **The east secret door is the only way out — those who couldn't find it eventually died of torment.** **Beyond 20 ft it's invisible amid the images; within 20 ft, DC 27 Perception finds it.** **A hero who *searches the hall* — impossible while the trap is active (encounter mode, not exploration) — finds it automatically.**
- **Hazard — Images of Powerlessness (level 12, magical trap):** **like J11 but its area fills the entire long hall;** **the heroes can avert their gaze here too.**

### J13. Chamber of Reflection · no encounter
▶ [23:00](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1380)

- **A bare room with plain white walls — a psychic refuge where initiates who passed the Challenge of Despair could recover before the next test.**
- **XP: 30** for **reaching this room after passing the Challenge of Despair.**

### J14. Third Challenge · no encounter — *Challenge of the Swamp begins*
▶ [23:28](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1408)

- **Sensory:** **double doors of black stone to the north; an enormous slab of rough-hewn rock blocking the passage south; two metal tubes jut in from the east wall, both irises open.** **The slab is one of two forming an enormous door** (**a gap of a few inches between them**); **only the [[Lady's Whisper]] can open them — anyone else must smash them (hardness 20, 80 HP each).**
- **The Lady's Whisper meets the heroes here** (after J13). **DC 22 Perception (Sense Motive)** reveals it **is angry at the heroes' progress** ⚠F7; **on a critical success they realize it plans to *betray* them if they keep passing [[Nhimbaloth]]'s tests.**
- **It points to the open pipes.** **When the heroes exit through the pipes it closes the irises behind them, then raises the stone slabs just enough to enter J18, where it waits again.**

### J15. Deadly Swamp · Moderate 10
▶ [24:57](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1497)

- **Arrival:** **the pipes end in a drop — 25 ft from J1, 20 ft from J9, 15 ft from J14** — **into reeking swamp muck: no fall damage, but it draws the room's inhabitants.**
- **Sensory:** **a massive cavern of thick, moist, stinking swamp; seven rusted metal boxes barely visible in dense reeds.** **At the south end, a rounded metal dam raised nearly to the ceiling so only a trickle dribbles over it; ancient gears either side appear to lower it, rust streaking them.** **At the lower north end the swamp drains into a murky flooded room.** *(The environment echoes the poisonous terrain of [[Nhimbaloth]]'s dead world, Void Bracken* ⚠F4*.)*
- **The challenge:** **find how to open the dam and reach J17.** **Difficult terrain** — **shallow water over sucking, treacherous ground.**
- **Toxic air (poison):** **each creature starting its turn in the swamp makes a DC 27 Fortitude save or becomes sickened 2 (sickened 4 on a critical failure) and can't reduce sickened while in the swamp;** **then it's immune for 10 minutes, pass or fail.**
- **The puzzle:** **three rusted pipes run just beneath the water and weeds, connecting to the seven boxes and forming the dam's control system.** **DC 25 Crafting, Engineering Lore or Thievery (Recall Knowledge)** reveals **their importance; on a critical success the heroes also learn the controls must connect to J16 in the north.**
  - **Each box has one valve, turned east–west = *off*.** **Turning it north–south (along the pipes) = *on*.** **All seven must be *on* before J16's wheels will turn.**
  - *(Optional: **each valve you turn also flips every other valve along its pipe and in its row** — a proper puzzle for players who like them.)*
- **Creatures — three chuuls** **imbued with Void Bracken's vile energy** — **fight until destroyed** — they attack before the heroes can do much.

### J16. Perilous Controls · Severe 10
▶ [28:28](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1708)

- **Sensory:** **swamp water floods most of the cave; three small islands each bear a metal box with a rusty wheel.** **It looks as shallow as J15's swamp — it's actually 25 ft deep, so the heroes will likely swim.** **The air isn't toxic here — no breathing saves.**
- **The wheels:** **all three, turned fully clockwise (3 Interact actions each), with all J15 valves *on*, drop the dam:** **it grinds down, releasing a cascade of fetid swamp water that raises the water level here and in J15 by several inches.**
- **Creatures:** **a pair of "dread hues"** ⚠F3 **lurk underwater, ambush intruders, and fight to the death.**

### J17. Infested Reeds · Low 10
▶ [29:40](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1780)

- **Sensory:** **usually a foot deep in swamp water draining from the Empty Vault when J15's dam is closed.** **The heroes most likely enter from J15 after lowering the dam — completing the Challenge of the Swamp.** **A circular pool at the center of a square chamber**, **fed by a channel in the west wall between two sets of black stone double doors; the water flows north through a metal dam.** **A thick knot of writhing reeds, like a grotesque tree, rises from the pool.** **The pool and channels are only 6 in deep — which is why the room floods so easily when the dam is closed.**
- **Creature:** **the writhing is an "isalo" infestation** ⚠F1 — **a grotesque aberration that moves out of the reeds to attack.** **Malevolent but unaffiliated with [[Nhimbaloth]]; it remembers little about how its larvae came here ages ago.**
  - **Below 150 HP it casts *control water* to raise the room's water 10 ft**, hoping to gain an edge.
  - **When destroyed, its larvae scatter into the water and slip into the swamp below — in time to grow a new host body.**

### J18. Maw of the Goddess · Low 10
▶ [31:24](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1884)

- **Sensory:** **a massive chamber with a vaulted ceiling 80 ft high** ⚠F8, **carved with intricate patterns of bones, swamp weeds and misty swirls.** **To the south, a huge dais of crooked, cracked stone teeth** bearing **a massive skull with empty eye sockets and a menacing visage.** **A channel of filthy water crosses the room, emerging at the base of the east and west walls; double doors bracket each outlet.**
- **Secret door:** **behind the giant skull** — **partly outlined in moisture, easier to find: DC 20 Perception.**
- **Creature — the [[Lady's Whisper]]** (**if not yet defeated**): **it waits on the dais.** **It points nowhere — it walks slowly toward the heroes and whispers:**

> ***"I know that you are pretenders. Defilers. You should not be here. [[Nhimbaloth]] thinks you serve some purpose to her plans — but I disagree."***

  **It attacks to stop them, using *unwilling teleportation* to shunt heroes back to J14 (they must come around through the swamp again).** **Fights until destroyed.**

### J19. Passage to the Depths · Severe 10
▶ [33:12](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=1992)

- **Sensory:** **a smooth natural cavern winds through darkness to a larger chamber with a wide pit at the far side.** **The pit is hundreds of feet deep and leads to deeper Darklands — "beyond the scope of this adventure path."** **A corpse slumps at the pit's edge: a recently killed strange humanoid — two heads, a chitinous body.** **The creature in the pit hollowed it out and filled it with gravel, so it clatters when disturbed unless handled very carefully** (a gruesome alarm).
- **Creature:** **a crab-like Darklands predator** ⚠F2 **lurks in the pit** — **the passage hasn't been used in centuries, but isn't wholly abandoned.** **When it hears activity above (the gravel's clatter) it skitters up and arrives in 2 rounds.** **It retreats, abandoning its potential meal, below 50 HP.**
- **Treasure:** **the odd corpse wears a strange jeweled torque worth 500 gp.**

## The Finale

### J20. The Empty Vault · Extreme 10
▶ [34:38](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=2078)

- **Sensory:** **a strangely shaped chamber, humid and oppressively clammy.** **Four wings each hold a massive pool of bubbling swamp water with flickering motes of light and darkness moving beneath the surface.** **The center is partly blocked by a circle of towering black standing stones (menhirs) crackling with eerie energy that shoots up a shaft in the ceiling.** **Narrow channels — "as though dug into the stone by a seven-fingered hand" — run from each pool and three of the walls to a central moat; from there the water flows under the east wall between a pair of black stone double doors.** **An island in the moat holds a smooth altar topped with a fist-sized black lens — the Ebon [[Fulcrum Lenses|fulcrum lens]].**
- **What the room is:** **the Empty Vault has always been a *generation pit* for the wisps that serve [[Nhimbaloth]] — a funnel for swamp, despair and ghostly energies**, **mystically linked to the swamp basins throughout the lower Vaults.** **Any follower of Nhimbaloth can speak telepathically to wisps beside a basin from here.**
- **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s link:** **the Empty Vault drew her soul after the [[Roseguard]] killed her** — **her ghost reappeared here 500 years after the most traumatic event of her life: not her death, but her family's exile from [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].** **She can't leave this chamber for long, so she acts through wisp proxies and bound agents.** **She keeps the Ebon lens on the altar to restore its energies** — **it can regain "glimmers" of Nhimbaloth's essence, but slowly.**
- **The seven rivulets (hazard):** **the swamp-water rivulets are noxious and dangerous — they mark where Nhimbaloth once touched the world.** **Stepping over one is easy but "pulls unpleasantly at the life energy" of anyone crossing.**
  - **At the end of any turn in which a hero crosses a rivulet (striding, stepping, flying — any means): DC 22 Fortitude save or *doomed 1*.**
  - **The DC rises by 2 for each *additional unique* rivulet crossed that turn** (*e.g.* **three rivulets = DC 26**).
  - ***Doomed* is cumulative on later rounds.**
  - **Crossing the *pools* does *not* trigger it — so there are safer routes than walking a straight line.**
  - **The rivulets don't affect followers of Nhimbaloth — including Belcorra and the dread wisps.**
- **The menhirs:** **the black stones help support the [[Gauntlight]]'s weight and emit the twisting ribbon of energy that rises up the shaft to the ribbon areas [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B35. Gauntlight Basement · hazard|B35]], [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C15. Gauntlight Reading Room · Moderate 3|C15]], [[floor-08-decaying-gardens#H37. Upper Pit · no encounter|H37]] and [[floor-09-on-the-hunt#I28. Central Pit · no encounter|I28]]** ⚠F11. **Belcorra can manipulate the ribbon to power the Gauntlight.**
- **Creatures — Belcorra Haruvex** (the final boss; **extreme encounter**):
  - **If she hasn't yet rejuvenated, *the Ebon fulcrum lens automatically rejuvenates her when the heroes arrive*** ⚠F12. **She's so incensed that another group dares oppose her in her sanctum that she attacks with full fury.**
  - **Targeting:** she **focuses on anyone carrying fulcrum lenses — especially a hero holding the fulcrum lattice** — **she realizes they're a potent weapon against her.**
  - **The soul feeders:** **one is here at all times; if the heroes gave the souls to the soul feeders in J8, those wisps came here and fight at Belcorra's side — making the encounter much tougher.**
  - **Tactics:** **Belcorra and the soul feeders use the menhirs for cover and dart about, hoping to lure the heroes across the doom-inducing rivulets.**
- **How she's destroyed:** **when the heroes *infuse Belcorra with the splinters in the Crimson, Emerald and Ochre fulcrum lenses*, Nhimbaloth comes for her and she's forever destroyed.** *(Repo: melee spell attacks infuse the three splinters; the Ebon lens is hers.)*

### Nhimbaloth's Entrance
▶ [39:32](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=2372)

*Once the heroes infuse [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] with all three of [[Nhimbaloth]]'s splinters:*

1. **The earth shakes; a tremendous, hideous wail comes from somewhere beyond the chamber — beyond the Material Plane.** **All creatures in the Abomination Vaults make a DC 30 Will save or become *frightened 3*; a critical failure also means *paralyzed for 1d4 rounds*.**
2. **Next round Nhimbaloth's essence floods the room:** **a terrible blackish-gray mist seeps in, stinking of a fetid bog** ⚠F13, **leaving a layer of clawing, slimy water on everything it passes.** **It twists into a shape with seven-fingered tentacles appearing and vanishing in its folds.** **This misty incarnation can't be attacked or damaged.**
3. **It flows into Belcorra, who shrieks:**

> ***"No! I deserve your power, not your attention! My death is full of rage and strength — it is not empty!"***

4. **Despite her protests, Belcorra's form is pulled apart into the mist — and the mist vanishes.**
5. **The [[Gauntlight]] collapses.** **An immense grinding noise comes from high above:** **Belcorra's greatest work collapses one level at a time — each round the artifact drops one dungeon level along the lighthouse footprint** (**not through the rest of the Vaults**), **obliterating anything directly beneath it.** ***On the ninth round it collapses into this chamber, destroying everything here.*** **The heroes had better be gone by then.**

### Conclusion
▶ [41:22](https://youtu.be/eY2dBv4Jt90?t=2482)

**With [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] destroyed, [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] and [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] are safe from her spiteful schemes.** **The heroes get a warm welcome in Otari — everyone wants the tale over a friendly fire, good food and more than a few drinks.** **They'll be told, retold and embellished for generations.** **The [[Gauntlight]] is gone; only the pit in the [[Gauntlight#The Fogfen|Fogfen]] marks where it stood.** **Perhaps the dangers lurking below will one day regain their strength and menace the surface — but those are tales for other heroes.**
