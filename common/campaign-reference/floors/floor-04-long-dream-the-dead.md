▶ [0:42](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=42)

Level 4 was **designed as a retreat for [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] to relax and revere her uncaring deity ([[Nhimbaloth]]).** **No faction of significance holds it today**; instead **several powerful individuals each claim a separate portion.** **The most significant ally the heroes can gain is the ghost of [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari Ilvashi]] — the fallen hero who gave the town its name.**

## Rooms D1–D21

### D1. Belcorra's Dining Room · Moderate 4
▶ [2:53](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=173)

- **Sensory:** **a pair of brightly glowing chandeliers over a long banquet table with luxurious high-backed chairs.** **The table is still set for a grand meal — some place settings scattered and toppled, as are a few chairs.** **Four skeletons in tattered finery lie about, some clutching their own necks.**
- **History:** when the **[[Roseguard]] assaulted [[Gauntlight|Gauntlight Keep]]**, [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] was **dining here with representatives of a now-extinct [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] thieves' guild she was courting as allies against the city.** **When word came that the Roseguard was handily beating the keep's defenses she took no chances:** **she politely excused herself, told her guests to finish their meals, and magically ordered her chef to poison them all and murder the other servants — then take his own life.**
- **Stairs (north door):** up to **[[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C29. Abandoned Guard Post · no encounter|C29]].**
- **Creatures:** **the guests died in agony and later became havens for corpse lights seeking hosts.** **When a living creature *other than [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]]* enters, the corpse lights glow and the skeletons stagger up to attack.** **Each time a corpse light is forced into wisp form it flees to D2, D3 or D6 to claim another corpse and return to the fight.**
- **Treasure (guest remains):** **48 gp · a bracelet of dashing · a viper arrow.**

### D2. Kitchen · no encounter
▶ [4:40](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=280)

- **Sensory:** **a squat cast-iron stove with no chimney in the SW corner; a large oak table of cooking utensils on the north wall; a skeleton slumped at the western cupboards.** **The stove magically turns smoke into clean air — built into the room, far too heavy to be worth hauling out.** **The skeleton is [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s cook, who killed himself after carrying out her orders.**
- **Secret door (north):** **DC 19 Perception.** **Utensils fixed to the wall must be manipulated in the correct order.**

### D7. Disposal Room · Low 4
▶ [7:31](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=451)

- **Sensory:** **an open metal hatch in the floor against the east wall; storage bins north and south heaped with old bones, broken tools and lumps of stone.** **Some bones (easily identified) are human: a servant slipped against a bin and died two days before [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s last meal — the other servants simply heaved her body in.**
- **The chute:** **the hatch drops into a long chute to the disposal pond (D17).** **Anyone entering slides down — 3d6 bludgeoning, DC 20 basic Reflex on landing.** **To stop: DC 18 Reflex to grab an edge, or DC 18 Acrobatics/Athletics to brace.** **Either way DC 18 Athletics is needed to traverse the chute safely** (as heard "dc8").
- **Creature — a poltergeist** (the servant's spirit, **racked with indignation**): **invisible; doesn't attack until spotted or someone nears the chute lip.** **It hurls debris from the bins or uses *telekinetic maneuver* to shove foes into the hatch; fights until destroyed but can't leave the room.** **It can be put to rest by disposing of its remains respectfully (cremating them, saying a blessing).**

### D8. Lounge · Severe 4
▶ [9:18](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=558)

- **Sensory:** **grizzly paintings of spectral beings among ruined neighborhoods, overgrown swamps and foggy graveyards.** **Three overstuffed chairs with end tables (empty wine glasses); a cluttered writing desk to the south; a large iron tub to the west between screens of shadowy marshes.** **Once [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s relaxation room — now a den for her one-time apprentice, the drow [[Volluk Azrinae]].**
- **The desk papers:** **Volluk's disjointed notes — and, usefully, detailed blueprints of the top three levels including all secret doors.**
- **Secret door (east wall):** **DC 20 Perception finds a lifelike graveyard painting that swings outward.** **Every ~10 minutes a faint scream comes from the other side (it sounds like the picture itself); after the first scream the DC drops to 12.**
- **The key:** **DC 25 Perception finds an iron key in a secret slot in a desk leg — opens [[Lasda Venkervale|Lasda]]'s manacles in D9.**
- **Creature — Volluk Azrinae, a worm-that-walks:** **still alive "after a fashion" — he fed himself to a vat of richly prepared leeches and now lives as a hideous mass of thousands of slithering leeches, roughly drow-shaped.** **He's working with the [[Voidglutton]] in D20 to restore the [[Gauntlight]], frustrated that Belcorra's ghost won't speak to him directly — he must serve her through the Voidglutton's will.** **The proud occultist is reading at his desk when the heroes arrive; on noticing them he rises "a squirming awful mess" and demands to know who they are.** **He has little interest in conversation; if they let him talk he uses Intimidation to roll initiative.**
- **Treasure:** **scrolls of *comprehend languages*, *paralyze* and *worm's repast*** · **a lantern of empty light** (on the desk — **Volluk carries it on trips aboveground, which "unknowingly spawned the rumors of a lantern-carrying stranger"**).

### D9. Lasda's Lament · Moderate 4
▶ [12:16](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=736)

- **Sensory:** **a circular chamber with smooth gray walls.** **A flickering, nauseating ribbon of pale blue light turns at the center; where it touches the ceiling the stone bubbles "like infected flesh."** **Below it, a screaming, thrashing dwarf is bound on a metal table, his body covered in blisters.**
- **The ribbon:** **as in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B35. Gauntlight Basement · hazard|B35]], it's a manifestation of the [[Gauntlight]]'s growing power** — **a 5-ft-diameter burst at the room's center.** **A living creature ending its turn in it takes 5d6 negative damage (DC 18 basic Reflex); undead regain that much.**
- **Why the dwarf:** **to reactivate the Gauntlight for its test firing on [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s graveyard, [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]] and the [[Voidglutton]] needed a *living anchor* for the light.** **Everyone else who touches the light is harmed, but it heals its anchor as fast as it harms them — keeping him in perpetual agony.**
- **The victim — [[Lasda Venkervale]]** ⚠F7: **a lawful good male dwarf bartender (level 2), formerly of the [[the_rowdy_rockfish|Rowdy Rockfish]] in Otari.** **Volluk abducted him just over a year ago on one of his pre-work walks from the Rockfish up to the graveyard, kept him prisoner in D11 for months, and — impressed by his hardiness — made him the anchor.**
- **Creature — a will-o'-wisp** **gorging on Lasda's terror:** **invisible at first; it flares an angry blue as soon as the heroes try to help.** **The heroes can't rescue Lasda until it's defeated.** **It pursues foes who flee and fights to the death.**
- **Side quest — freeing Lasda:**
  - **Four manacles.** **Use the D8 key, or three successes at DC 22 Thievery per lock** (unless they can work the locks from afar).
  - **Each round a hero works a manacle they're exposed to the necromantic ribbon.**
  - **Once freed and clear of the ribbon, Lasda recovers mobility after 2d4 rounds of gasping.** **He can tell his harrowing story and partly describe D1–D11.**
  - **The heroes may have heard of his disappearance from his worried mother, [[Brelda Venkervale|Brelda]], at the Rowdy Rockfish.** **If they return him she's overjoyed and gives them a family heirloom: a minor sturdy shield emblazoned with an angry-looking rockfish.**
- **Consequences of freeing him:** **the necromantic energy here and in B35 and [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C15. Gauntlight Reading Room · Moderate 3|C15]] vanishes** — **which stops [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] firing the Gauntlight's beam at Otari again, but only temporarily** ([[Otari Ilvashti|Otari's ghost]] explains she's preparing the Gauntlight for its true purpose far below). **Also, the haunts on the upper four levels no longer reset if defeated.** **If Volluk and the Voidglutton both survive and get several days, they find and install a new victim — ideally an NPC whose kidnapping spurs the heroes to act.**

### D10. Scalathrax Pen · Moderate 4
▶ [16:10](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=970)

- **Sensory:** **a rough-hewn cavern strewn with bones and scraps of metal and cloth.** **Three irregular rock passages lead out, plus three secret doors — all three obvious from inside.**
- **Purpose:** **it once merely linked the southwestern and northwestern wings; now it's a pen for three scalathrax.** **One was already sent via the [[Gauntlight]]'s beam (from D9) to [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] in Chapter 2** — **that's the creature the test-fire dropped among the party** *(resolves [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]]'s flag F7, "scalora")*. **The other two await [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]]'s next test.**
- **Creatures — two scalathrax:** **poorly fed, they attack on sight and pursue relentlessly.**

### D11. Torture Chamber · Moderate 4
▶ [16:56](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=1016)

- **Sensory:** **three 5-ft-square iron cages, bars spiked on the inside, hang from chains in the middle of the room.** **Blood-crusted manacles dangle from the chains on the west wall; a stout wooden table with more manacles stands in the SW corner — on it a whip of blood-red leather with a silver handle.**
- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] imprisoned and tortured those lacking the qualities she wanted for fleshwarp experiments or arena battles below** — **when they had nothing left to offer, or she got bored, she sent them to D13 for sacrifice.** **[[Lasda Venkervale|Lasda]] hung in the central cage for many months.**
- **The north cage:** **silver-lined runes on its exterior — the destination of the inactive teleportation circle in [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C5. Spying Hall · no encounter|C5]]** (**C5b** — the SW alcove of C5's Spying Hall; see [Floor 3](floor-03-cult-of-the-canker.md)). **Restore that circle with *awaken portal* and it's a handy way to imprison anyone pushed into the C5 alcove.**
- **Secret door (south):** **DC 20 Perception finds a hidden switch under the table.**
- **Creature — [[Vaulgrist]], an evangelist velstrac:** **bound here by Belcorra's magic for centuries; the endless years instilled listlessness — she rarely even moves.** **Lasda briefly rekindled her hope of cruel amusement, but only a few months into her groundwork he was taken away, and her ennui returned worse.**
  - **She barely bothers with the heroes and morosely tells them to leave and never return.** **No trick: if they leave at once she lets them go.** **If they persist or question her, she sighs in frustration and attacks — no interest in talking; fights until destroyed but won't leave the area.** **With all the manacles here she can use *animate chains* from anywhere in the room.**
- **Treasure:** **the +1 ghost touch whip** — **one of Vaulgrist's favorites, no longer used.** *(**Keep it:** it matters on Level 5 — [[Cratonys]] in [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E2. Large Monster Holding · Moderate 5|E2]] **recognizes it** and stands down.)*

### D12. The Ghostwalk · Moderate 4
▶ [19:30](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=1170)

- **Sensory:** **a wide hall with a 25-ft ceiling; stone sarcophagi carved with tormented, emaciated figures stand upright in alcoves along the walls.** **A 15-ft-wide section of wall has crumbled into a series of ledges dropping to a cavern (D14)** ⚠F6. **Eerie blue lights: the south ones brighter and steady, the north ones flickering softly.**
- **The trophy hall:** **except for the two northernmost alcoves and the alcoves toward D10, each sarcophagus holds the defiled remains of one of [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s enemies — stripped of gear and displayed.** **Stepping up to one turns its stone lid transparent, showing the long-dead body in rags with obviously fatal wounds** (**mostly long-dead heroes with no tie to the AP**).
- **The barrier:** **the corpses were once linked to enslaved ghosts that guarded the hall — but their undead presence has been drawn into a *magical wall at the south end*.** **A large flight of stairs south leads down to [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E1. Upper Shaft · Moderate 5|E1]], but a shimmering wall of ectoplasm blocks it** — **silently shrieking ghosts flit through its swirling mist; the stairwell beyond stays visible and tauntingly inaccessible.** **It acts as a *wall of force* heightened to 9th level, powered by the agonized souls of the heroes displayed here.** **Its creation was an unintended side effect of the [[Gauntlight]]'s test-firing.** **Only the icons of Belcorra's killers can bring it down** (as [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari's ghost]] explains in D18).
- **Secret door (alcove → D10):** **built into the back of the only empty sarcophagus — DC 20 Perception finds the latch that swings it open like a door.**
- **Creatures — two cairn wights** ⚠F2 **(traitorous apprentices) in the two northernmost sarcophagi:** **if anyone comes within 15 ft of them, both lids pop open and they attack** — **relentlessly pursuing foes across the level.**

### D13. Upper Temple of Nhimbaloth · Severe 4
▶ [22:26](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=1346)

- **Sensory:** **a vast, mostly empty octagonal chamber.** **The walls are carved and painted as a sprawling graveyard half-sunk in a swampy morass — every grave in the mural is open and empty.** **Carved shrieking ghosts stream up into the hungry pupil of a malevolent eye at the apex, 30 ft up.** **A 15-ft-diameter platform, raised 3 ft, holds an altar of an irregular rock slab with seven deep indentations — as if a huge seven-fingered hand had gripped it and left grooves.** **A faint blue light pulses now and then inside one of the indentations.** **Small objects are heaped on the altar.**
- **Knowledge:** **DC 25 Religion (Recall Knowledge)** identifies **a shrine to [[Nhimbaloth]], Outer God of despair, ghosts and swamps.** **Her shrines are often bare of statues or benches, symbolizing the emptiness of death; worshippers kneel or prostrate on the floor while priests lead sermons and sacrifices at the altar.**
- **Hazard — despair:** **any living creature entering makes a DC 22 Will save or becomes frightened 1 (frightened 2 on a critical failure) and can't reduce frightened while in the room.** **A critical success grants 24-hour immunity.**
- **Creatures:** **DC 16 Perception from the entrance identifies the altar's heap as four crawling hands** — **they scuttle out and attack.** **When the hands animate, four flickerwisps slither out of the indentations and join in.** **None pursues foes out of the room; with no foes present they return to the altar.** **They fight until destroyed.**
- **The altar:** **produces a new flickerwisp each day at dusk (never more than four at once).** **The malevolent aura and the wisp generation persist *until the heroes place the four [[Roseguard Relics|Roseguard icons]] on the altar stone*** (as [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari's ghost]] directs in D18) — **then the despair ends and no more wisps form.** *(This drops the D12 barrier.)*

### D14. Pavilion · Severe 4
▶ [25:25](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=1525)

- **Sensory:** **an immense, cold, damp cavern; water drips from condensation into a large still lake.** **Thick patches of moist mushrooms, some nearly 4 ft tall, grow along the west shore; a narrow trickle enters from a tunnel to the south.** **A 35-ft stone pavilion stands mid-lake, raised 10 ft on more than a dozen stone columns and encrusted with damp pale fungus.** **Two stone bridges arch from it — to a door in the north wall and a door to the east.** **A faintly flickering campfire burns in the pavilion, smoke curling into the ceiling.** **The fungus is harmless.** **The lake has steep banks and a nearly uniform depth of 15 ft.**
- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] built it as a place to meditate.** **The Vaults' denizens avoid it — "a lingering presence of [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari's ghost]] in D18 subconsciously influences them to stay away."** **Living creatures like its current resident don't feel it.**
- **Creature — [[Jaul Mezmin]], a murderous werewolf:** **grizzled and feral; a violent criminal wanted in [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] for killing several locals 30 years ago.** **His last victim was Ayla Lathenar, wife of [[Keeleno Lathenar]] (owner of [[otari_market|Otari Market]]).** **He hid in plain sight as one of the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 19. Stone Ring Pond · Temple · level 4|Stone Ring Pond]] druids; when found out he escaped by leaping from the Otari cliffs into the sea — the tides dragged him into the sea caves, nearly drowned, and after weeks he found his way here.** **He's lived here ever since: pasty skin, reeking of mushroom and sour sweat, eating mostly cave crickets, with a series of loyal wolves as his only company (when one dies he goes out through the caves to bond with another).** **He once meant to return to Otari; after so many years he knows no other life.**
  - **On spotting the heroes** he staggers to the pavilion's edge and **howls and barks wildly to warn them off** (his wolf howls too).
  - **Recognition:** **DC 27 Society (Recall Knowledge)** realizes **he's the killer who vanished from Otari a generation ago** (*a hero familiar with Jaul's legacy treats the result as one degree of success better*).
  - **If attacked, or if they try to enter his pavilion:** he **assumes hybrid form and attacks.** **If they hang back he keeps howling until someone calms him:** **as soon as they use any *linguistic* skill on him (Lie, Make an Impression, Coerce) he stops, blinks, and remembers he can talk.** **His first words are a halting warning: *this is his territory; they aren't welcome.*** **Initial attitude: Hostile;** **pushed too hard with questions he grows frustrated and attacks from range with his magic.** **He might give his name or reveal who he is, but the paranoid werewolf will likely lose patience.**
  - **Mercy:** **at 30 HP or fewer he begs for mercy and promises a reward — Ayla's necklace.** **Shown mercy, he'll talk more:** **he knows the eastern half of the level (D14–D19)** — **warning of a shadowy ghost on the island to the south (D18) and that anyone entering the Nightmare Stairs to the north (D19) should keep their eyes closed if they want to escape.** **If freed he leaves through the sea caves and flees the region (whether he kills again is your call).**
- **Side quest — the bounty:** **bring Jaul in dead or alive for a 50 gp bounty.** **If they don't recognize him, a hero examining the inscription on his necklace attempts DC 18 Society (DC 13 if affiliated with Otari Market):** success identifies **the name Ayla Lathenar.** **Bringing Keeleno proof the werewolf is dead** (he won't accept his wife's murderer living) **earns a 20% discount on all purchases at Otari Market for life.**

### D15. Twisting Tunnel · no encounter

### D16. Haunted Stairs · no encounter
▶ [31:36](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=1896)

- **Sensory:** **a staircase up from a narrow landing; the south wall facing it flickers and glows with ribbons of eerie green light.** **The stairs lead up to [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C33. Restricted Collection · Severe 3|C33]].**
- **History:** **fleeing through the Vaults, [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari Ilvashi]] stopped here for about an hour to patch wounds and regain his nerve, then went through the D14 door hoping for an escape route — but the enormous otyugh that lived there in those days attacked at once.** **He fled south to D18; the otyugh was too big to reach him there but too stubborn to leave, and he finally died of the disease it gave him** (as at [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B28. Haunted Washroom (Otari's Spirit Echoes) · no encounter|B28]] and [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C3. Haunted Reading Room · no encounter|C3]]). **His spiritual energy remains.**
- **The message:** **when the heroes arrive, another message manifests as glowing light on the wall: *"[[Gauntlight]] is a weapon built to raise/raze [[locations/absalom|Absalom]]. It must be brought down."*** ⚠F4 **The words then slide into a writhing ribbon of footprints leading into D14 and on a panicked, twisting route through D17 into D18.** **The manifestation can appear only once per day.**

### D17. Disposal Pond · no encounter
▶ [33:05](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=1985)

- **Sensory:** **a rusty metal chute protrudes from the ceiling to the west, ending over a pool of slimy water; a huge mound of fungus in a strangely monstrous shape lies under it against the west wall.** **The chute leads up to D7 but is slick — DC 18 Athletics to climb.** **The pool is cold, unpleasant and only 3 ft deep.**
- **The mound:** **the immense otyugh once kept here for trash disposal died long ago; its oddly preserved body is encased in fungus.** **DC 23 Occultism (Recall Knowledge)** identifies it.

### D18. Otari's Room · Severe 4
▶ [33:57](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=2037)

- **Sensory:** **dark water only 3 ft deep; a low rocky islet rising less than a foot above it, covered in pale green mushrooms and mold.** **A human skeleton in rotten leather armor, clutching a rapier, lies in a heap at its center — the remains of [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari Ilvashi]].**
- **History:** **he survived the battle with [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] and a desperate flight through the upper levels — only to be trapped here by the otyugh in D17.** **Weakened, then infected with filth fever from the fight, he lingered on this tiny islet two days before dying.** **His spirit remains both as the strange warnings on the walls throughout the Vaults and as a ghost driven by vengeance on Belcorra and fear the [[Gauntlight]] might again harm his hometown of [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].**
- **The ghost:** **as the heroes near, it rises from the skeleton, looking as he did in the late stage of filth fever, with a translucent green shade.** **He can't leave the area; the heroes are the first potentially helpful adventurers to reach him, so he's eager to help.**
- **Creatures — a will-o'-wisp and two flickerwisps** **that have tormented him for years dart out to protect their "fading victim" before he can speak** (**he can only warn the heroes about "the eyes of the [[Nhimbaloth|Empty Death]]"**). **In the fight he helps as he can:** **the aberration(s) learned long ago to stay out of his reach, but he can strike them by briefly possessing a hero's shadow** ⚠F3. **After the wisps fall, he provides a wealth of information.**
- **Treasure — his gear (he urges the heroes to take it):** **his *infiltrator's thieves' tools* — one of the four icons for the D12 barrier** · **a dagger of venom** · **a sneaky key** · **+1 studded leather armor.**

#### What Otari Knows
▶ [36:20](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=2180)

**Role: he presents the task needed to reach the lower levels.** **He remembers little of his life, but becomes very emotional on learning that the other [[Roseguard]] survived and founded a town named for him.** **His dungeon knowledge is centuries old, but his supernatural link gives glimpses of its inner workings and key players.** **Convey these points, as answers or volunteered:**

1. **Belcorra harbored a grudge against Absalom and meant to use the Gauntlight as a weapon against the city.** **The Roseguard never learned its use — only that it could transport creatures over great distances and animate the dead with its light.** **None of the other Roseguard knew of the Vaults beneath Gauntlight; [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] learned only in his last days.**
2. **Something about the Vaults amplifies ghosts, spectral undead and haunts.** **He first thought it was a Gauntlight side effect, but now suspects a much more ancient, ominous source deep below.** **He knows nothing of Nhimbaloth, but if told, suspects her influence.**
3. **Belcorra is now a ghost who can't move far from her anchor.** **He can sense her deep underground — and her range is slowly growing.**
4. **He suspects she means to fully reactivate the Gauntlight and start her long-delayed attack on Absalom — she must be put to rest first.** **He doesn't know how, but believes clues await in the deeper levels.**
5. **The D12 barrier** is **powered by the souls of Belcorra's defeated enemies.** **The Roseguard finally killed her, so *their psychic resonance can drop it*:** **the heroes must place the four [[Roseguard Relics|Roseguard icons]] on the altar in D13.** **Otari's thieves' tools (here with his bones) are his icon.** **The others: the brooch of the cleric [[Aesephna Menhemes]] (bearing the symbol of her god, [[Erastil]]** ⚠F8**), the spellbook of the wizard [[Zarmavdian]], and the trusty longsword of the fighter [[Vol Rajani]]** — **all found in Otari (the first part of Chapter 5).**

- **His fate:** **once the heroes take on facing Belcorra and opening the way down, his soul is free to travel to its final reward.** **Make sure he's shared all of this before he fades.** ***Darker option:* instead of freedom, his spirit is captured by the Temple of Nhimbaloth at the bottom of the Vaults** — **the heroes learn this through a vision or nightmare, or see his tormented soul dragged downward; he stays trapped until Belcorra is defeated.**

### D19. The Forever Stairs · no encounter
▶ [40:14](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=2414)

- **Sensory:** **a 5-ft-wide hallway whose walls shimmer and ripple with faint wisps of pale blue energy.** **A staircase west leads up; one east leads down; both end at small landings.** **It should quickly become clear that something is very wrong:** **the hallway loops back on itself — go clockwise and the stairs always lead up, yet you end where you began after a full circuit.**
- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] made this distorted-space effect early on, experimenting with ways to deliver her monsters directly into [[locations/absalom|Absalom]]'s streets.** **She dropped spatial warping as too inefficient at scale but kept this hall as a "defensive moat" around her quarters.**
- **Hidden door:** **the doors to D13 and D14 are always present, but the alcove and door to D20 are hidden by an illusory wall (5th-level *illusory object*; Will DC to disbelieve 26).** **A hero who succeeds at DC 20 Perception (searching the north hall) or DC 20 Arcana/Occultism (investigating it) spots anomalies in the blue energy pointing to that wall section.**
- **XP: 30** the first time the heroes reach D20.

### D20. Belcorra's Suite · Extreme 4
▶ [42:01](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=2521)

- **Layout:** **four angled rooms (D20A–D20D) with the same basic description; each also has a plain stone archway filled with blank stone — an inactive portal to the depths of the Vaults.** **Each room has two exits, one on a semicircular step a foot higher in the dusty floor.**
- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s personal suite while she oversaw construction; she abandoned it for a deeper home, taking her belongings.** **As a ghost her spiritual energy reacted as [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s did — manifesting lingering images.** **Each time the heroes enter one of the four rooms they see a brief ghostly image of it as it was, with a vision of Belcorra herself** (they'll likely not study them at first, because of the creature here):
  - **D20A — sitting room** (two chairs, low table): **Belcorra meets [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]] *as he was in life, a drow*; silently, she talks animatedly while he listens attentively.**
  - **D20B — lounge** (two plush divans): **Belcorra relaxes reading a large tome.** **DC 18 Perception reads its title: *The [[Whispering Reeds]]* (now in [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C35. Secure Collection · no encounter|C35]]).**
  - **D20C — private dining room** (a well-set table): **she sits alone drinking wine and enjoying an elegantly plated roast arm that looks disturbingly human.**
  - **D20D — bedroom** (large bed along the west wall): **the north stone arch shimmers as a portal opens; Belcorra steps through with a cruel smile and blood on her hands.**
- **Creature — the [[Voidglutton]]:** **a powerful, horrific entity that has lived in these chambers since Belcorra left.** **Unlike many of her minions it came of its own free will, drawn across dimensions by echoes of [[Nhimbaloth]]'s presence.** **It volunteered to guard the upper shrine (D13), but took over these rooms when she moved deeper.** **It basks in the latent fears and pains of the dungeon's haunts and ghosts.**
  - **When Belcorra rose as a ghost it visited her, learned of her plan to reactivate the [[Gauntlight]], and — knowing it would take time — helped refine the test-fire plan and carried it out with Volluk's aid (Chapter 2).** **It has lurked here since, expecting her to contact it again for the next stage.**
  - **It roams D20A–D20D** (it finds Belcorra's echoes soothing) — **an equal chance it's in any of the four when the heroes arrive.** **It notices the intrusion immediately and moves to confront.**
  - **No interest in negotiation; attacks at once and fights to the death, but won't pursue heroes who flee these rooms.** **If it clearly has the upper hand it may offer to let most of them flee — if they leave a single survivor to torment to death over the next few months.**
- *(Repo: the Voidglutton is an **extreme** encounter — a level-8 creature against a level-4 party.)*

### D21. Trophy Vault · no encounter
▶ [46:42](https://youtu.be/4Cc9qDDY20o?t=2802)

- **Sensory:** **the walls of this octagonal chamber are sheathed in foul, moist webbing of stringy, semi-transparent slime.** **Three bodies are mounted in it "as if on display": a lavender-skinned elf, the legless body of a spider-like humanoid, and a hideous humanoid with transparent flesh.**
- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] took all her treasure when she left, but the [[Voidglutton]] has used this place as a den for years** and **keeps a few favorite trophies here — creatures whose minds it fed on and found particularly delightful, with some of their potent gear.** **The bodies are encased in its ectoplasmic webbing and must be cut free to be searched; once free they swiftly decay** (no longer held in stasis).
- **Treasure:** **the upper torso of a drider from the Level 6 fleshwarping labs — a vial of lesser mist form elixir** · **a drow outcast from [[Yldaris]] (Level 9) — a strand of holy prayer beads on one wrist** · **an urdefhan (Level 9) — a +1 striking composite longbow in a case.**
