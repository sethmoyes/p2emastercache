## The Big Picture (GM-only)

**What the middle levels are for.** The upper levels were [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s personal space; the middle levels were **for work**. She trawled the Darklands for predators and bloodthirsty aberrations, but feral power wasn't enough — she needed to mold them into an army to assault [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].

| Level | Original purpose | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **5 — Arena / Training Grounds** | Gladiators fight for the right to lead her monstrous troops; a test of minions' and prisoners' resolve; she hoped to run a premier gladiator facility and cherry-pick the best | Dumping ground for the seugathi's failed experiments |
| **6 — Laboratories** | Formerly support rooms for monsters and trainers, plus a **preserve for large beasts**; repurposed for the seugathi | Seugathi fleshwarping labs under **[[Jafaki]]** |
| **7 — Prison** | Smaller; Belcorra never meant to hold many captives (displeasing creatures became raw flesh for Jafaki's experiments or food for her monsters). **[[Urevian]]**, one of her chief administrators — a **contract devil** — *insisted* on building it | A warped mirror of Hell |

**The seugathi.** Wormlike aberrations with a "curious eagerness to serve" Belcorra, met in the Darklands. Under Jafaki they sought to perfect fleshwarping and create flawless monsters.

**After Belcorra's death.** A decade into building the dungeon, the **[[Roseguard]]** killed Belcorra (they had no idea about any of this). The Abomination Vaults fell into disarray at once. Urevian tried to assert control; Jafaki's aberrant creations fought his forces to a **standstill**. The stalemate has barely shifted in centuries: Jafaki just wants peace to keep experimenting; Urevian "idly prepared" for the surface invasion his contract demanded, knowing it was almost impossible with Belcorra dead.

**The Darklands traffic.** Word of the seugathi's skill spread, and "arrogant or desperate creatures" arrive seeking transformations — few consider the results improvements. **[[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] knows nothing of this**; it all comes from far below the middle levels.

**Belcorra's return (a few years ago).** The dungeon hummed with power. Jafaki (research-focused) missed the significance; **Urevian did not** — he could finally fulfil his contract. But he'd already spent centuries on a well-drafted **loophole** that lets him simply return to Hell *without* helping her at all:

- He needs a specific mortal soul: **[[Vol Rajani]]'s** (one of the Roseguard). Vol is long dead, but the clause still applies if he receives the soul of **Vol's last descendant, [[Carman Rajani]].**
- He couldn't collect it while Belcorra was merely dead; now that she's back he'll **abandon his extensive preparations** if someone brings him Carman's soul — and he has **selected the dungeon delvers from Otari** (the PCs) to do it.

> **Why Carman's fate matters:** the chapter synopsis says the party's choice about Carman is "a choice they don't yet realize will have a meaningful repercussion later." The repercussion is Urevian's contract ([[floor-07-soul-keepers|Floor 7]]).

### Carman's Background
▶ [7:42](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=462)

How a blacksmith became a thief:

1. **Childhood.** Grew up on stories of Vol's bravery, dedication and skill. As a boy he didn't understand why his family sword hung in the library instead of his house — his parents insisted it was *theirs*.
2. **Young adult.** Worked at [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s smithy, **[[blades_for_glades|Blades for Glades]]**; later **won the shop in a lucky wager** against the prior owner. Never forgot the sword. Officially the **Menhemes family claims ownership** of Vol's blade but lets it stay at the library as "purported generosity" — which [[Carman Rajani|Carman]] found offensive.
3. **The break.** One summer his **parents were waylaid and slain by bandits** on the road from [[locations/absalom|Absalom]]. He became a broken man: erratic work at the smithy, stopped relaxing with friends or helping the community.
4. **The spiral.** Squandered his inheritance on entertainment, fine clothes and **rare whiskey**; spent more time gambling and drinking at **[[crooks_nook|Crook's Nook]]** than at the smithy. When the money ran out he had a **sizable debt** and owed favors.
5. **Petty crime.** Grifting travelers, hustling them at **knife-throwing boards** at Crook's Nook — but his vices outpaced the earnings, so he moved to **outright thievery**, apparently under **[[Yinyasmera]]'s** tutelage ⚠F9. He held **"Ace of Blades"** — the title given to the best knife-thrower at Crook's Nook — so long it became his nickname among fellow **[[Osprey Club]]** members.
6. **Settled routine.** Smith by day, carousing at Crook's Nook by night, occasional petty larceny. Then he heard rumors people thought he was wasting his life. It stung, and he resolved to **run for mayor against [[Oseph Menhemes|Oseph]]** to prove the Rajanis were equal to the Menhemes.
7. **Four campaigns:**
   - **1st:** nobody took it seriously — a joke.
   - **2nd:** a platform of nothing but **sharp personal attacks** on the Menhemes family. The town felt the joke had gone sour.
   - **3rd:** answered jibes with **violence** (brawls "to prove he was a better man")… then actually **put in effort to help the community** and lost by a much smaller margin. He took it as proof that **kindness can't get you further than your fists**.
   - **4th:** forced a campaign with **open bribes and blackmail**. Oseph won by a **landslide**, showing up on election day **wearing the [[Roseguard Relics#Rajani's Cooperative Blade|Cooperative Blade]]** as celebratory regalia.
8. **The conclusion.** Carman decided the magic sword must be the key — everyone in Otari knows it **brings the town good luck**, so whoever holds it "has the town in their pocket." As Vol's heir he *deserved* it. He begged Oseph to sell; Oseph suspected a scheme (or something tied to Carman's crimes/debts) and answered with **delays, excuses and price increases**. Carman decided: *if he wanted the family sword back, he'd have to take it.*

### The Theft
▶ [11:16](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=676)

- **While the heroes were in the Abomination Vaults**, [[Carman Rajani|Carman]] crept into the [[dawnflower_library|Dawnflower Library]] and **started a small fire in the book restoration room**. While staff dealt with it he **broke the display case** and fled with the sword.
- Staff put the fire out **after an hour**, no serious injuries, only a few lost books — "a miracle the acolytes attribute to **[[Sarenrae]]'s** protection."
- By the time anyone noticed the theft, Carman was **already on his way to Smuggler's Refuge**.
- The town guard quickly makes him the **prime suspect** (he'd pestered the mayor about the sword for years). A few **witnesses saw someone matching his description** running from the library "clutching a cloak over some long object."

- She asks them to **find [[Carman Rajani|Carman]] and bring him back alive** to face trial and an appropriate punishment.

### Crook's Nook
▶ [13:41](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=821)

- Asking around brings the owner, **[[Yinyasmera]]**, over. She **brazenly asks for 10 gp** — "a special fee for food and drinks."
- Heroes who **pay up, or succeed at DC 20 Diplomacy** to request information, get her to open up: [[Carman Rajani|Carman]] "has been a little too bold to remain in her good graces" and **this theft was the last straw.** She says he has likely **gone into hiding at Smuggler's Refuge** outside town.
- If Yinyasmera **already has a favorable opinion** of the heroes from earlier actions, she gives the information **free**.

### Smuggler's Refuge · Low 5
▶ [14:21](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=861)

**Setting.** A cave **about an hour from town**, a short walk through the forest **north of an abandoned fish camp**. Too far inland for heavy cargo, but once busy with smugglers moving lighter goods — they stashed loot in the cave's many **niches**, planning to return later or to use it as a **drop point** for buyers. With smuggling in decline, [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s young people now come here **for a break from the lumber town.** (A map appears in the book.)

**Sensory.** Rotted crates, forgotten clothes and debris — occasionally used, rarely cleaned. A **cold campfire ringed with logs** at the cave's center. **Faint mildew and smoke** hang in still air. Dripping water, a mineral tang, broken crates, moldy bedding, old campfires. [[Carman Rajani|Carman]] first built a fire, then put it out when he realized the cave **doesn't vent smoke.** A few old cots, heaps of discarded clothes, empty crates; Carman has taken **the best cot and the freshest clothes.**

**The secret door.** In the **east wall**: an exceptionally well-hidden secret door to Level 5. **DC 28 Perception** to find. **Even when found it won't open from this side** — the mechanism swings the heavy stone wall *inward*, and a **cave-in on the other side** blocks it. The heroes can clear it **from the other side, in area E7** (see E7).

**Carman — how he's found:**

| Situation | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Unaware of the heroes** | Sits on his cot **chewing dried fish, turning the [[Roseguard Relics#Rajani's Cooperative Blade\|Cooperative Blade]] over and over.** Undecided: **return the sword and hope for a stiff fine**, or **flee to his ancestral homeland, Nidal**. |
| **Aware of the heroes** | **Hides behind a crate; Stealth for initiative.** He knows them by reputation and assumes they've come to take him prisoner, so he **fights**. Shouts: *"You'll never take me alive!"* and *"This is my sword! I took what's rightfully mine!"* |
| **He surrenders when…** | the heroes plainly **don't reciprocate his aggression**, **or** he's reduced to **fewer than 30 HP**. |

### Talking with Carman
▶ [17:03](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1023)

If he gets to explain before being taken in, he tries to justify the theft. (Cleaned-up lines — good to voice at the table.)

> *"I tell you, it's mine! The sword is mine! How can I steal something that already belongs to me? It belonged to [[Vol Rajani]] — I'm her only surviving descendant. That uptight Menhemes says it belongs to his family just because a pack of **kobolds** had their hands on it for a while and his ancestors snatched it from them. He says an heirloom is an heirloom, and he's right — but it's **my** heirloom. Look, I tried to buy it off him. Every time I saved enough coin he raised the price — 50 gold, then 100, then 200, then he said it wasn't for sale. I had no choice but to take it. It belongs to Vol. To the Rajani family. **It belongs to me.**"*

He pleads for release and stoutly claims his right. Likely questions and his answers:

| The heroes ask… | [[Carman Rajani\|Carman]] answers… |
|---|---|
| **Why is the sword so important to you?** | "It belonged to my family. Vol was the best of the [[Roseguard]] and the best of the Rajanis, and I wanted to be **mayor**. I've tried four times and always lost to [[Oseph Menhemes\|Oseph]]. If I had the sword — people say it gives the town good luck. If I had it, they'd **want** me to be mayor, so I could give them luck." |
| **Why do you want to be mayor?** | "To be in charge. There's no reason a Menhemes should **always** run this place. Oseph says it's because his family descends from heroes — well, **so do I.** I can do a much better job than that old bastard." |
| **Why did you set fire to the library?** | "The fire was just a distraction. I didn't want to hurt anyone — and they should've put it out sooner. It's not like I burned anything but some paper." |
| **Even if it's rightfully yours, that's arson. Don't you deserve jail?** | "Arson? Hm. Vandalism, maybe. I'll **pay the fine** for starting a fire — but the sword's mine." |
| **Why should we let you go?** | "Because I'll pay you. **50 gold. 100.** Take it all — but I keep my sword." |
| **How did you get that much gold?** | "Saved it. Borrowed some. Okay — borrowed **most** of it. Look, I just *had* to get that sword." |
| **If we let you go, what will you do?** | "I don't know. Stay here a while, maybe, and think it out. I guess I can't really go back to town now. Maybe I'll head to **Nidal** — they say Vol was from there. Some say she was **royalty.** Maybe I can get the life that's due to me." |
| **If we let you go, can we borrow the sword first?** | "As long as you're not taking it — or me — back to town, I'll **go with you** to do whatever you need to do." |

### Carman's Fate
▶ [20:04](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1204)

[[Carman Rajani|Carman]] will go along with almost any demand, **especially if the party has shown they can beat him in a fight.**

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Let him go** | The party still needs the Blade to breach the barrier, so **Carman goes with them for that task** (he won't let the sword out of his sight) — **but he doesn't fight** in the Abomination Vaults. If the heroes run into trouble after the barrier drops, he **retreats to the cave** to consider his options and **likely stays a few days.** |
| **Turn him in** | [[Lardus Longsaddle\|Captain Longsaddle]] tosses him in a cell and **jokes about throwing away the key.** Longsaddle tells the mayor the heroes have the Blade; **[[Oseph Menhemes\|Oseph]] is fine with them borrowing it as long as it ends up back with the town.** Longsaddle **takes Carman's money** as a fund to repay costs of his crimes but **lets the heroes keep Carman's other equipment.** |
| **Kill him** | Townsfolk understand it's a **risk when apprehending a criminal.** Oseph **arranges Carman's burial in the [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari\|Otari]] cemetery** and lets the heroes borrow the Blade. |

**Treasure (Smuggler's Refuge).** Besides what Carman carries: his **adventurer's pack** near the fire holds a **week of improvised rations and two waterskins** ⚠F10. **DC 20 Perception** while searching the cave finds a **long-forgotten bottle of aged whiskey** in a pebble-covered niche — worth **3 gp** ⚠F10.

**XP.** **30 XP** for capturing Carman **alive**, *in addition to* combat XP for defeating him.

## Part 2 — Dorianna Menhemes and the Motley Man

*A slow-burn subplot that runs across Floors 5–7. The threat sits in **[[floor-07-soul-keepers#G18. War Room · Moderate 7|G18]] (Level 7)** — the party can't reach it yet, and **[[Ysondkhelir]] never physically approaches [[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]].***

| When | What's happening | Party's window |
|---|---|---|
| **Two nights after the party opens the seal** | Nightmares begin ("Dreams") | Notice, investigate |
| **Days–weeks later** | "Decline" — daytime exhaustion, imaginary friend, erratic and childish | Diplomacy to earn trust; gather two clues |

### Dorianna's Dreams
▶ [22:01](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1321)

- Mysterious events beyond the dungeon: **[[Dorianna Menhemes]]**, the mayor's **eldest daughter** (a teenager), is on the cusp of developing **psychic powers.** No one on the surface knows.
- **[[Ysondkhelir]]**, a **denizen of Leng in area [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G18. War Room · Moderate 7|G18]]**, has sensed it. With the barrier to the lower levels down he can **twist her dreams into nightmares and take the role of her savior**, intensifying the plan over **weeks of nighttime adventures** in the **Dimension of Dreams**.
- His goal: eventually **thrust Dorianna physically into the Dimension of Dreams** to augment other denizens' plots.
- **Two nights after the heroes open the seal**, Dorianna starts having **horrifying nightmares**: she wakes **several times a night screaming, clutching a stuffed griffin she hasn't touched in years.** She can't recall specifics beyond her fear and **a man shrouded in mismatched fabric — the "motley man" — who comforts her.**
- **How the party hears:**
  - **Menhemes support earned** → they've met Dorianna and hear about the nightmares from **the other children.**
  - **Otherwise** → **Mayor [[Oseph Menhemes|Oseph]]** seeks out whichever hero is best known as a **healer** or an **authority on occultism**, mentions the nightmares and the motley man "who acts as a protector and savior within them," and asks them to **discreetly look into it.**

### Asking Wrin
▶ [23:47](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1427)

- [[Oseph Menhemes|Oseph]] views **[[Wrin Sivinxi]]** ([[wrins_wonders|Wrin's Wonders]]) as an **unreliable eccentric** and won't think to involve her — but she's an **expert in occultism**.
- If asked, she **gladly accompanies** the heroes, **so long as she doesn't have to meet the girl indoors** ⚠F13.
- **If the party has no one with occultism training**, Wrin is the built-in solution.

### Examining Dorianna
▶ [24:22](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1462)

- Even at first exam she looks **gaunt and pale** "as if she's caught a severe malady."
- **DC 15 Medicine** confirms she's **in good physical health.**
- The heroes **initially find no magic or curse.**
- **Searching her room** finds several of her **childhood toys brought back out** — she says vaguely she misses them and wants to play with them again.
- Her nightmares are **tenacious**: any magic used to help her sleep **fails** unless the caster succeeds at a **secret DC 27 counteract check.**
- **XP: 30** for taking **care and time** to help her, even without figuring out what's wrong.

### Dorianna's Decline
▶ [25:19](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1519)

- **Nightmares lessen**, but she's **exhausted and anxious by day.** She talks to the [[Ysondkhelir|motley man]] as an **imaginary friend even when awake**, including during activities with others. Her mood is **erratic and childish**; she **scratches absently at her right hand.** Her sibling wants nothing to do with her; even her parents find her hard to bear.
- She's afflicted by Ysondkhelir's **outcast's curse** spell. The heroes might **detect or remove it** — but **he recasts it the following night.**
- **Earning her trust:** **DC 26 Diplomacy.**
  - **+4 circumstance bonus** if the party has **[[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#Area 3. Menhemes Manor · Manor · level 4|Menhemes Manor]]'s support.**
  - **One hero per day** may attempt it.
  - **Critical failure:** wait **1d4 days** before anyone tries again.
  - **Success:** she insists the **motley man is real** and discusses **one** of two subjects (Gifts / Wrist Writing). A **later day's success** — or an **initial critical success** — gets **both.**

### Gifts
▶ [26:40](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1600)

- The [[Ysondkhelir|motley man]] gives her gifts in the form of her **old childhood toys**; the best is the **stuffed griffin.**
- A cursory exam shows **something hard sewn inside.** Opening it reveals a **large, flawless ruby, worth 30 gp.**
- **The ruby returns:** even if the heroes take it, the **next time [[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]] dreams it reappears inside another childhood toy** — it's a key to her and doesn't leave her for long. (See *The Ruby*, below.)

### Wrist Writing
▶ [27:14](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1634)

- [[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]] absently points out **bruises on her right wrist** she can't remember getting.
- A hero **trained in Occultism** can attempt **DC 22 Occultism** to decipher writing in the bruises. **Speaking Aklo: +2 circumstance bonus.**
  - **Success:** the words relate to **dream-traveling rituals.**
  - **Critical success:** the word **"Leng"** appears among the bruises.
- Either clue points to a **malevolent extraplanar creature — a denizen of Leng.** (Leng: a terrifying extraplanar location **just past the Dimension of Dreams**, home to cruel, sinister people.)
- **DC 24 Occultism** realizes the **nature** of the creature. **Both clues obtained → any Occultism-trained hero realizes it without a check.**
- Even so, the heroes **can't do much yet** — [[Ysondkhelir]] never gets physically near her.
- **XP: 80** for discovering a denizen of Leng is involved.

### Dorianna's Madness
▶ [28:40](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=1720)

**Trigger:** about when the heroes **finish Level 6 (Laboratories)** and **start investigating Level 7 (Prison)**.

### Psychic Patterns · exploration activity

[[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]]'s link to [[Ysondkhelir]] is now so strong the party can **find him through it.** A hero **trained in Occultism** realizes they can learn more by **examining her psychic patterns while she dreams.**

- **Cost:** **8 hours.** **Check: DC 23 Occultism.**
- **Success:** learn the **first fact they don't already know.** **Critical success:** **two facts.** **Critical failure:** a **false fact of your invention.**
- **The facts (in order):**
  1. **Strong link** between Dorianna's mind and the Dimension of Dreams; a denizen of Leng has strengthened the conduit; **unless it's destroyed, Dorianna will eventually be drawn wholly into that dimension and lost.**
  2. **Images of the denizen's lair:** a **stone chamber with a very old map of the Inner Sea region painted across it**, and a **map of tattered paper pieces that together form a detailed map of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]].** ⚠F13
  3. The denizen's **name (Ysondkhelir)** and an **accurate mental image** of him.

### The Ruby (attuned to Dorianna) · activity

The ruby in [[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]]'s possession can **track [[Ysondkhelir]].** She doesn't care if they take it, but it is **attuned to her and returns each night** — so the party must **come back to [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] to recover it every day.**

> **Activation** — 10 minutes (envision) · **Frequency:** once per day
> **Effect:** you focus on the ruby; it casts a **5th-level *locate*** that **only targets Ysondkhelir.**

### Payoff

- After [[Ysondkhelir]] is defeated, the ruby **no longer returns to [[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]]** and becomes an **unusually shaped wand of *locate***.
- **Dorianna swiftly recovers.**
- **Mayor [[Oseph Menhemes|Oseph]]** effusively thanks the party and arranges for **each hero** to receive **one item of 7th level or lower of their choice, from [[locations/absalom|Absalom]], at his expense.** Items take **a week** to arrive.
- **XP: 120** for freeing Dorianna from Ysondkhelir's clutches.

### E15. Waiting Room · hazard
▶ [1:04:12](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=3852)

- **Hazard:** **each minute** a creature spends here **with any exposed skin**, it must attempt a **saving throw** or contract **fungal rot** (**level 6 disease**).

### E16. Administrator's Antechamber · no encounter

- **Sensory:** a sitting room **glowing with a pleasantly crackling fire** in a **web-choked fireplace** on the east wall. The **magic sustaining the fire has lasted centuries.** **Harmless spiders** live in the fireplace, drawn by the insect-attracting light.
- **The door (northwest):** a **large divan** is pushed against it; **several pitons** are hammered into the gap between the stone door and its frame.

### E17. Imprisoned Administrator · Severe 5
▶ [1:06:13](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=3973)

- **Sensory:** a **glowing circle of runes** covers the floor. A **desk against the north wall** is heaped with **parchment covered in cramped writing.** The **splintered remains of a bed** are piled in a corner.
- **Wards:** the occupant **warded the room against teleportation and ethereal travel.**
- **History:** in the chaos after [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s fall, someone **locked the arena administrator [[Chafkhem]] in here.** Because he'd **warded his own room against interdimensional travel, he was effectively imprisoned** and **starved.** The **Osirian wizard** cobbled together reagents to **mummify himself with parchment**, hoping to **escape and take revenge on [[Jafaki]]**, whom he believes ordered him locked away. (The reward condition below hints he's wrong: it names **the imp [[Szek]]** as the possible real culprit. Repo: Szek is in **[[floor-07-soul-keepers#G9. Observation Room · no rating|G9]]** and did lock Chafkhem away.)
- **Failed escape:** he's tried to make a **temporary portal through the wall**, but **one mistaken assumption ruined all his calculations** — a source of great frustration for the **vainglorious mummy.**
- **The magic circle:** gives him a **+2 status bonus to skill checks when performing rituals**, and he's **quickened** inside it — **the extra action may only be used to sustain a spell.**
- **Creature — Chafkhem** (male **mummy ritualist**, **level 8**):
  - When the heroes unseal the room he **holds up his parchment-wrapped arms to show no harm.** He's **genuinely thankful and quite charming by nature** — and **as bossy in undeath as in life**, soon **demanding** (not requesting) information on the **current state of the seugathi and the Abomination Vaults.**
  - **If not indulged**, he **decides to kill them and animate them as undead who "don't talk back nearly as much."**
- **Side quest — working with Chafkhem:**
  - **Initial attitude: Indifferent.** Improve with **DC 27** (= his Will DC) via **Diplomacy (Make an Impression)**, or **Deception (Lie)**, or **Arcana (Recall Knowledge)** if it impresses him.
  - **Good side:** he speaks **with open contempt about the seugathi** (Belcorra let them run their fleshwarping lab on the level below, **displacing the arena functions he controlled**). He doesn't know how many remain but names their leader: **Jafaki**, "an alchemist and fleshwarper," whom he **blames for locking him in to die** centuries ago. He wants **revenge on Jafaki** and suggests the heroes take it for him.
  - **He knows Belcorra is dead.** If told she's **returned as an undead**, he's concerned and **makes plans to leave the Abomination Vaults for his native Osirion.** *Whether to let an evil mummy walk is the party's call.*
  - **Departure:** he needs **2d4 days** to put his papers in order and **replicate the arcane patterns of his magic circle.**
  - **Reward if the heroes return in that time** with **proof that they killed Jafaki** *or* **proof that the imp Szek orchestrated his imprisonment**: gratitude plus
    - a **sketch of the entire Arena level** including **every secret door and passage**, and
    - the **magical passphrase that disables the trap in E20.**
    - He has no blank parchment, so he **writes on his spellbook page containing *freedom of movement*** — the heroes can **later learn a spell from that page** in addition to having the map.
- **Treasure — Chafkhem's desk:** **hundreds of pieces of parchment**, every inch written on (for years it was the only medium he had). They **serve as his spellbook** (heroes can **learn a spell** from them) and contain the **awaken portal** and **create undead** rituals. **Any hero who investigates the papers finds both rituals.**

### E20. Secret Hallway · Severe 5 (hazard)
▶ [1:13:36](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=4416)

- **Sensory:** **bones and dismembered limbs** litter an **irregular, branching hallway.** The passages are **jagged**, with blocks of **one-way transparent stone** showing the **gladiators' quarters** (opaque from inside the rooms, undetectable; touching one lets you **hear the room via permanent *clairaudience***).
- **Purpose:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] and [[Chafkhem]] spied on the gladiators from here**, paranoid about **insurrection.** When neither was present they **armed a deadly trap** to protect it. The severed limbs belonged to **seugathi** ⚠F12 **and several fleshwarped creatures** who **tried and failed** to get past it over the past years.
- **Secret doors:** the **two secret doors** in the branching hall — to **E14** and **E24** — are **obvious from this side.**
- **Hazard — shuffling scythe blades (level 8):** the **15 ft × 25 ft junction** where the branches meet is covered with **dozens of pressure plates**; **anyone moving anywhere in that area triggers it.** The **three narrow holes** have no plates, but the **blades pass through them.** The trap **lashes out with its blades, retracts them, then secretly shuffles them** so the next strike is **hard to predict.** *(Disable with the **passphrase from Chafkhem** — see E17.)*
- **Currently:** it **interferes with the seugathi's efforts to explore here.**

- **Meaning:** it shows how the arena's greatest warriors **would be sent into battle by the [[Gauntlight]]'s magic** — meant to **spur gladiators to fight harder** for the **right to battle on the surface in [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s name.**
- **Callback:** the heroes will remember this function from the Gauntlight's **recent test firing that awakened [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s dead and sent a **scalathrax** into their midst in Chapter 2.**

### E25. Gladiators' Mead Hall · Moderate 5
▶ [1:19:13](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=4753)

- **Sensory:** **overturned tables and benches of petrified wood** lie around the edges of an **expansive chamber**, mixed with **pale bones.** **Preserved monster heads and silver plaques** line the walls; a **glowing circle of runes** is etched in the **east alcove.**
- **Purpose:** gladiators' **leisure hall.** The heads are **creatures killed for sport: a basilisk, a chimera, and three manticores.** The **plaques** commemorate arena victories, **dated within a 10-year span, 4235 AR–4244 AR** (matches the repo timeline: [[Gauntlight]] finished 4234 AR; [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] died 4244 AR). The **sturdy furniture, though upended, is intact.**
- **South doors → a very steep staircase down to the Testing Grounds ([[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F9. Testing Grounds · Moderate 6|F9]]).** (As heard: "rarely did anyone descend these stairs, though losers from the fights below ascended them.")
- **The teleportation circle:** **permanent, active,** connects to the **Testing Grounds directly beneath (F9)**; **works both ways** but **does not connect** to the portal-chamber network.
  - Arrival in the Testing Grounds: a **blast of pale blue fire and a loud clanging** — **dramatic only, not bright or hot enough to hurt.**
  - **Going down:** a **sigil on your forehead reads "Challenger"** in **Aklo.** **Coming back up:** it reads **"Victor."** A **new casting of the sigil replaces the old.**
- **Creature — bone gladiator (variant skeletal hulk, level 7):** the **scattered bones of gladiators slaughtered here right after Belcorra's fall** still hold the "grim memory of death and combat." When a living creature approaches they **rattle and slide together into a massive gladiator built from the bones of several humanoids.** It **pursues fleeing foes** but is **too big to fit through any door out of the room** — so **escape is easy.** **Fights until destroyed.**

### E26. Arena Balcony · no encounter (see F26)
▶ [1:22:03](https://youtu.be/Fn5ZiA7VjKM?t=4923)

- **This is only the top half of the arena.** The **arena floor ([[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F26. Arena Floor · Severe 6|F26]])** holds most of the threat.
- **Layout:** a **wide walkway** rings a **cavernous chamber**, looking out over a **stone arena floor 30 ft below.** **Six balconies with built-in seating** give the best view; each has a **bright torch** lighting the whole arena while **leaving the viewing level in shadow.** An **enormous metal gong** hangs from **thick chains** at the east end. **Two steep staircases** lead down from the **central balcony on the north side** to the arena floor.
- **History:** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] and her guests watched the blood sport from **sumptuous seating**, **cheering** as gladiators fought each other or **terrifying monsters.**
- **Controls:** the **north central balcony** has **two large levers and crank wheels** controlling the **portcullises at the bottom of the stairs** to the arena floor.
- **Watch out:** anyone exploring the balcony likely **draws the attention of the two basilisks** glaring from the Grand Concourse (E10). From here the heroes can **easily see the ⚠F4 and the shanrigol behemoth on the arena floor** — unless they're **absurdly noisy or attack the behemoth, it stays dormant** ("probably for the best if the heroes are still only 5th level").
- **Secret door (east balcony):** **DC 25 Perception** finds it *and* the **special series of knocks** that turns the door **ghostly and incorporeal** so the heroes can pass through ⚠F15.
- **Treasure:** the **six burning torches** are all **ever-burning torches**, easy to remove.
