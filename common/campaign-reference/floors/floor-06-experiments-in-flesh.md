▶ [0:42](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=42)

The Level 6 rooms **support the gladiator games in the arena above**, but the level also holds **extensive laboratories** for creating "increasingly bizarre and powerful creatures." **A small team of seugathi** pursue their fleshwarping experiments **while resisting encroachment from the devils below.** The heroes meet these wormlike monsters — and also find **a strangely civilized tavern in the midst of the dungeon.**

### F1. Central Shaft · Low 6
▶ [3:18](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=198)

- **Layout:** the **wide spiral staircase continues down**, levelling out long enough to pass a **single door east** and a **hallway west to a double door.** Between them, a **rounded cloudy window** overlooks a **large pool in a natural cavern (F4).** A **dim orange glow and a sulfurous stench** rise from far below. Stairs lead **up to [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E1. Upper Shaft · Moderate 5|E1]]** and **down to [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G1. Lower Shaft · no encounter|G1]]**; the stairs and walkway have a **narrow iron handrail** — a fall is **40 ft to G1.**
- **The window:** **magically transparent stone** that is part of the **[[Gauntlight]] support — i.e. part of the artifact — so it can't be damaged** and it **can't be manipulated** like the other transparent stone in the dungeon (**no control glyphs**).
- **Why it's guarded:** [[Jafaki]] knows controlling this staircase is **the best way to keep [[Urevian]]'s troops out** and the **ideal place to capture new research subjects.**
- **Creatures:** **one seugathi** on guard, plus **one grothlut chained to the handrail** (it can move anywhere along the walkway in the **northern half** of the room).
  - The seugathi **watches for attacks from below**, so heroes coming **down from above may catch them off guard.** **Moaning and gibbering from the landing above is normal** and doesn't alert them.
  - **If warned:** the seugathi uses their **wand of *see invisibility*** to **suppress their mind fog aura and feign friendliness just long enough to put newcomers at ease**, then tries to **take the intruders prisoner** — **to the death if necessary.**
  - The grothlut **moans and charges** at any intruder but **loyally follows the seugathi's commands.**
- **Reinforcements:** Jafaki **doesn't replace** these guards. If the landing is **unguarded for more than 5 days**, **Urevian sends a cautious barbazu (from [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G3. Drill Field · Moderate 7|G3]] or [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G5. Hellforge · Moderate 7|G5]]) and two driders (from [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G4. Storage Cells · Low 7|G4]] or [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G15. Barracks · Severe 7|G15]])** to secure it (captions say "drans"; [[floor-07-soul-keepers|Floor 7]] confirms G4 and G15 hold driders).
- **Connections:** E1 (up) · G1 (down 40 ft) · F4 (window view).

### F3. Rocky Cave · Moderate 6
▶ [6:07](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=367)

- **Sensory:** **mossy walls** in a **pebble-strewn cavern**, **ceiling 30 ft**; a **wide rounded window 15 ft up** overlooks it (from F1). **Dripping water echoes from the east.** The **moss is edible — and surprisingly tasty.**
- **Creatures:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] bound several earth elementals here** — less for any grand purpose than **to keep wandering guests out of her nature preserve (F4).** (Captions name **sod hounds** and a **living landslide** ⚠F10.)
  - They **spring from the walls and floor 1d4 rounds** after anyone enters. **Ethereal shackles** are visible on their limbs — their binding. The shackles **stop them leaving the cave**, but they **can lurk inside walls and floors.**
  - An elemental **below 5 HP retreats into the stone** and **doesn't return for several days.**
- **Side quest — unbinding the elementals:** a hero who's an **expert in Arcana or Occultism** realizes the binding is **weak and can be undone.**
  - **Location:** the ritual must be performed **in sight of the binding location** — **the stairs up to F2** or **the window in F1** are good spots (**the elementals can't attack anyone there**).
  - **Cost/time:** **4 hours**, **60 gp of gems.**
  - **Primary caster:** must be an **expert** in the skill; **DC 25 Arcana or Occultism.**
  - **Secondary casters:** up to **4**, each **DC 23** (secondary check per the Core Rulebook) with **Arcana, Nature or Occultism**, **trained.** Fewer than four — or none — is fine.
  - **On success:** the elementals **return to their home plane.** The **living landslide grants a boon to one participating hero (likely the primary caster): the crystal sensibility of a sod hound for one week.**
- **Treasure:** the sod hounds **buried six gemstones worth 15 gp each** (payment for their service). **DC 27 Perception** finds them; the hero with the **crystal sensibility finds them automatically.**

### F4. Deep Pool · Moderate 6
▶ [8:52](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=532)

- **Layout:** a **rocky shoreline drops steeply into a large pool** under **stalactites.** **Five square shafts** in the ceiling lead **up** (→ the five pits of **[[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E12. Sentencing Chamber · Moderate 5|E12]]**). To the south, a **barrier of transparent stone** protects a **winding raised ledge along each side** of the cavern, and a **short bridge, likewise encased in transparent stone,** crosses the pool. Pool **averages 15 ft deep**; tiny underground streams refresh it.
- **Purpose:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s underground nature preserve.** She kept **a pair of hydras** because **hydra blood is useful in alchemy, magic and fleshwarping.**
- **Creatures — two hydras** (descendants of the original pair, kept by **[[Jafaki]]** for the same reasons). Jafaki **induced a chemical stupor** to **extend their lives, lower their food intake and reduce trouble.**
  - On arrival they're **asleep with only their heads above water.** If **at least half the party avoids notice**, they **stay asleep.**
  - **Any significant disturbance of the water** (e.g. someone falling in from above) **rouses them.** Because of the **alchemical hibernation, each takes 3d4 rounds to wake fully** and is **slowed 2** meanwhile. **Each time a hydra takes damage, reduce its remaining rounds by 3.**
  - They **defend their territory to the death** but **don't pursue** beyond the cave.
- **Connections:** ← **E12** (five shafts, 55-ft drop) · ← **[[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E13. Gladiator Readiness Room · Low 5|E13]]** (ladders) · F1 window view.

### F5. Deep End · Moderate 6
▶ [10:45](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=645)

- **Layout:** the **southern end is entirely flooded.** A **walkway encased in transparent stone** runs along the **east wall 10 ft above the water.** The **hydras' old nest** is at the far end where the pool **dips to 25 ft**, but **they're afraid of the recent arrival there** and avoid it. An **overhang above the deepest part** is pierced by a **well capped by a sturdy iron grate**; the **bolts appear broken from underneath**, so the grate **lifts off easily — DC 20 Athletics.** Anyone **in the water can climb up into F9** this way.
- **Secret door:** a hero searching the walkway finds a **suspicious masonry square near floor level** — **DC 20 Perception.** Pushed like a button, it **opens a secret door to F8.**
- **Creature — a "slag gagon"** ⚠F3, a **devil [[Urevian]] sent to spy on [[Jafaki]]** and their plots **several weeks ago.**
  - **Paranoid** and unwilling to risk exposure, it spends most of its time **fouling the water under the well** if anyone comes to fill the bucket.
  - If the heroes approach, it assumes **Jafaki's allies** have found it and **attacks desperately** — unwilling to admit defeat to Urevian. **Fights until slain.**
  - It carries **one of five pieces of Urevian's pendant**, used to **open the door to the contract devil's domain in [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G10. Urevian's Domain · Severe 7|G10]].**
- **Treasure:** the **abandoned hydra nest** at the deepest point holds **equipment and bones of long-dead gladiators.** The **only intact piece is a +1 resilient scale mail with lion motifs.**

▶ [14:11](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=851)

  - **West plinth:** **four keyholes clustered in a metal plate.** Turning the proper key **opens/closes the southern of the two doors**, also **with a loud grinding noise.**
  - **No keys?** A **Master in Thievery** can defeat one of these locks with **five successes at DC 30.**
- **Where the keys are (three copies of each on this level):**
  - **Round key:** shelf in **[[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E5. Medical Supplies · no encounter|E5]]** · **F10** · [[Jafaki]] carries one.
  - **Four-prong key:** floor of **F6** · [[Vischari]]'s safe in **F16** · Jafaki carries one.

▶ [16:14](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=974)

- **Layout:** a gallery **15 ft below the ceiling** runs along **all but the east side** of a **large bloodstained room (F9)** whose **floor is 5 ft lower.** A **window of cloudy transparent stone** gives an excellent view **but blocks access.** **Two rounded observation areas** overlook the room from the north. A **small room to the south** has a **small table, an old chair, and stairs up (→ [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E21. Staging Area · no encounter|E21]]).**
- **History:** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] used the walkway to **watch her champions closely** as she pitted them against opponents, **settling disagreements through ritual combat.**
- **Sound:** a **magic enchantment on the two observation vestibules transmits sound from the testing grounds into the gallery — not the reverse.**
- **The table:** **chalk and writing slates** where the seugathi **take notes** on creatures in the testing grounds.
- **The NW door:** **no visible latches**, but the adjacent wall has **two keyholes** — a **round indentation** and a **circular stone with four holes** — that **work identically to the F7 keyholes.**
- **The glyphs:** the **east wall bears faintly glowing glyphs** that **simultaneously control *all* the transparent stone ringing the gallery** (make it ethereal, opaque, or transparent; as for [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E9. Elite Viewing Room · Moderate 5|E9]]).
- **Secret doors:** two — **north → F5** and **southeast → F20.** **DC 20 Perception** finds them; both open via a **masonry square near floor level that presses like a button.**
- **Creature — a seugathi researcher** observes the **destrachan** in F9 and takes notes on a slate. On spotting intruders they become **eager to see how the destrachan performs**, retreat to the **east end of the gallery**, and **turn the wall ethereal.** Once the wall isn't solid, the **destrachan's echolocation** finds the heroes and it **joins the fight as the seugathi directs** — **together a Severe 6 encounter.**
- **Connections:** E21 (stairs up) · F5 (secret door N) · F20 (secret door SE) · F7 passage (NW door).

It **fights to the death** and **pursues fleeing foes.** A seugathi in the gallery (if present) **takes notes throughout.**

### F11. Warped Brew Tavern · Moderate 6
▶ [23:27](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=1407)

> ⚠F2 — **the tavern is F11** (it was already "F11" in the [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds|Floor 5]] captions). The repo's Level 6 summary says "Warped Brew Tavern (F16)"; **F16 is [[Vischari]]'s office**, not the tavern.

- **Sensory:** **polished wooden tables, chairs and a long bar.** The smell of **spilled beer and fried morsels** fills the room. An **elevated stage in the southeast corner** overlooks the seating. **Doors on all four walls**, including a **double door west (the main entrance)**; **two doors behind the bar.**
- **History:** once **an elaborate dining hall for [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s gladiators and guests**; over the years it became **a tavern for fleshwarped creatures, those waiting to be fleshwarped, and a few rare visitors.** It has had its current name **for more than a century.** Because of **[[Jafaki]]'s general lack of discipline**, many of the level's denizens come to **drink, gamble, argue about who's going under the seugathi's knives, and listen to Shadow Malice — the resident band.**
- **The SW door → a steep stairway up to [[floor-05-into-the-training-grounds#E9. Elite Viewing Room · Moderate 5|E9]].** When the **poltergeists there started flinging debris down it, the patrons stopped using it** except as a **garbage disposal.** **Greater difficult terrain.**
- **Creatures (five usually present):**
  - **Lallizanx** — female **drider**, **ostensibly the bouncer**, flipping through a book of **crudely drawn drow erotica.** Cares little what happens. **Casts *suggestion*** on any hero who persistently bothers her, insisting they leave her alone.
  - **Two [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]]** playing a card game called **Towers.**
  - **A morlock bartender** — looks forward to **becoming like the powerful driders** who drop in.
  - **Another morlock** complaining to the bartender about **how long she's waiting for her fleshwarping.**
  - **Shadow Malice on stage: 1-in-4 chance** — or simply **decide** whether the band is playing.
  - **Vischari** (urdefhan manager) — arrives from **F16 in 3 rounds** if a fight breaks out.
- **Attitudes:** everyone **assumes surface intruders came to start a fight.** If the heroes **quickly show peaceful intent**, patrons **keep a close eye but resume business.** All patrons start **Unfriendly.** The bartender has **only a few drinks, sold at inflated prices**, and makes only a **half-hearted attempt to stop the heroes leaving** through either door behind the bar. **The morlock patrons idolize Shadow Malice and bring the band up in conversation.**
- **If a fight breaks out:** **Vischari and Lallizanx fight to the death** ("home turf"). **Morlocks below 10 HP flee.** If **Shadow Malice is present, they retreat to F18** rather than risk injury.

#### Side quest — Battle of the Bands
▶ [27:31](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=1651)

**Trigger:** if the heroes seem **curious about Shadow Malice** (band present or not), the **morlocks start chanting the band's name and issuing challenges in Undercommon.** It sounds like a setup for a fight, but **they're calling for a battle of entertainment, not weapons.** The cries bring the band on stage if they aren't there, and **Ryta** (vocalist) explains:

> *"You want to challenge us? Well, let's get to it. We'll each face off one on one — each pair squares off with some kind of show: music, acrobatic tricks, displays of strength, whatever. Then we perform as two groups — mine, then yours. Let the crowd tell us who's the best overall."*

| Phase | Mechanics |
|---|---|
| **1. Individual** | Each hero is **paired with one of the four band members** (if the party has more than four, some **sit out**). The band member gives **their best solo performance** on an instrument of their choice; the paired hero then attempts a **DC 22** check with a **relevant skill** (Performance, Acrobatics, Athletics, or anything you deem fit) **to impress the crowd.** That DC is **the band member's Performance DC — 22 for all four.** **Record each hero's degree of success.** |
| **2. Group** | Shadow Malice performs a **high-energy number**, then **passes over their instruments** for the heroes to use (if they have none). This phase **requires a Performance check**. Ryta grins: *"This stage is for performers. Friends — to compete."* **One hero = lead performer: DC 22 Performance.** **Any number of others can Aid.** The lead gets **+2 for each hero who *critically succeeded* in phase 1, +1 for each who *succeeded*, −1 for each who *critically failed*** — **these bonuses/penalties stack** (unlike the normal rules). |

| Group-phase result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Critical success** | As success, **plus Ryta gives the heroes the band's instruments** — "they belong in the hands of such talented performers." The others **grumble but solemnly comply.** |
| **Success** | Ryta **acknowledges the heroes as the band's superiors on stage** and **resolves to leave them alone.** **Word reaches Vischari, who treats them with "a modicum of respect."** |
| **Failure** | Shadow Malice sees the heroes as **out of their depths** and **attacks right on stage.** Everyone else **just watches — "a bloody part of the show."** |
| **Critical failure** | As failure, **but the patrons join the fight after round 1** — retreat fast. |

- **XP:** **30** (the usual side-quest award) for taking part; **+80** if the heroes **win.**
- **Treasure:** the **gambling morlocks** hold **15 gp, 181 sp, a gold tooth (5 gp)** and a **perfectly fossilized tuna skeleton.** On the **table behind the bar:** **cash box with 28 gp and 74 sp**, and a **vial of "slothing" (soothing?) toxin** ⚠F10 the bartender uses to **poison troublesome patrons.**

#### Jafaki's Background
▶ [52:43](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=3163)

- **Origins:** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **first spotted [[Jafaki]] and several other seugathi slithering through the Darklands** while building the Vaults. They **expressed eagerness to serve her and advance their own experiments.** She saw they could **improve her forces**, and **tasked them with building a perfect soldier, then armies of them to conquer [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].** Knowing **perfection was a high price**, she **funneled all the resources she could** to them.
- **Breadth:** the seugathi pursued **many** paths — **fleshwarping, monster breeding, even magical constructs.** Belcorra didn't mind: **nearly all of it produced useful monsters**, and she **passed the innovations to other alchemists and mages** who could duplicate the work.
- **The coup:** after the [[Roseguard]] killed Belcorra, the seugathi kept going. Their first obstacle was the **bone devil Tarkannah**, who **stayed loyal to Belcorra even after her death** and so **took a firmer hand in overseeing the seugathi**, trying to **constrain and direct their sprawling research.** **Rather than comply, the seugathi rebelled: Jafaki ambushed and killed Tarkannah and seized the laboratory level.**
- **The war:** that started **a brief war between devils and seugathi** — **months of fighting** — until **[[Urevian]] discovered the seugathi weren't interested in expanding, only in defending** what they had. **An uneasy truce**, each on their own level.
- **The border isn't absolute:** **Urevian happily lets potential raw materials pass through the prison**, and the **seugathi's castoff creatures often end up in the devils' growing forces.** Jafaki **has little concern for the armies growing on the level below**; the seugathi **ignore the imp and zebub spies** that occasionally pass through — but **Jafaki and colleagues can make marching a large army through costly or even impossible.** **Jafaki plans to permit free passage only in exchange for concessions** to fund their experiments — even though **Urevian is sure to balk.**
- **The obsession:** Jafaki's research has **drifted through various horrid sciences** — **fleshwarping, reanimation, selective breeding, golem creation** — yet they're **as focused on the perfect soldier as when they spoke to Belcorra centuries ago.** *"Good soldiers they've created — and all have died by the dozens or hundreds in the arena. Great soldiers — some of Jafaki's finest works have been nearly unstoppable. But **nearly** is not perfect."* **Jafaki will pursue perfection as long as it takes.**

#### Encountering Jafaki · Severe 6
▶ [56:06](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=3366)

- **Where:** Jafaki does their best work **in this chamber** ⚠F7, though they may be **elsewhere on the level** evaluating successes; they even **make the best of failures** (e.g. **cultivating the shanrigol behemoth in F26**). Jafaki **won't venture out in response to the heroes** — they see them as **annoyances** and **trust others to handle it.** **Once the heroes show up here, Jafaki realizes they may have underestimated them.**
- **Creatures — Jafaki and a drider:**
  - **Jafaki sends the drider to answer any knock**, since other seugathi sometimes come for guidance. **Heroes can exploit that habit** to **surprise the preoccupied researcher.**
  - If they **force the door or pick the lock**, Jafaki **knows they're intruders** and prepares. The **drider scrambles up the ceiling.** **Jafaki envenoms their rapier and casts *levitate*** to stay out of melee reach.
  - **Both use spells as often as possible.** The drider uses ***fireball* and *acid arrow*** for direct damage. **Jafaki prefers spells that disorient and turn the heroes on each other — *confusion* —** hoping to **work out which hero is toughest and take that hero captive for experimentation.** When damage is better, Jafaki **hurls chemical bombs** to hit as many targets as possible.
  - **Both fight to the death** — the drider **out of loyalty**, Jafaki because they **can't abandon their life's work.**

#### Side Quest: The Flesh Golem Project
▶ [58:03](https://youtu.be/eccWAPxRKlQ?t=3483)

- **The project:** Jafaki is **building a flesh golem with occult machinery**, using **the morlock's head** and the **neatly labeled body parts scattered about the room.** A **sketch pad** holds **assembly instructions** and notes that the **ambient necromantic energy in the Vaults will animate the flesh golem.** The **key is the occult machinery keeping the head alive — it focuses the ambient energy.** **Finishing requires Jafaki's knowledge — beyond the heroes' current capabilities.**
- **What the heroes can do:** **disconnect the machine to end the morlock's torment.** **DC 22 Occultism.**
  - **Success:** the **morlock head dies and the machinery can never be used again.**
  - **Failure — or simply smashing it:** the morlock head still dies, **but the accumulated invisible energies seek another source of stitched skin and meat to animate as a flesh golem.** **If the heroes left Belcorra's fleshy statue intact in the Temple of the Canker ([[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C34. Temple of the Canker · Moderate 3|C34]]), the energies animate *that* as the golem.**
  - **The golem** wakes with an **instinctive drive to find and kill whoever ruined the machine.** It **always knows the distance and direction** to the heroes responsible and **immediately begins pursuing them through the Vaults.** Being far away, it **might take hours to catch up (your discretion).** It **fights until destroyed.**
- **XP:** **80** (instead of 30) for **successfully disconnecting** the machinery; **if they fail, award the same 80 if they defeat the flesh golem in combat.**
