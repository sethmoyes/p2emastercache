## Chapter 8 Background (GM-only)
▶ [2:49](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=169)

**Why the [[Gauntlight]] works.** The Gauntlight is **active, but its true power is yet to be realized.** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **dug deep to build its foundation, following the psychic pull of the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]]** — a place sacred to **[[Nhimbaloth]]**, the heart of an **ancient subterranean temple.** The Gauntlight **draws on the Empty Vault's energies** as foundation and power source. Inside the Vault Belcorra **empowered four [[fulcrum lenses]]**; **installed in the lighthouse they focus Nhimbaloth's attention** and unleash the artifact's full power. But **the lenses are dangerous — they invite Nhimbaloth's uncaring gaze**, and **even Belcorra can't withstand the Outer God's scrutiny.** So although they hold great power, **the lenses are also Belcorra's greatest weakness.**

**The two levels above the Vault.** Belcorra repurposed the **massive natural caverns** above the Empty Vault: **Level 8 = a lush cavern of fungus growths and freshwater lakes teeming with subterranean plants and fish; Level 9 = a wilderness where predators and prey alike thrive.** The heroes must descend through both to reach the Vault.

### Children of Belcorra
▶ [4:10](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=250)

- **Origin:** uninterested in running the caverns herself, [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **enthralled deep gnomes already living there**, compelling them **with threats and favors** to tend her **subterranean farm.** She called these loyal, diminutive guard-partners **the [[Children of Belcorra]].**
- **After her death:** they **dismissed the rumors as a trick or test** and kept working. Over the years **more and more feral monsters got in**, making the farm harder to run. **Believing Belcorra or another harbinger would return**, they **chose to become undead** to fight their dwindling numbers and age: they **immured each other in muddy shores to rise as bog mummies**, or underwent **harder transformations into more powerful undead.**
- **Decay:** timeless lives and lack of direction took their toll — **many fell into torpor, obsession or isolation.** The farm they'd tended **filled with monsters and subterranean explorers — caligni, drow, urdefhan.** Even Belcorra's return as the Ghost Queen **couldn't energize all of them**, but many **realized how badly they'd failed their charge.**
- **New mandate:** Belcorra told them to **round up the level's dangerous creatures to serve her in the surface assault**, under a new supervisor — **a naga named Vthis** ⚠F1. **She chose poorly:** the naga is **corrupt, lazy and more interested in creature comforts than guiding the deep-gnome undead.** The Children resent her but **won't contradict their ghost queen.** Their pace is **plodding at best** and has done **little to reduce the infestation.**

### Belcorra's Wisps
▶ [6:33](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=393)

Although she trusts the Children and Vthis to sort out the farm, [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **still keeps tabs.** **A few will-o'-wisps and dread wisps can communicate with the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]].** They **hover over basins of brackish swamp water** (with reeds that thrive in the dark), drawing nourishment from **their connection to the swamp fed by the Empty Vault.** The wisps **quickly inform Belcorra of unexpected intruders — like the heroes — leading her to make an appearance.**

### Event: Belcorra Attacks!
▶ [7:09](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=429)

> *"Ghost stories work best when the ghost is an active and terrifying adversary."*

- **Trigger:** whenever **any wisp in Chapter 8 or 9 sees the heroes**, it **immediately alerts [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] through the swampy basins** throughout the lower Vaults. She's **worried that the heroes have come this far** and resolves to **harry them until other denizens can overcome them.** The event **may happen several times** in the last chapters.
- **XP:** **30 XP each time the heroes survive one of her attacks; 80 XP if they defeat her and force her to rejuvenate.**

#### Using Belcorra Effectively
▶ [7:59](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=479)

- The heroes probably face her **several times before they get the [[fulcrum lenses]]** needed to destroy her for good. She learns about them **from her wisps.** Use her **whenever her appearance would cause the most tension** — **at least once per dungeon level, as often as every couple of days.** **Don't have her fight beside other creatures until the AP's finale** — she's tough enough alone.
- **She adapts** each time: she may cast ***spell immunity*** (vs. the heroes' best spells), ***true seeing*** (to keep them in sight) or ***dispel magic*** (vs. their defenses). She **mocks their failures and rages at their successes.** Remember: **she has known about them since the [[Gauntlight]] test-fire in Chapter 2.**

| Attack | What she does |
|---|---|
| **First** | As soon as she notices them she **travels through the Gauntlight's footprint (areas H37 and [[floor-09-on-the-hunt#I28. Central Pit · no encounter\|I28]])**, casts ***phantasmal calamity*** **from a distance**, and **retreats through walls if needed** so they can't follow. |
| **Second** | She wants them to **fear her**, so she comes **closer**, using **single-target spells** like ***feeblemind*** and ***phantasmal killer*** while **demanding they flee "her" Abomination Vaults.** Message delivered, she **retreats after a few rounds.** |
| **Later** (after they recover **a fulcrum lens**) | She now sees them as **an actual threat**, but **anger overrides caution:** she attacks **with full might — corrupting gaze, ghostly hands and spells.** If she **defeats a hero** she **retreats, confident they'll now leave her alone.** |
| **Through agents** | She has **living allies — the urdefhan cult and the [[Children of Belcorra]]** — and may **send them after the heroes**, making sure the heroes learn **they act on her orders.** **Neither group can muster large numbers at once**, so the heroes can handle them. |
| **Against their allies** | She **learns of the heroes' alliances** (e.g. **caligni, drow**). She **won't face entrenched leaders like [[Dulac]] or [[Quara Orshendiel\|Quara]]** but might **murder a few guards or aim monsters where they hurt the heroes' allies most** — always **leaving evidence, usually one injured survivor**, so the heroes know it was her. |
| **In the footprint** | She's **most effective against heroes who entered the Gauntlight's footprint (H37, I28)** because of her ***Haunted Lighthouse*** ability. |
| **The final attack** | If the heroes **beat her shortly before reaching the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare\|Empty Vault]]**, she **uses the Ebon fulcrum lens to hasten her rejuvenation** and meets them in her sanctum. **She never surrenders or flees in the Vault.** They may fight her there **before they can permanently defeat her** — then they must **retreat, gather the lenses, and return.** **Once they can focus [[Nhimbaloth]]'s gaze on her, they can destroy her for good.** |

## The Cult of Urthagul
▶ [12:05](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=725)

- **History:** a group of **caligni refugees** reached the [[floor-09-on-the-hunt|Hunting Grounds]] (Level 9) **years ago, fleeing "some deeper calamity."** Their two leaders — **a spellcaster, [[Galudu]]**, **and a warrior, [[Dulac]]** — led them into **an old lodge** on Level 9. They **explored upward**, reached **an abandoned shrine of [[Nhimbaloth]]**, and **found a gug mystic, [[Urthagul]]**, living in **a cave that resonates with strange psychic whispers.** **Some caligni — led by Dulac — revered the gug and his eerie visions**; **others were skeptical.** **Schism:** the **self-named Cult of Urthagul lives in the shrine (H2–H10 area)**; **Galudu's caligni live in the lodge below.** They **aren't enemies** but **rarely interact.**
- **Now:** the cult is **increasingly insular and zealous.** Urthagul **hardly notices them.** They **hunt chuul eggs from the lake, ritually cook and eat them.** **Dulac and her second, Padli, gained occult powers from meditating on the whispers** and **use them to keep the cult in line.**
- **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s move:** when she awoke she visited the shrine, **was surprised to find the caligni**, and **visited Urthagul**: the gug's odd behavior **wasn't only the cave's psychic emanations** — **he'd found the [[Fulcrum Lenses|Crimson Fulcrum Lens]] and was communing with a splinter of Nhimbaloth's essence inside it.** She judged the lens **probably safe in the gug's claws**, but **didn't want it in the hands of "some interloper cult."** She **tasked her wisps with keeping the caligni contained in their shrine.** The cult is **frustrated at being kept from the object of their veneration.** **Dulac is desperate for a solution, so the heroes may find the caligni eager to talk.**

#### Distracting Whispers
▶ [31:13](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=1873)

**Creatures in the cave are immune to emotion effects, but the whispers still work on the mind.** **Each minute**, a creature must attempt a **DC 24 Will save.** **Failure:** **stupefied 1** (**stupefied 2 on a critical failure**) **for 24 hours**; the condition **is cumulative up to stupefied 4.** **Each time a creature's stupefied value increases it hears a different "truth":**

| Stupefied | The whispers say… |
|---|---|
| **1** | *"The lenses. She knows the secret of the lenses. Three are within reach — one very close, one lost [to] [[Yldaris]], one held by the blood [___]."* ⚠F12 |
| **2** | *"She keeps them apart while she works her petty vengeance — but the essences shall become one."* |
| **3** | *"Find the lenses and place their essences together within the host."* |
| **4** | *"Join the essences within Haruvex, and she can fall again. This is a promise of the Empty Death."* |

**Aberrations and sorcerers with the aberrant bloodline** react differently: **no stupefied — they're fascinated while in the cavern** (it ends when they leave). **If they linger, they involuntarily babble strange truths that echo in the whispers — which is how Urthagul got his reputation as a mystic among the caligni.**

- **Creature — Urthagul** (gug): on the **northernmost island, amid a nest of glowing toadstools**, **hunched, purring, stroking the [[Fulcrum Lenses|Crimson Fulcrum Lens]] in one of his four hands.** He's been here **decades, perhaps longer.** **Formidable, but the whispers and the lens have taken a toll:** he **rarely eats — even less now that the cultists don't visit** — and **his glowing treasure commands nearly all his attention.**
  - **When the heroes enter,** he **glances their way and no more.** They can **explore or pass through without waking him to action.** **If spoken to,** he **doesn't answer except to shift the lens to the hand farthest from anyone who might take it.**
  - **If they use force or try to take the lens,** he **attacks with relentless brutality.** He **prefers his jaws, supplemented by the lens's powers.** **His *Furious Claws* gives him only three claw attacks instead of four — he won't let go of the lens.** **Fights to the death** but **doesn't leave the cavern.**
- **XP: 30** for recovering the Crimson Fulcrum Lens.

### H14. Rickety Dock · Low 8

- **Creature — Iribo**, a **drow shootist** and **lookout.** She **investigates any sign of intruders (e.g. her spider's whistle).** **In a fight she tries to incapacitate foes quickly with poison and calls the drow in H45** — **but doesn't recklessly fight if negotiation looks possible.** **A longtime friend of Yldaris's leader [[Quara Orshendiel|Quara]]**, she **knows Quara might use surface dwellers.**

### H45. Planning Room · Moderate 8
▶ [1:17:06](https://youtu.be/1L_igJ-hrak?t=4626)

- **Sensory:** **once richly appointed, now full of mold and decay:** **shattered furniture covers the floor** and **tattered, mildewed spectral(?) tapestries hang on the walls** ⚠F13. **In the north, curtains cover a wide bay window of what looks like transparent stone.**
- **Purpose:** the **elder Children planned their lush gardens here**; it's now **a staging area for the small drow contingent.** **The curved floor-to-ceiling bay window gives a clear view of the garden.** **It's enchanted stone: transparent from inside, ordinary natural stone from outside.** **The drow might have gotten a good look at the heroes passing through H34 — but they keep the curtains closed most of the time.**
- **Creatures — Nyzuros** (a **drow hunter**) **and four drow wardens** huddle over **a map of the gardens.** **Their chief worry is a goliath spider at the crossroads (H35), which has already eaten two drow scouts.** They're **debating whether to fight it (glory) or summon aid from [[Yldaris]].** **Nyzuros commands the contingent** (though **Iribo is their most respected fighter**).
  - **Whether the heroes arrive quietly or are drawn in by noise,** **the wardens are hot-headed and quick to attack; Nyzuros is more circumspect** and **wants to know how and why the intruders came.**
  - **Attitude: Indifferent**, **improvable like the H41 hunters; Friendly or better and he escorts them to Yldaris personally.**
  - **A shortcut:** **if the heroes killed the goliath spider (H35) and promise to let Nyzuros and his team take credit, his attitude automatically becomes Friendly.** **His forbearance has limits — if the heroes have killed other drow, he turns against them.**
- **XP: 60** for **talking to Nyzuros rather than fighting; +80** for **successfully negotiating his escort — unless they already arranged one (H41 or Iribo).**
