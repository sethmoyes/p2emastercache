## About the Adventure Path
▶ [0:04](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=4)

- ***Abomination Vaults* is Paizo's 28th Adventure Path** (Pathfinder 2e). **Released originally as a three-book series in 2021 — *Ruins of [[Gauntlight]]*, *Hands of the Devil*, *Eyes of [[Nhimbaloth|Empty Death]]* — and re-released in 2022 as a single book, *Abomination Vaults*.** **It has quickly become one of the most popular beginner adventures for PF2e.**
- **Related material** (not required): ***Troubles in Otari*** and the Beginner Box adventure ***Menace Under Otari*.**
- **Content warning:** **typical Pathfinder action and adventure, plus themes of suicide, ableism, body horror and human experimentation.** **Player consent — including the GM's — is vital; talk to your players first and modify descriptions/scenarios as appropriate.**
- *(A symbol in the video marks content the Pocket GM added that isn't in the book; wording may differ slightly from the printed text.)*

## Campaign Overview
▶ [5:16](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=316)

The ghost sorcerer **[[Belcorra Haruvex]]** is the primary villain; the story behind her spite is **one of exile and loss.**

### Belcorra's History
▶ [5:31](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=331)

- **The family:** **the Haruvex family tree "both sprawls and surges with sorcery."** **The strongest bloodlines carry an *aberrant strain* that brings prestige and power — generations married within the family to strengthen it.** **The most powerful Haruvexes lived in [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] 500 years ago and made *secret obeisances to the Outer Gods* for wealth and power.** **None approached the power of the youngest scion, [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].**
- **The child:** **she was told every day, for as long as she could remember, that she was *the harbinger of [[Nhimbaloth]], the Empty Death* — her family's greatest hope for power and prosperity.**
- **The exile:** **the Haruvexes performed abhorrent rites, including bloody sacrifices; the authorities caught on and drove them out** (**out of the Isle of Kortos, i.e. Absalom's home isle, in 4219 AR**), **forced to leave their estates and luxuries behind.** **Belcorra was six.** **As she grew, her rage over losing her home hardened into an overwhelming hatred of Absalom and everyone in it.** **Most of the exiles died in poverty; Belcorra grew strong and proud.**
- **The return:** **at 17 she interpreted visions from Nhimbaloth that led her back to the Isle of Kortos, above a subterranean shrine to Nhimbaloth called the *[[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]]*, on a lonely wooded stretch of coast a few dozen miles from Absalom.** **She erected a magical lighthouse — the *[[Gauntlight]]* — and dug deep beneath it to reach the shrine.** **Her plan: draw subterranean monsters and violent beasts into a secret dungeon, the *Abomination Vaults*, and use the Gauntlight's magic to *send them into Absalom* to wreak havoc.**
- **The build-up:** **she expanded the Vaults and raised a defensible keep around the lighthouse; leveraged family acquaintances to acquire powerful retainers and champions to lead her monstrous forces.** **While she stalked her Vaults with aberrations, rumors reached Absalom, and the *[[Roseguard]]* — heroes headquartered there — set out to end her.** **They faced Belcorra in Gauntlight Keep and killed her there.** **One of the four, the rogue *[[Otari Ilvashti|Otari Ilvashi]]*, died in the fight.** **Grieving, the Roseguard sealed up the keep, founded what became the town of Otari, and retired — *never knowing of the keep's lower levels or the creatures still inside.***

### A Dungeon Full of Monsters
▶ [8:45](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=525)

- **The horrors trapped in the Vaults developed "some measure of an ecosystem":** **while many monsters beneath the citadel died, others prospered; now several communities — *[[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]], urdefhan, ghouls* and stranger creatures — call it home.** **Only the longest-lived remember any life other than the dungeon.**
- **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] bound several *devils* as overseers and administrators; many still keep order.** **Apart from the regions those devils control and *a drow outpost in the lowest levels*, the Vaults are lawless and dangerous.**
- **The ten levels, top to bottom:** **(1) the ruins of [[Gauntlight|Gauntlight Keep]] · (2) the servants' quarters · (3) the library · (4) Belcorra's former living chambers · (5) the arena · (6) the laboratories · (7) the prison · (8) the farm caverns · (9) the hunting grounds · (10) the temple level, which holds the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]].** **Each has its own character; they get progressively more dangerous.**

### Belcorra's Rise
▶ [10:30](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=630)

- **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s spirit didn't rest.** **Consumed by rage and empowered by [[Nhimbaloth]], she arose as a powerful ghost *500 years after her family's exile* from [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] — the event that so dramatically changed her life.** **She now lurks in the lowest depths, plotting revenge against Absalom and the heroes who vanquished her (the [[Roseguard]]).**
- **The Roseguard are long dead, but the town they founded remains — and *the icons of their adventuring careers now have the power to penetrate Belcorra's protections.*** **At first no one in Otari sees the danger — her machinations and monsters are out of sight.** **But before she moves on Absalom she means to use the [[Gauntlight]] against *Otari*, the town built by the adventurers who killed her five centuries ago.** **Only Otari's newest heroes can stop her vengeance and put her spirit to rest.**

### Gamemaster Advice
▶ [15:57](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=957)

- **Levels are built for a party of a particular level — but each has *more than enough encounters to level up*.** **Part of a big dungeon crawl's joy is exploring every nook; a party that faces every encounter may reach a higher level well before the end — *"that's fine."*** **Later encounters still challenge a high-level party even if the heroes retreat, rest and recover less often.**
- **Freedom to explore (especially the middle levels):** **let the players judge whether they're in over their heads and retreat.** **Harder-than-expected challenges give more XP, easier ones less — keeping progression on roughly the same track.** **For a slower game, the AP works well with *slow advancement* (Core Rulebook p. 509).**
- **Story-based progression is tricky in a dungeon crawl** (**heroes can move between levels by several routes**). **Rather than leveling because they reached a lower level quickly, consider requiring specific *milestones* (below).**
- **The final three levels are where the story comes to a head:** **it's critical that the heroes find the *three [[fulcrum lenses]]* and learn how they work — specifically how they can permanently destroy [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].** **Belcorra has more freedom to harry the heroes in the lower levels, and by the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]] they've earned her ire.** **With luck they defeat Belcorra and topple the [[Gauntlight]].**

## The Town of Otari
▶ [20:39](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1239)

*Home base for the whole campaign. Every floor doc's "Otari-side" hooks point back here. Facts in bold are from the video; the **Player's Guide** (repo copy: [`../etc/players_guide.md`](../etc/players_guide.md)) is used only to fix names and is called out where it adds something.*

### Otari's History
▶ [20:42](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1242)

- **Starting point:** **[[locations/absalom|Absalom]] has long dominated the urban scene on the Starstone Isle** and civilization has thrived across the island, **but Otari's beginning is recent and had a few false starts.** **When [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] arrived in 4230 AR — drawn by sinister visions — the coast was practically uninhabited;** she built the [[Gauntlight]], the keep around it and the dungeon below **without attracting notice.**
- **The [[Roseguard]]:** **they learned of her villainy and slew her, at the cost of one of their own — the rogue [[Otari Ilvashti|Otari Ilvashi]]** ⚠F1. **Having lost both their friend and their taste for adventuring, the three survivors retired.** **They spent much of their savings on several buildings and a sizable pier in a sheltered cove south of their final battle** — **their lost friend had been fond of settling in a small seaside village, so they named the new village after him.**
- **The first flowering:** **for some decades Otari prospered as a fishing village whose population never grew beyond a hundred or so.** **The three survivors lived to a ripe old age and died of natural causes — "an accomplishment many adventurers never achieve."**
- **The decline:** **when the last Roseguard, [[Aesephna Menhemes]], died in 4290 AR, Otari's fortune dwindled — within a few years its citizens had all drifted elsewhere.** **It lay abandoned for decades** until **the Stone Scale kobolds moved in.** **When the kobolds began luring passing ships in during bad weather to ambush crews and steal cargo, new adventurers arrived quickly and vanquished them.** **The adventurers moved on; their backer, Maklani Menhemes — Aesephna's granddaughter — stayed.**
- **The revival:** **Maklani planned to revitalize Otari as a lumber town:** **it already had dozens of buildings that only needed restoring, and the Osprey River was ideal for a mill and lumber yard.** **One setback: the steep slopes and cliffs around Otari made getting timber to the ocean difficult.** **Her solution:** **a wooden flume carrying lumber from the woods to her mill, and from the mill down a loading ramp running from the top of a 200-foot-tall cliff to the harbor below.**
- **The price of success:** **the mill and flume made Maklani wealthy and revitalized Otari — and made her an enemy of the Kortos Consortium, which had a stranglehold on the island's lumber industry.** **Two other small lumber companies broke from the Consortium and settled in Otari, each paying to use Maklani's invention.**
- **Today:** **Otari is a thriving settlement, the largest on the coast between Absalom and Diobel** ⚠F9. **Maklani is long dead; her descendant [[Oseph Menhemes|Oseph]] carries on the family trade and serves as mayor.** **Unsavory Kortos Consortium representatives have secretly infiltrated the town and want to undermine its security** — **but a far greater threat looms: Belcorra has returned and is preparing to light the baleful fires of the Gauntlight once again.**

### Otari at a Glance
▶ [24:33](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1473)

- **Layout:** **a coastal valley between two looming cliffs.** **West:** **the cliffside rises to a well-cared-for graveyard** (Area 20). **East:** **a feat of engineering — the trestle carrying the wooden loading chute from the cliff top down to a loading platform built into the harbor, delivering mill-processed lumber swiftly to a waiting ship below.**
- **People:** **friendly and welcoming for the most part, but reticent toward anyone they suspect of coming from [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].** **Trade with Absalom matters, but the townsfolk are proud and feel no need to accommodate visiting city folk.**
- **Food & drink:** **fish, plus local-farm vegetables — mostly potatoes, carrots and other roots; more unusual fare is easy to get from Absalom.** **Coffee is a mainstay, the favorite drink of the hard-working, early-rising residents.**
- ***From the Player's Guide (not in the video):*** *Settlement 4, N town, population **1,240** (60% human, 8% halfling, 7% half-elf, 6% elf, 5% dwarf, 5% gnome, 3% half-orc, 2% goblin, 4% other); Common, Dwarven, Elven, Halfling; religions Cayden Cailean, [[Erastil]], Gozreh, [[Sarenrae]]; threats: aberrant horrors, eerie hauntings, kobolds, smugglers; **consumable items up to level 10 are for sale in its shops.***

### Exploring Otari
▶ [25:40](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1540)

- **Unlabeled buildings** are the wooden homes of people who work for nearby businesses or for **one of the town's three lumber concerns** (Menhemes, Chertel, Inkleholtz — Areas 3, 15, 16).
- **Most significant locations end with a stat block:** **name, function, level** (**sets values such as the highest-level task available to Earn Income there**), **address, wares & services (including spellcasting), and the most significant NPC** — **their initial attitude toward the heroes and the DCs most likely to matter** (**Perception DC and Will DC**, used for Make an Impression and similar).

### Location Support
▶ [26:37](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1597)

- **The mechanism:** **shift the NPC's attitude to Helpful** — **which may require completing a listed task, but usually just a successful Diplomacy check to Make an Impression.** **That location then supports the party in certain exploration and downtime activities;** the benefits come from **the location's traits** (table below). **Support generally lasts the whole campaign, though the GM may decide certain actions cost a location's support.** **Heroes can have several locations' support at once, as long as they make enough friends.**

| Trait | What the party gets |
|---|---|
| **Church** | **Faith-aligned characters** (**GM's call whether a hero's faith aligns with the church's NPCs**) **may use the site to retrain faster:** **what normally takes a week takes 5 days; what normally takes a month takes 3 weeks; retraining is generally free.** **+1 circumstance bonus to Identify Magic, Treat Wounds or Treat Disease on site.** |
| **Discount** | **10% discount on wares.** |
| **Employer** | **Tasks up to the location's level to Earn Income with a specific skill.** |
| **Garrison** | **Martially inclined characters retrain faster (as Church).** **+1 circumstance bonus to Gather Information or Treat Wounds on site.** |
| **Housing** | **Accommodations at a Comfortable cost of living, for no charge.** |
| **Library** | **Scholarly characters and arcane/occult spellcasters retrain faster (as Church).** **+1 circumstance bonus to Create Forgery, Decipher Writing or Learn a Spell on site.** |
| **Lodge** | **Primal spellcasters retrain faster (as Church).** **+1 circumstance bonus to Identify Magic or Subsist on site.** |
| **Pub** *(caption: "Paula")* | **Socially inclined characters retrain faster (as Church).** **+1 circumstance bonus to Gather Information, or to Earn Income with Performance.** |
| **Workshop** | **Use the location's resources to Craft items tied to its purpose:** **+1 item bonus to associated Crafting checks.** |

*Note: the video never assigns a trait to each location by name; support terms below are as narrated per area. Traits above are the menu the book draws from.*

## Otari Directory — Areas 1–20

*Each stat block is given as: level · address · NPC (alignment, sex, ancestry, role, level) · starting attitude · Perception DC / Will DC.*

### Area 1. The Giant's Wheel (Otari Mill) · Lumber mill · level 5
▶ [29:09](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1749)

- **What it is:** **the Otari Mill, known locally as the Giant's Wheel for its towering 30-foot-diameter water wheel.** **That size is needed for the torque to power the mechanized treadmill that loads logs into the flume and to keep the saws running.** **Much of the yard's income comes from the town's two larger lumber families, who pay to have their lumber processed here.** **The noise:** **the grinding, churning wheel competes with the whine of the saws and the clatter of the loading ramp.**
- **Who runs it:** **the Menhemes family leaves day-to-day administration to a heavily scarred half-orc foreman, Klorte Hengus** ⚠F7. **In his youth he made a name for himself in [[locations/absalom|Absalom]]'s gladiatorial arena, the Irorium, fighting with paired hatchets — and picked up a moniker (Player's Guide: "the Lumberjacker").** **He's put that violent past behind him and fails to see any irony in now being an honest-to-goodness lumberjack.**
- **Stat block:** level 5 lumber mill · **10 Millers Road** · **Klorte: LN male half-orc foreman, level 5** · **Indifferent** · **Perception DC 20 / Will DC 20.**
- **Support:** **Earn Income using Survival to patrol the flume's length** (*GM option: encounters with local wildlife to liven up a shift of flume walking*). **If Klorte warms to them he might even give them one of his old +1 hatchets.**

### Area 2. Loading Flume
▶ [30:55](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1855)

- **A mechanized treadmill runs from the mill into a wooden flume filled with water from the Osprey River.** **Treadmill and chute carry timber from the Giant's Wheel hundreds of feet south to a loading chute that descends into the harbor,** so timber loads straight onto ships.
- **Local youths love riding the chute; after a few unfortunate accidents, Klorte keeps a wary eye out for such foolishness.**

### Area 3. Menhemes Manor · Manor · level 4
▶ [31:27](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1887)

- **Who lives here:** **[[Oseph Menhemes|Mayor Oseph Menhemes]], his wife Mnala** ⚠F10, **their five children and a small army of servants.** **One of the town's oldest structures — a sprawling, cross-shaped building larger than the clan needs.** **The entire west wing has been closed for two generations; the east wing is now a publicly accessible museum of Otari's history** (sidebar below).
- **How Oseph works:** **he keeps his roles apart — mornings as a public figure, afternoons managing his company, Otari Lumber.** **The heroes will deal more with the family, particularly Oseph's oldest daughter, [[Dorianna Menhemes|Dorianna]]** ⚠F10, **in chapter 5** ([Floor 5's Otari-side chapter](floor-05-into-the-training-grounds.md)).
- **Stat block:** level 4 manor · **10 Highside Road** · **Oseph: N male human mayor, level 4** · **Indifferent** · **Perception DC 19 / Will DC 19.**
- **Support:** **Earn Income using Society to tutor the family's children and prepare them for [[locations/absalom|Absalom]]'s social scene.**

#### Sidebar: Public Museum of the Roseguard
▶ [32:45](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=1965)

**The [[Roseguard]] made their name in Absalom, defeated the villainous [[Belcorra Haruvex]] and founded what is now the logging town of Otari.** **Otari honors them on Founders Day — 3 Desnus** *(caption: "three Destinies"; date per the Player's Guide).* **The Roseguard were:**

| Hero | Role and character |

## Wrin Sivinxi
▶ [1:05:25](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=3925)

*The campaign's quest-giver. Stat line: **CG female tiefling elf oddities merchant, level 5** (Area 4).*

- **Origin:** **grew up in the small village of Glitterburg in Kyonin** ⚠F15 — **which no longer exists:** **demons in the service of Treerazer destroyed it 10 years ago.** **[[Wrin Sivinxi|Wrin]] feels no sadness, because no one died:** **days before the demon army struck she had a vision of the disaster, warned her parents and neighbors, and their trust in her visions gave the town time to relocate everyone.** **The demons found Glitterburg deserted; when they burned it in frustration they suffered badly from the magical traps the elves had left behind.**
- **Leaving home:** **a few years later, when she came of age, she left her parents and friends, eager to find her own place.** **She trusts her visions and her unusual faith in a pantheon of deities of space and time she calls the Cosmic Caravan — reading the nightly stars to decide about tomorrow.**
- **The shop:** **her knack for finding lost things and gathering strange treasures made a curio shop the obvious choice.** **Otari wasn't chosen — it was simply the port she reached when the weight of her collection finally outweighed its portability.** **She opened [[wrins_wonders|Wrin's Wonders]] 4 years ago; many townsfolk regard her as a good-natured eccentric.** **Sales of the strange and bizarre aren't brisk in a small town, but she's patient and happy.**
- **Her dream:** **to earn enough to fund a trip west** — **an abandoned observatory in the small town of Crook Cove on Ravounel's southwestern coast** ⚠F15 **that she hopes to buy and turn into a temple of the Cosmic Caravan.** **For now she's content in Otari, sleeping under the stars.**
- **Look:** **her tiefling ancestry is impossible to miss, and long ago she decided to embrace it:** **pupil-less eyes, small ram horns curling around her elven ears, a thin tail, fingertips sparkling with motes of glittering light.** **A quick smile and welcoming attitude have always won her friends.**

### Campaign Use
▶ [1:07:53](https://youtu.be/sAqvBOPr1wA?t=4073)

- **Purpose:** **[[Wrin Sivinxi|Wrin]] motivates the heroes' first explorations and gives them a focal point.** **The AP assumes she's a contact the heroes share, so when she notices a strange buildup of eerie light atop the distant [[Gauntlight]], it's natural for her to confide her fears in them** ([Floor 1](floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog.md)).
- **Ongoing help:** **astrology readings to aid their explorations;** **she won't give her stores away for free, but hers is the best place to buy and sell the odd magical treasures heroes find in a dungeon crawl.** **She can also introduce them to the spirit world — her tutelage unlocks the *Ghost Hunter* archetype** (*see the Adventure Toolbox video in this series*).
- **Emergency use:** **despite her claustrophobia, if the heroes are in over their heads in the Abomination Vaults, Wrin can face her fears, brave the dungeon and mount a rescue** — **but use her that way only as a last resort, to keep the campaign from reaching an untimely end.**
