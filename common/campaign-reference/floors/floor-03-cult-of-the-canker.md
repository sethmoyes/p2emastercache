### Chapter 3: Cult of the Canker
▶ [1:58](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=118)

- **Purpose:** this level was [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s **sprawling library and research rooms.** **The servants and librarians who worked here are now dangerous undead.**
- **Features:** **unlit except where noted.** **Ceilings 8 ft in halls, 10 ft in rooms; the largest rooms and halls are vaulted at 12 ft.** **Wooden doors, many marked with signs (see C11) to help scholars navigate.**
- **The [[Cult of the Canker]]** is **the most widespread faction.** **Originally librarians, caretakers and scribes**, this loyal staff **kept working after Belcorra died — on a way to restore her to life.** **When food ran low, rather than risk alerting the [[Roseguard]] by going to the surface, the researchers turned to cannibalism** (**plenty of servants to eat in the nearby quarters**). **Ghoul fever tore through their ranks — they were so obsessed they barely noticed.**
- **Their research now has a "distinctly ghoulish bent":** they **gather enough *cankerous flesh* to fuel Belcorra's full resurrection** — **but the ghouls' hunger keeps compelling them to eat the very flesh they're collecting.** **That slows them, but not their zeal.**

## Rooms C1–C40

### C1. Visitors' Reading Room · Trivial 3
▶ [3:43](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=223)

- **Sensory:** **a large cross-shaped chamber with long couches (leather cushions cracked with age), dead plants slumped in planter boxes, and a long dry fountain shaped like a lighthouse.** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **allowed visitors only here and in the reading rooms to the north** — **a librarian fetched tomes for them.**
- **Stairs (east door):** up to **[[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B16. Pantry · no encounter|B16]].**
- **Secret door (south):** **DC 27 Perception** — **hidden behind a heavy sofa.**
- **Creature — [[Augrael]]** ("Orel"; repo: a ghoulish [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlock]], a great recurring NPC), **a unique undead:** **a morlock ghoul exiled by his living king among the Ghost Queen morlocks above — and reviled by the Cult's ghouls on this level.** **Not steeped in the malice or fervor of either, he's calm and collected; he greets the heroes coolly but cautiously.**
  - **He wants only to live in the dungeon reading and feeding on undead flesh** (**he has little taste for flesh that was merely once living**).
  - **Memory:** **hazy recollections of the morlocks coming up from below and fighting the ghouls; the ghoul fever he caught fragmented his memories.** **He knows a rough layout of C1, C6, C10, C11, C15 and C33** (**those memories are freshest**).
  - **Attitude:** **Indifferent — Friendly if the heroes prove that his chief morlock rival, [[Graulgust]], is dead.**
  - **Recruiting him:** **DC 20 Diplomacy (Request)** — **1 day on a success, 1 week on a critical success** — **or DC 20 Intimidation (Coerce): 1 day on a success, and on a *critical failure* he attacks in rage.** **He won't leave this level under any circumstances.**
  - **His habits:** **he gluts himself on the ghouls the heroes defeated — "off-putting and macabre."**
- **XP: 30** for gaining Augrael as an ally.

### C8. Korlok's Post · Moderate 3
▶ [12:21](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=741)

- **Sensory:** **the walls are covered in hundreds upon hundreds of tiny hash marks — as if someone has been counting days for ages.** **An iron door with a barred window in the NW wall; a large open padlock hangs from its latch** (→ C9).
- **Creature — [[Korlok]], a barbazu** ⚠F1: **the *second* of the two devils [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] stationed on the upper levels** (the first is a zebub janitor one level up). **He takes his post literally:** **Belcorra's last order was *"Wait here and let no one out until I come for you"* — she never did.** **Centuries of that have started to grate.**
  - **On the heroes' entry he stands at attention and, slightly bored, says they're under arrest and should surrender their gear before stepping into the adjoining prison.** **He fights to the death if attacked or if any hero tries to leave (it gives him an excuse to leave his post).**
- **Side quest — Korlok's contract:**
  - **He's willing to talk a few minutes; attitude Indifferent.** **Improve it — likely DC 21 Diplomacy (Make an Impression), or Deception (Impersonate/Lie) — and he admits his frustration.**
  - **He knows Belcorra is dead, but without his contract he can't study it for a loophole.** **His offer: he'll let them go *this once*, but next time he'll arrest them unless they bring him his contract** (**he suspects it's hidden somewhere in the library**).
  - **If they deliver it,** he studies it and finds **his service was meant to end once *99 weeks and 99 days* passed without a new order from Belcorra.** **Before vanishing back to Hell, the grateful devil shares what he knows:** **a zebub janitor on the level above and a contract devil named [[Urevian]] on a lower level** — **scorn for the zebub; Urevian is "shrewd but well known for keeping his word."**

### C11. Main Library · Moderate 3
▶ [15:42](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=942)

- **Sensory:** **hundreds of stone shelves carved into the walls of a 15-ft-wide hall — many gaps, but a staggering number of books, scrolls and folios remain.** **A faint blue glow flickers beneath a sign to the south.** **The Cult keeps the stacks organized.**
- **Research bonus:** **8 hours with the tomes teaches the library's organization: +1 item bonus to Recall Knowledge on regional history, arcane or occult topics when using *Investigate* here.**
- **The signs (all in Undercommon):**

| Sign over… | Reads |
|---|---|
| C15 (south) | **Reading Room** |
| C12 | **Repair Storage** |
| C13 | **Workroom** |
| C17 | **Safety** (with a carving of leaping flames) |
| C18 | **Personal Quarters** |
| C19 | **Scrivener's Office** |
| C26 | **[[Belcorra Haruvex\|Belcorra]]'s Office** |
| Hall to C30–C32 | **North Wing** |
| C33 | **Restricted Collection** |

- **Creatures — four ghouls in long pale blue robes** guard and **fetch books for the scriveners or cult leaders.** **They don't attack at once — they lick their lips as they size up the party's "fleshy bits."** **One asks if the heroes are here *to donate flesh for the canker*** — **a hero foolish enough to agree must lop off an appendage (a finger, toe or ear will do); the ghoul scurries off to C34 with it.** **Any other interaction makes them impatient — they attack** and **fight until destroyed.** **If the fight lasts more than 4 rounds or is unusually loud, the ghouls in C15 come to investigate.**
- **Side quest — [[Morlibint]]'s books:** **a hero who knows Morlibint of [[odd_stories|Odd Stories]] ([[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]) — or succeeds at DC 14 Society (Recall Knowledge) on seeing the books — realizes he'd love this collection.** **Told about it, the wizard gives a long list of specific titles, publishers and themes to seek.** **Each room's Treasure notes its book *Bulk*:** **DC 18 Perception to sort through a room's books: 1 Bulk on a success, 2 on a critical success (from that room's total).** **Morlibint pays 5 gp per Bulk.** **Delivering at least 12 Bulk completes the side quest** (**XP not stated** ⚠F7); **he'll buy more if found.**
- **Treasure:** **only 2 Bulk of general-knowledge books here** (little interest to Morlibint).

### C13. Librarian's Workroom · Moderate 3
▶ [20:02](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=1202)

- **Sensory:** **three worktables along the walls, one stool, and a skeleton in a light brown librarian's robe lying beside it.** **Three specialized benches for binding books, repairing pages and preparing parchment — all old and useless.**
- **Creature — the ghost of the librarian [[Jerelle Kadrin]]** ⚠F3: **she couldn't escape the other scholars' increasingly gruesome scheme, so she locked herself in and drank a jar of bookbinding arsenic — rather than be eaten alive.** **She died quickly and in agony, and rose as a ghost that pantomimes repairing books.** **Suspicious but not hostile unless the heroes show aggression; she becomes irate and attacks if they speak ill of [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]].** **If they talk rather than fight, they can see she's compelled to reenact her life's work.**
- **XP:** **putting her to rest without fighting = XP as if defeated.**
- **Treasure (her remains):** **the key to the C13 door · a gold paperweight (10 gp) · a crafter's eyepiece.**

▶ [26:17](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=1577)

  - **Absorbed: −2 Initiative if interrupted.** **He seethes and curses, wasting an action each round for the first two rounds to voice displeasure — announcing the *blasphemous limericks and humiliating odes* he'll tattoo onto their corpses.** **Single-minded: pursues foes relentlessly; fights until destroyed.**

### C27. Belcorra's Reading Room · Moderate 3

### C34. Temple of the Canker · Moderate 3
▶ [44:19](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=2659)

- **Sensory:** **soft lights illuminate several smashed, empty display cases along the curved north wall.** **A hideous towering woman of hundreds of stitched-together pieces of flesh from many creatures, draped in tattered filthy sheets; the stench fills even this vast room.**
- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] displayed her most valuable tomes here.** **The grave knight [[Caliddo Haruvex]]** ("Kedo Harx" in captions), **a distant relative and loyal guardian of her treasure vault far below, retrieved the books and took them back to the vault on her orders** *(→ [Floor 9](floor-09-on-the-hunt.md), [[floor-09-on-the-hunt#I56. Caliddo's Vigil · Low 9|I56]]–[[floor-09-on-the-hunt#I58. Vault of Secrets · no encounter|I58]]).* **The magical display light remains, so the room is well lit.**
- **The statue:** **an incomplete shell akin to a flesh golem — the Cult means it to be Belcorra's new body.** **Inanimate for now.**
- **Secret door (west):** **particularly well hidden — DC 30 Perception.** **None of the ghouls know it exists.** **(All secret doors on Levels 1–3 are shown on the blueprint in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D8. Lounge · Severe 4|D8]].)**
- **Creature — [[Nhakazarin]], High Priestess of the [[Cult of the Canker]]** (a ghoul; captions "Narin / Nakazarin"): **cruel and sadistic even in life; born with an infestation of undeath in her soul, she was one of Belcorra's necromantic advisers and, after Belcorra's death, the only one who rallied the level's servants to prepare her resurrection.** **She works with flesh harvested from [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]], monsters and other ghouls, stitching it into her masterpiece: a 15-ft statue of Belcorra of decaying meat and stained bone.**
  - **She demands the heroes abandon their gear and lives, promising *glory with the canker* if they accept ghoul fever and are locked in the Author's Hall (C38) until they transform.** **If refused she laughs imperiously and attacks, using Intimidation for initiative, promising they'll join her cult "willingly or not."**
  - **Tactics:** **she prefers to fight adjacent to a statue so she can use *Consumed Masterpiece*.**
- **Note (long game):** **track the condition the heroes leave the statue in — it may animate later depending on their actions in [[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F25. Main Laboratory · Severe 6|F25]]** *(if they fail to shut off [[Jafaki]]'s occult machinery — [Floor 6](floor-06-experiments-in-flesh.md))*.

### C35. Secure Collection · no encounter
▶ [47:29](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=2849)

- **Sensory:** **a small room with built-in shelves: books to the north and south, loose scrolls in the west shelf, one chair and a small writing desk in the middle.** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] kept her most valuable books in the big chamber to the east (C34) but stored everything about her [[Gauntlight]] and Abomination Vaults work here — undisturbed since her death because the ghouls don't know it exists.**
- **Belcorra's journal** (north shelf, written in Aklo): **8 hours of reading teaches:**
  - **her family's history — from exile to poverty and her return to the region to build a lair from which to take revenge on the city that disgraced and impoverished them;**
  - **in the last pages, the Vaults' construction:** **fleshwarping laboratories, an arena where she pits enemies against monstrous champions for amusement, prisons, torture chambers, links to the Darklands, and *a great temple where the [[Nhimbaloth|Empty Death]] herself once brushed against this fragile world*;**
  - **she speaks of her minions in the abstract — naming only one: her "amusingly vain apprentice," [[Volluk Azrinae]].** **One late entry says he deserted a community of upstart metalsmith drow below to serve her** (→ [[Yldaris]]; no more detail).
- **Loose papers** (Aklo): **8 hours sorting and reading them teaches the Gauntlight's functions "as if fully researched."**
- **Treasure:** **a folio of esoteric diagrams (between the teleportation books on the north shelf):** **Belcorra's network of linked teleportation circles, stressing the *awaken portal* ritual to power them should time or mischance drain them — the *awaken portal* ritual is included.** **On the south shelf, the rare magical text *The [[Whispering Reeds]]*:** **77 supposed encounters with the Outer Goddess Nhimbaloth and the physical/mental scars each left — heavily annotated in Belcorra's hand, especially where Nhimbaloth appears as an *eater of ghosts*.**

### C36. Scriptorium · Severe 3
▶ [50:38](https://youtu.be/Jg9ctse9BNA?t=3038)

- **Sensory:** **tables divided into narrow carrels with old wooden chairs pushed in, stacks of paper and tattered books amid dry inkwells and brittle quills.** **A skeleton in a shiny chain shirt lies slumped to the south, clutching something.**
- **Secret door → C37:** **DC 18 Perception on the east wall finds an empty carrel that folds up.**
- **Creature — the ghost of [[Chandriu Invisar]]** (captions "Chandu / Chandrea"; a drow, director of the scriptorium):
  - **History:** **she had a poorly hidden adoration for [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s apprentice, [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]].** **After Belcorra's death she begged him to flee with her; he spurned her: *"Belcorra needs me now, in death, even more than in life. You should consider the same."*** **She returned in despair and was murdered by her disgruntled scribes, tired of her bullying — clutching Volluk's portrait as she died.**
  - **Now:** **she floats among the workspaces looking over scribes' shoulders, unaware any time has passed since the hour before Belcorra's death.** **She takes the heroes for tardy new scribes and orders them to transcribe old books.** **Anything but complying — even leaving — angers her and prompts an attack.** **If they sit at a carrel and work (or pantomime it) she's satisfied until they stop.** **She entertains brief questions and answers any that don't make her realize Belcorra is dead or that she's a ghost.**
  - **Her rounds:** **every 10 minutes she drifts through the secret door to C37 (DC 12 Perception to notice it hints at a hidden door) and returns in 1d4 rounds — a window to escape.** **If they return later, she doesn't remember them.** **If they attack her or force her to face the truth, she cries out in despair and attacks.**
- **Treasure:** **1 Bulk of books ([[Morlibint]])** · ***What the Worm Knows*, *Grave Feasts* and *Secrets of the Skull*** (the three books mentioned in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B3. Volluk's Chambers · no encounter|B3]]'s notes — **Morlibint has no interest in necromancy; sell them for 8 gp each elsewhere — [[Yinyasmera]] might find a buyer**) · **Chandriu's +1 chain shirt (good condition)** · **the framed portrait of Volluk she clutches** — **shows his beauty and "imperious, vain smirk," a gold plaque with his name; worth 10 gp — *and possibly a weapon against Volluk in [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D8. Lounge · Severe 4|D8]]*** · **DC 15 Perception while searching her remains finds a tiny iron key beneath her — it unlocks the C37 cabinet.**
