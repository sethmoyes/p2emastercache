## Deadtide for Otari
▶ [2:40](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=160)

- **What happens:** the [[Gauntlight]] **isn't fully functional but has enough restored power that [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] activates it as a *test firing* before the involved process of restoring it fully.** **She targets [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s graveyard, animating the dead in it.** **Her ghost watches through the Gauntlight's lens but can only observe** (limited to the sight of her haunting deep in the Vaults). **The Gauntlight shudders; its light blazes with a sickly pale illumination; the beam arcs south, landing not on Otari itself but on the sprawling graveyard at the town's western edge.** **It stays fixed there, visible from the Gauntlight all the way to town.**
- **The heroes can't stop it** — it comes from the Gauntlight and can **only be stopped by Belcorra's will.** **Because the dead are buried, most take some time to dig free.** **The heroes may hurry to the graveyard — but you can run these events as written even if they first tend wounds or delay.** **If they take more than a couple of hours, town guards have already put a barrier around the cemetery but are afraid to enter** (a few corpses have already shambled free). **Dealing with the undead should fall to the heroes; if they aren't self-motivated, have [[Lardus Longsaddle|Captain Longsaddle]], [[Oseph Menhemes|Mayor Oseph Menhemes]] or [[Vandy Banderdash]] ask.** **If they keep hesitating, the undead get worse.**

### Otari Graveyard · Severe 2
▶ [4:40](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=280)

- **Sensory:** **the graveyard's soil roils and churns with an eerie blue light.** **Dozens of skeletons and shambling corpses haul themselves free and rush south — without hesitation — and *plummet over the cliff's edge onto the domes of the [[dawnflower_library|Dawnflower Library]] below.*** **Most of the raised dead die in that fall; a few remain in the graveyard.** **The damage is done: [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s citizens are horrified and panicked.**
- **Why it matters:** **stepping into the graveyard or taking on the few remaining undead is an important symbolic stand — the heroes' first real step toward being recognized as Otari's saviors.**
- **Creatures:** **five skeleton guards and three zombie shamblers remain.** **Technically a Severe encounter for level 2 — but the undead are scattered (none within 30 ft of another), though they move quickly toward any fight or distraction.** **Stealth and ranged tactics can thin them before they close.**
- **Town guards (four):** **horrified and little help; they flee if attacked.** **At your discretion — especially if a hero requests or coerces their aid — some fight, or drag fallen heroes to safety.** **Treat them as *frightened 3* at the start of any fight they join.**

### Abomination's Arrival · Moderate 2
▶ [6:38](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=398)

- **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] watches with amusement from afar.** **As soon as the graveyard fight ends she decides to test the [[Gauntlight]]'s *primary function: teleporting monsters among her enemies.*** **She orders her apprentice [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]] to bring one of the scalathrax from [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D10. Scalathrax Pen · Moderate 4|D10]] into [[floor-04-long-dream-the-dead#D9. Lasda's Lament · Moderate 4|D9]]** — **the delay gives the heroes a few minutes to recover** — **then focuses the beam on the heroes' location.** **The light raises goose flesh on those it touches and scatters the remaining guards.**
- **Creature:** **an instant after the beam lands, a spider-like scalathrax ⚠F8 manifests among the heroes — and the Gauntlight goes dark.** **The aberration uses Stealth for initiative, attacks immediately, and fights to the death.** **A hero who witnesses its sudden arrival and succeeds at DC 20 Arcana or Occultism (during or after) realizes it was *teleported by the beam.***

### Dying Light
▶ [7:57](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=477)

- **After delivering the scalathrax the [[Gauntlight]] goes dark — it will be *at least a month* before [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] can activate it again** (to animate more undead or teleport another monster — to attack the heroes or just spread panic).
- **[[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] doesn't know that schedule:** **the fear it could shine again at any moment terrifies the town — people won't go out after dark, and rumors about Belcorra's return run wild.**
- **The hook:** **if the heroes played a key, visible role, [[Oseph Menhemes|Mayor Oseph Menhemes]] soon contacts them — asking them to find out what caused this and, if possible, prevent a repeat** (**have other town notables present**). ***Reward: 50 gp per hero from the town treasury if they return to Gauntlight Keep and ensure the Gauntlight never shines on Otari again.***

### Servants' Quarters Features
▶ [9:09](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=549)

- **The first underground level once housed [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s servants — they lived and worked out of sight of [[Gauntlight|Gauntlight Keep]] above.** **When she was killed the servants panicked — some fled, some hid, and others *became food for the cannibal librarians on the next floor down (Level 3).*** **Today it's largely ruins.**
- **Light:** **unlit unless noted.** **Ceilings: 8 ft in halls, 10 ft in rooms.** **Doors: sturdy wood, *swollen shut with moisture* — a hero *trained in Athletics* opens them with one Interact action; everyone else needs two.**
- **The largest faction — the Children of the Ghost Queen ([[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]]):** **they once lived deep below, in caverns beside the Vaults.** **When Belcorra awoke they climbed up to "prepare the way for her," enduring a devastating clash with the [[Cult of the Canker]] ghouls and later driving out the [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]].** **Belcorra has always thought morlocks pests — even as a ghost — but their leader [[Graulgust]] hopes to change her mind by building her a temple.** **But just as [[Nhimbaloth]] cares little how Belcorra is honored, the morlocks' Ghost Queen doesn't care whether her devout morlocks live or die.**

### B2. Volluk's Workshop · no encounter
▶ [11:03](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=663)

- **Door:** **locked.** **Without the key from [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A20. Repair Storage · no encounter|A20]]: DC 20 Athletics to force, or three successes at DC 20 Thievery.**
- **Sensory:** **seven square alcoves, each with strange mechanical apparatus or alchemical tools, ring a damp stone chamber.** **The north alcove holds a large glass tank of liquid with a goblin corpse in outdated servants' clothing floating inside.** **A semicircular worktable in front of the tank is covered with tools, moldy papers and a large open book — the bulk of [[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]]'s notes.**
- **The notes:** **DC 12 Arcana or Occultism (Recall Knowledge):** **the workshop is devoted to both construct-building and necromancy;** **a critical success adds that its emphasis is *transferring mortal souls into constructs or vermin swarms.***
- **The goblin — [[Borbo]]:** **once Volluk's devoted servant; he volunteered his body and soul for a technique Volluk promised would grant immortality.** **Being turned into the soul doll *[[Mr. Beak]]* wasn't the immortality he had in mind — yet, as Mr. Beak, he stayed loyal.** **The corpse is well preserved in the tank; even removed, it takes months to decay.**
- **Other alcoves:** **little of interest, with two exceptions:**
  - **Secret door (SW alcove):** **leads down to [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C10. Twin Stairwells · no encounter|C10]] and back up to B32.** **DC 20 Perception identifies a toolbox bolted to the wall — pulling the tools opens the door.**
  - **Incomplete soulbound doll (NW alcove):** **resembles an armless, legless goblin with a back indentation the size of Mr. Beak's *soul focus gem* (from [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A23. Lens Workshop · Moderate 1|A23]]).** **Inserting the gem lets it speak — but not attack, move or cast until construction is complete** (**a hero can finish it with enough work and expense**, but **Borbo stays erratic and violent**).
- **Talking to Borbo:** **returned to this body he wakes quickly and sobs, lamenting in Goblin that his master betrayed him.** **He's inconsolable until a hero calms him (DC 16 Diplomacy), frightens him (DC 16 Intimidation to Coerce), or lets him see his old body in the north alcove.** **Then he goes silent and can speak Common, Elven or Goblin.** **He has little useful to say and is prone to self-pity and vitriol toward Volluk — but the right questions yield four things:**
  1. **Where the secret door is.**
  2. **A secret treasure stash behind a loose brick in his old room (B4).**
  3. ***"Master Volluk was a powerful mastermind drow occult artist craftsman who had a relationship with a book lady from down below and who worked for Mistress [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]], who is very frightening and absolutely none of my business."***
  4. ***Volluk is vain as well as cruel:* present him with a nice image of Volluk (e.g. a painting), then threaten to destroy it — Volluk loses his composure and likely makes a tactical error.** **The only painting Borbo knows of is in [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A22. Hall of Portraits · no encounter|A22]] — destroyed;** **the *only* surviving image of Volluk in the Vaults is in [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C36. Scriptorium · Severe 3|C36]]** ([[Chandriu Invisar|Chandriu]]'s portrait — [Floor 3](floor-03-cult-of-the-canker.md)).
- **Releasing Borbo:** **once he's told them all this he pitifully asks to be freed.** **Smashing the soul focus gem frees his soul — and for this compassion *Pharasma* grants the hero who smashed it a momentary prophetic glimpse of their fate: a +2 status bonus to a single check, applied even *after* seeing the result; one month to use it.**

### B12. Graulgust's Throne Room · Severe 2
▶ [24:46](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=1486)

- **Sensory:** **a sofa-like throne of an old mattress, reconstructed furniture and dozens of bones sprawls along the north side.** **A narrow rivulet of muddy water trickles from a tunnel to the south and pools in the center.**
- **Creature — [[Graulgust]], self-styled king of the [[Ghost Queen morlocks]]** (a morlock *occultist*), **who chose the majordomo's old bedroom as his throne room.** **A morlock servant attends him always — sharpening his fingernails and toenails, rubbing his feet, feeding him worms, whatever he demands.** **He occasionally patrols and checks the [[Belcorra Haruvex|Ghost Queen]] Shrine (B14), but you meet him here the first time.**
  - **Communication:** **he fancies himself a worldly diplomat — but speaks only Aklo and Undercommon.** **If he can't communicate he grows annoyed and sends his servants to fight; he joins only once they're dead or he's attacked directly.**
  - **At 30 HP** he spends an action **howling for his pet grothlut (B13)**, which comes as fast as it can through the SW tunnel.
  - **When he dies,** **any morlocks present are *fleeing* for a round and *frightened 3*.**
- **Side quest — the Ghost Queen's test (if he can communicate):** ***he congratulates them on their bravery in seeking him out and offers a choice:***
  1. **Become sacrifices for the Ghost Queen (his servants kill them), or**
  2. **Prove their worth by *slaying the water monster* nearby (B19)** ⚠F3 **and bringing back her head.** **He lets them leave only via the tunnel** ⚠F3. **If they return *without* the river drake's head he attacks at once.**
  - **If they bring the head,** **Graulgust is delighted — the morlocks now have an easy way out to forage.** **He names them *Lackeys of the Ghost Queen* and walks them through her territory — B10, B9, B8, B14, B21, B22.** **At B22 he offers a new choice:** **surrender their gear, become true slaves, and be imprisoned in B24 — or begin a crusade for him against the *[[Cult of the Canker]]* ("heretic ghouls") who must be slaughtered for the glory of the Ghost Queen.** **If they accept, he sends them downstairs via B16, ordered not to return until all the ghouls are dead.**
  - **If they succeed,** **he realizes they're too powerful to dispose of, names them *Missionaries of the Ghost Queen*, and commands them to leave the morlock areas and never return.**
- **What he can share (once they've earned trust):** **the morlocks haven't explored the north of this level, but he can reveal the secret door to B15 and give a scribbled map of Level 3 — [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C1. Visitors' Reading Room · Trivial 3|C1]], [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C6. Guard Room · Low 3|C6]], [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C10. Twin Stairwells · no encounter|C10]], [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C11. Main Library · Moderate 3|C11]], [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C15. Gauntlight Reading Room · Moderate 3|C15]] and [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C33. Restricted Collection · Severe 3|C33]].** **Asked about the deeper levels he falls silent:** ***"You are of the surface and do not deserve to know of our homeland."*** **(At your discretion, mind control or coercion might force more — but the morlocks left the depths long ago and much has changed since Belcorra's return.)**
- **Never say her name:** **Graulgust never speaks the Ghost Queen's name aloud; if a hero says "Belcorra" in his presence he shrieks in anger and warns them never to say it again — and attacks if they do.**
- **Betrayal:** **once they've won his trust, the morlock occultist is shocked if they turn on him — if they attack him after he believes they work for him, he is *slowed 1 for two rounds* as he grapples with no longer commanding obedience.**

### B13. Grothlut Den · Low 2
▶ [29:48](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=1788)

- **Sensory:** **water drips down the walls, especially along a jumble of dangling pale roots to the south; below them, a large soggy nest of mud and bones.** **The cavern is the product of years of erosion; its damp, earthy air suits the [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]]' new pet.**
- **Creature — a grothlut** (fleshwarp) **that [[Graulgust]] rescued from a cage deeper in the Vaults.** **It dotes on the morlocks "the way a loyal dog adores its master" and attacks anyone but Graulgust who enters — to the death.**

### B14. Shrine of the Ghost Queen · no encounter
▶ [30:31](https://youtu.be/2qMR08mRnJs?t=1831)

- **Sensory:** **originally a kitchen, refitted as an improvised shrine.** **The west end, where a large cooking fireplace stood, is now an altar of sorts — over a human corpse looms a towering feminine statue of bones draped in moldering cloth and hanging moss.** **A bare patch on the north wall is painted in mud and blood with the same feminine form.**
- **History:** **built to honor [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] — but the [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]] fear her as much as they revere her, so they enter rarely, only for periodic sacrifices.** **The latest: one of the thieves from [[crooks_nook|Crook's Nook]]** (**a human man with a bird tattoo on his right shoulder — the mark of the [[Osprey Club]]; DC 16 Society identifies it as Thieves' Guild membership**).
- **Knowledge:** **DC 15 Religion:** **the divinity isn't a well-known goddess; the bloody scrawl over the secret door probably depicts someone important to the cult.** **Heroes who've seen an image of Belcorra (e.g. the painting in [[floor-01-a-light-in-the-fog#A22. Hall of Portraits · no encounter|A22]]) recognize the statue as a crude likeness.**
- **Secret door (north wall → B15):** **easier than most — DC 18 Perception while examining the crude decoration.** **The morlocks found it soon after arriving but fled from *the shadow inside* the one time they opened it; they think it leads somewhere sacred, decorated the wall to bait their patron, and haven't opened it since.**
