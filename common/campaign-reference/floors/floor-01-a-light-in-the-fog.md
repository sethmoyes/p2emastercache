### Chapter 1: A Light in the Fog
▶ [2:27](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=147)

The nursery rhyme [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]'s parents sing at bedtime:

> *"When the fog is creeping and the moon is low,*
> *When the town is sleeping, [[Gauntlight]] starts to glow.*
> *That's when she arises for her midnight lunch —*
> *Naughty kids are prizes for her teeth to crunch.*
> *But if you obey me and obey the rules,*
> *You're safe from [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] — she just eats the fools."*

- **What Otari believes:** **everyone who grows up in Otari has "a healthy mix of fear, respect and curiosity" for the ruins and the strange lighthouse at the swamp's heart.** **As they grow older they learn the accepted "truth": the heroic founders slew the wicked sorcerer Belcorra long ago; thrill-seekers explored the ruins for a while, but common knowledge now says the place is a *haven for pests* — no longer dangerous or valuable, completely picked over.** **Nearly 500 years on, she's menaced Otari only through sinister rhymes — *or so they think.***
- **What's true:** **many old legends are true (she did practice cannibalism — though not only on foolish children).** **The belief that she's no longer a danger is *false*:** **two years ago, on the 500th anniversary of the most significant event of her life — her family's exile from [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] for abhorrent rites — Belcorra's spiteful ghost awoke deep beneath the keep.** **The lighthouse and ruins are only the highest level of a vast complex she stocked with malicious creatures: the Abomination Vaults.** **Her spite has festered; she hungers for revenge on Absalom (for destroying her family) *and* on Otari and its founders — the heroes who killed her.** **She bides her time, re-energizing the Gauntlight and reasserting dominance over the feral, forgotten monsters below.**

### Sidebar: Beyond Gauntlight
▶ [5:15](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=315)

**The ruins are only a ~20-minute walk from [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]].** **The AP focuses almost entirely on the dungeon — gloss over the trips as you like.** **If players want variety, use encounters or short adventures from the standalone *Troubles in Otari* (the Otari Hinterlands, suited to levels 2–4) — a good way to gain extra XP before the deeper levels.**

### Getting Started
▶ [6:04](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=364)

- The campaign **begins as the heroes approach the aboveground ruins for the first time.** **Their mutual friend [[Wrin Sivinxi]] has already briefed them on her sightings of a strange glow atop the ruins' towering lighthouse.** **Nobody but Wrin can see it from town** — **but the heroes can confirm from nearby at night: the lighthouse top glows a faint, eerie blue after dark.**
- **You needn't start at the dungeon's front porch:** **ease in by roleplaying the meeting with Wrin** (perhaps an **astrology reading**) **or a stretch in town — last-minute shopping, a trip to the tavern.** **But the fun truly starts when the heroes head up the *[[Gauntlight]] Trail* to the ruins.**

### Gauntlight Ruins Features
▶ [7:10](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=430)

- **Read-aloud seed (approach):** *frogs and mosquitoes, water sloshing on muddy shores, all muffled by the ever-present [[Gauntlight#The Fogfen|Fogfen]] mist; as it clears a shadow looms from the cloying swamp vapors — a sprawling ruin of stone and wood squatting on an island in the soggy marsh, its upper floors largely collapsed, only the ground floor's stone walls standing; above it towers an out-of-place monument — a colossal lighthouse whose painted walls and iron-cast crown have resisted the swamp's corrosion.*
- **Access:** **the overgrown trail leads right to A1;** **other entrances lie along the south side.** **Water around the ruins is shallow, but on other sides 10–15 ft deep — a boat or swimming.**
- **Structure:** **apart from [[Gauntlight]] itself (A25), the keep's upper levels collapsed long ago, even falling to the ground floor in places.** **Areas marked "rubble" are open to the sky and difficult terrain.** **Ceilings are 10 ft where the roof remains.**
- **Light:** **only sunlight filtered through the constant fog.**
- **Doors:** **all cracked and rotten unless noted — force splits them or drops them off their hinges with a wet, splintering sound.**
- **Magical protection:** **Gauntlight is magically protected from damage; the effect has bled into the ruins, slowing the last 500 years' decay somewhat — though time is inexorable.**
- **The main faction — the [[Mud Lickers]]:** **what's left of a small mitflit gang led by a large bearded gremlin, *[[Boss Skrawng]]*** ⚠F1. **They believe they once lived in glory underground, until much larger, meaner *[[Ghost Queen morlocks]]* drove them out, and they've struggled since.** **Skrawng has formed the "inkling of a plan" to raise an army of giant insects and conquer [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] — and has exuberantly promised to restore Mud Licker glory that way, so the gang loyally works to keep intruders out.**
- **GM tip:** **note what the players' characters generally do while exploring;** **use the Core Rulebook's exploration activities or ask each player to describe what they do and pick the best fit.** **Rooms list effects by whether a hero is *Investigating*, *Searching*, or doing another activity.**

## Rooms A1–A25

### A1. Damp Entrance · Low 1
▶ [10:37](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=637)

- **Sensory:** **mildew and mold smear the damp walls green and black.** **Door frames in the north and south walls rotted away long ago; a few collapsed chairs lie amid heaps of foul-smelling gray lumps; thick sheets of dusty cobwebs hang above, hiding the ceiling.** **Once the keep's primary entrance — a one-story outbuilding of sturdy stone.**
- **The "drop ceiling":** **the cobwebs hang at 7 ft, leaving a 3-ft gap under the stone roof.** **The [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] have rigged a shoddy network of soggy ropes and planks up there.** **DC 12 Perception spots it through the webbing; a critical success also spots a 3-ft-diameter hole in the NW roof corner they use to come and go.** **DC 15 Nature (Recall Knowledge) identifies the gray lumps as *mitflit dung* — the room is a latrine as well as an advance guard post.**
- **Creatures — three mitflits** lurk above the cobwebs and **can't resist tormenting the heroes.** **They wait until at least two heroes are inside, then imitate a high-pitched pixie voice: *welcoming them to their "parlor," warning of "mean bugs" in the keep, and suggesting they try the "magic pixie mud" on the floor.*** **They're terrible liars (−1 Deception, speak only Undercommon) — but if it works they're overcome with giggles and give themselves away.**
  - **When spotted, or the trick fails:** **they shriek in anger and fear; one climbs out the roof hole and tries to cross the vine from A1 to A9 to warn his boss in A10; the other two throw darts.** **As soon as any takes damage it shrieks, casts *bane*, and flees to A9.**
  - **The maggot steak:** **they keep a nasty chunk of giant maggot meat as a snack; a fleeing one tosses it onto the A3 pond shore to lure the slurk out against the heroes.**
- **Treasure:** **searching finds a peridot bead (2 gp — one mitflit thought it was candy) and a chipped mitflit tooth in a dung pile.**

#### Sidebar: Wandering Monsters
▶ [13:53](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=833)

**There are no wandering-monster tables — but the denizens still leave their homes.** **Make the dungeon feel dynamic by learning each level's denizens and having them react:** **noisy exploring → nearby creatures investigate or ambush; camping in the dungeon rather than returning to town → they may visit or attack the camp; clearing a level and returning weeks later → new monsters may have moved in.** **Keeping inhabitants active makes the place feel more dangerous and unpredictable.**

### A2. Decrepit Drawbridge · no encounter
▶ [14:50](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=890)

- **A wooden drawbridge, its timbers gray with age, spans a 20-ft gap between the outbuilding and the island ruins; rusty iron chains from the ruins' north wall to the bridge's south end look ready to fall apart.**
- **Sabotage:** **the [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] have undercut its supports.** **DC 5 Perception spots it.** **A Small or smaller creature can cross safely; Medium creatures must cross *one at a time* or it collapses with a groaning crash, dumping everyone on it into 4-ft-deep water.** **A creature near the end can grab an edge with a DC 10 Reflex save.** **After a collapse, DC 10 Athletics climbs up to A1 or A4.** **The slurks in A3 come to investigate fast.**

### A3. Slurk Pond · Low 1
▶ [16:11](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=971)

- **Sensory:** **thick reeds ring a muddy pond of murky, algae-green water; the half-decayed body of a 3-ft scaly humanoid lies half-buried in mud on the north bank.** **The pond is 4 ft deep, but a few more feet of mud make it greater difficult terrain to wade.**
- **The vine:** **DC 12 Nature (Recall Knowledge) picks out an unusually long vine — it runs from the top of A1's watchtower to the north end of A9's walkway; the [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] strung it to avoid the ground.** **It won't support anything larger than Small.**
- **Creature — a slurk:** **the former guardian and only survivor of the *Stone Scale kobolds* who recently lived in the ruins.** **The mitflits play *"Dodge the Squirt"* — running across the vine while the slurk spits slime at them.** **It sleeps mid-pond with its snout above water by day and flops out to hunt at night.** **Loud noises (a collapsing drawbridge), sudden free food (a tossed maggot steak), or splashing at the shore wake it.** **It attacks eagerly but never leaves sight of the pond; fights to the death.**
- **Treasure (the dead kobold):** **the slurk won't eat it — lingering loyalty to the murdered tribe — and its presence kept the mitflits from looting it (not from eyeing it).** **Mostly ruined gear, except: 4 gp in a rotten belt pouch and a spike snare in a little backpack.**

### A4. Maggot Training Hall · Moderate 1
▶ [18:27](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1107)

- **Sensory:** **piles of rotting timbers and loose stone throughout a large L-shaped hall; clumps of moss and tangled vines spill down from ceiling gaps.** **Several doors, most barely on their hinges.** **Debris completely blocks the wooden stairwell to a big hole in the ceiling.** **Scars of fire and damage mark the walls.**
- **History:** **the [[Roseguard]] confronted [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] in this vast room; the fight spilled into the servants' lounge (now the A8 sinkhole) and caused some of the keep's collapse.** **DC 15 Recall Knowledge (any skill tied to magic)** confirms **powerful destructive spells did some of the damage centuries ago.**
- **Doors:** **A11's door needs DC 18 Athletics to force.** **A14's door is mostly buried — climb the rubble through a roof hole (DC 10 Athletics).**
- **Secret door (NW):** **DC 20 Perception finds a stone wall section that slides aside** → **a hidden alcove with grooves from determined scraping.** **It held a teleportation circle to C5a; fortune hunters found it centuries ago and gouged out all the silver in the runes — the damage drained the magic and *permanently destroyed the circle*.** **DC 18 Arcana or Occultism (Recall Knowledge) explains the room's purpose and the permanent damage** — **it foreshadows many other circles the heroes can reactivate.**
- **Creatures — three [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] and a giant maggot:** **they once numbered dozens below; the [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlock]] eviction, attrition from Dodge the Squirt and "misjudged vermin empathy" thinned them; now only three remain here.** **[[Boss Skrawng|Skrawng]], after finding the giant maggots (A5) and giant fly (A6), ordered them to train and breed more — once grown, he'll lead the whole gang mounted on giant fly "war steeds" to raid and rule [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]].** **They're training their first maggot, hoping it keeps its training after maturing into a fly (none knows how long that takes).** **On sighting the heroes they send it to attack — "fly war steeds need a taste for people to grow up nice and mean."**

### A5. Maggot Stable · Low 1
▶ [22:09](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1329)

- **Sensory:** **a collapsed wooden staircase in the NE corner winds up to a moss-shrouded hole; the [[Gauntlight#The Fogfen|Fogfen]] is visible through a total collapse in the west wall.** **A horse-sized dead frog lies sprawled in that collapse, back legs bitten off at the hips** — **[[Fresnel Kes]], the river drake of [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B19. Flooded Cavern · Low 2|B19]]** ⚠F9, **attacked it, then found its toxic skin unappetizing after gnawing off the legs.**
- **Creatures — two giant maggots:** **the fly in A6 seeded the carcass; they burst out and attack anything within 10 ft — pursuing relentlessly to the death.**
- **Knowledge:** **DC 18 Arcana or Nature (Recall Knowledge) on the carcass:** **a draconic creature ate the legs; on a critical success, the caustic mucus around the bite marks a *river drake*.**

### A6. Fly Pen · Moderate 1
▶ [23:33](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1413)

- **Sensory:** **a watch post with a high wooden ceiling and a thick mound of filth and animal carcasses on the floor.** **The ceiling is 15 ft and has a trapdoor to a small parapet (DC 18 Perception spots it; the ladder is gone — DC 15 Athletics to climb).**
- **The "captive":** **on [[Boss Skrawng|Skrawng]]'s orders the [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] captured "a cornerstone of their plan to rule [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]" — a pony-sized green-and-blue fly they call *Buzzy Buzz*** ⚠F3. **They think it can't escape; it found the trapdoor at once and stays because the food keeps it satisfied.** **A *second* fly (brown-and-blue) sometimes visits too; they get along, but the mitflits have never seen both at once — so they think it's one fly that changes color.**
- **Creatures:** **the heroes meet the green-and-blue fly first — it lunges, taking them for food; a round later the brown-and-blue fly darts in through the trapdoor.** **Any mitflit who sees both is visibly confused — then horrified when the flies fight to the death.**

### A7. Dining Room · hazard
▶ [25:29](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1529)

- **Sensory:** **a collapsed ceiling leaves a ragged hole in the wooden roof; rotten tapestries, designs hidden under mold, hang in tatters between the arrow slits and ruined side tables; a fancy dining table lies mostly pulverized by the fallen ceiling.**
- **Hazard — Stone Scale Spirits (level 2, complex haunt):** **not every haunt here is [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s; this was the Stone Scale kobolds' communal hall until a violent coup tore them apart, and their spirits linger.** **A half dozen ghostly kobolds rise from the rubble in a howling vortex.** ***GM guidance:* this may be the party's first haunt — present the ghosts as *a spectral phenomenon to be dealt with another way*, not monsters to fight;** **nudge toward Intimidation (scare them off) or Religion (lay them to rest).** **Haunts are puzzles: understanding *why* it's here is how you end it.**
- **Ending it:** **DC 20 Perception finds many tiny bones in the rubble — 30 minutes of work uncovers six kobold skeletons.** **Destroying the bones or removing them all *permanently disables* the haunt.** **A hero trained in Religion (or DC 15 Religion) realizes the link and that this is necessary.**
- **Treasure (with the bones):** **3 gp · 20 sp · a box of components for a caltrop snare · a wolf fang · a vial of black adder venom with "YUM" clumsily scrawled on it.**

### A8. Sinkhole · Moderate 1
▶ [28:04](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1684)

- **Approach:** **roll a secret DC 10 Perception per hero to hear high-pitched squeals and shrieks ([[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] goofing off); anyone specifically listening hears automatically.**
- **Sensory:** **almost the whole ceiling has collapsed, and the floor crumbled into a 10-ft sinkhole of rubble, mud and glistening fungus.** **Burn scars and acid scorch marks decorate the walls and rubble.**
- **History:** **the [[Roseguard]]–[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] battle ended here when she used a last-ditch explosion — the collapse swallowed the group's rogue, *[[Otari Ilvashti|Otari Ilvashi]]*, trapping him below.** **Belcorra fled to the lighthouse (A11).** **As in A4, DC 15 Recall Knowledge (magic skill) confirms a devastating magical battle.**
- **Climbing:** **DC 12 Athletics.** **DC 18 Perception spots a buried spiral staircase down to [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B8. Servants' Rooms · Severe 2|B8]]** (**clearing the rubble takes many days**).
- **Creatures — four Mud Licker mitflits** **supposed to gather mushrooms and grubs, currently wrestling over a bright blue grub: −2 to Perception for initiative unless the heroes announce themselves before attacking.** **They hurl darts from below; one who runs out of darts or takes damage tries to climb out and run to warn [[Boss Skrawng|Skrawng]] in A10.**

### A9. Walkway · no encounter
▶ [30:29](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1829)

- **A 15-ft stone walkway spans a narrow stretch of swamp water, joining the ruined keep and a large stone outbuilding (A10);** **double doors block each end.** **Before the southeast door stands a 7-ft skeleton in armor of gnarled roots, bone and rusted metal, a morning star in one hand, spike-tip resting on the walkway.**
- **The trick:** **it's a statue built by the mud lickers — frightening but harmless.** **DC 12 Perception notices the wooden struts and crude lashings.** **Any damage makes it clatter apart loudly, alerting A10.**
- **Treasure:** **the morning star — a +1 morning star (the [[Mud Lickers|mitflits]] like how it props up the skeleton and don't know it's magic).**

### A10. Mud Licker Throne Room · Severe 1
▶ [31:35](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=1895)

- **Sensory:** **splintered framing shows a thin wall once divided this old barracks; now one large chamber with rubble, swamp vegetation and mud heaped like foul nests.** **A larger heap of rubble, sticks and bones looks *almost* like a throne.** **Before it, a flattened mound of sand with a few dozen bits of wood and stone stuck in it covers the floor.**
- **The sand map:** **DC 10 Society: it's a *crude map of [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]*;** **on a critical success the hero notes arrows marking attack routes from the [[Gauntlight#The Fogfen|Fogfen]].**
- **Creature — [[Boss Skrawng]]** ⚠F1 **with his pet giant solifugid, *Bite-Bite*** ⚠F2 **and mud licker minions:** **always here — berating other [[Mud Lickers]], playing with Bite-Bite, sleeping on the throne, or planning attacks on his sand map.** **If he knows heroes are near, he sits at attention with Bite-Bite beside him.**
  - **Unlike the other mitflits he speaks *Common*, and imperiously demands why the heroes have come to bother him *before he attacked Otari.*** **If he realizes they didn't come to confront him, he backpedals — claiming he has no plans, he's harmless and friendly — and scatters the map with his trident.** **Heroes who lie about their reasons can talk to him and learn what he knows.** **His "negligible bravery and greater impatience" soon start the "assault on Otari" by attacking.**
  - **Tactics:** **orders minions into melee while he hangs back with a blowgun behind the throne.** **At the end of each of his turns the throne shakes dangerously.** **It collapses only if damaged: AC 10, hardness 2, 3 HP.** **If it collapses he's flustered — falls prone and must spend at least one action on each of his next three turns spluttering and cursing in Undercommon.** **He begs for mercy at 5 HP or fewer, but his followers fight to the death while he lives.**

#### What Boss Skrawng Knows
▶ [34:51](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=2091)

- **The dangers in A1–A10, the giant scorpion in A14, and the "swamp dragon" under the boathouse in A16** ⚠F5 (**the river drake**). **The "bogeys" in the keep — the haunts in A7 and the glowing ghosts in A15.** **He never visited the outbuilding on the NW island (A18).**
- **Visitors:** **a boat of "big and sneaky-looking" people came a week ago and went into the swamp dragon's cave while it was out — the dragon returned soon after; he assumes they were eaten.**
- **The light:** **eyes wide, he says the [[Gauntlight]]'s glow is "the creepiest haunting of them all" — it glows every night and he fears a big ghost is up there.**
- **The [[Ghost Queen Morlocks|morlocks]]:** **the Mud Lickers used to live in a dungeon level beneath the keep until "a bunch of mushroom-eyed people" (morlocks) chased them up many nights ago.**
- **Maps:** **a crude map of Level 1 and a partial map of Level 2 — if the heroes Request or Coerce a favor *and* give him something to write with; a bribe of ≥ 2 gp automatically makes him helpful.** **The Level 1 map omits A12–A13, A19–A23, A25 and all secret doors; the Level 2 map shows only the mitflits' old areas — [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B8. Servants' Rooms · Severe 2|B8]]–[[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B14. Shrine of the Ghost Queen · no encounter|B14]] and [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B16. Pantry · no encounter|B16]]–[[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B19. Flooded Cavern · Low 2|B19]] (with a warning about the "swamp dragon" in B19).**
- **Side quest — Skrawng's revenge:** **he hates the mushroom-eyed people and promises a "big pile of shiny gems" if the heroes go downstairs, kill them all, and defeat their king ("a magic priest mushroom-eye guy who can hurt your brain just by looking at you" — [[Graulgust]]).** **He has no gems.** **If they succeed and bring proof, he realizes they're tougher than the morlocks, that he made a terrible mistake promising treasure he doesn't have — and throws himself on their mercy, agreeing to *abandon his plots against Otari forever.***

### A11. Gauntlight Base · Moderate 1
▶ [37:48](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=2268)

- **Doors:** **the south door is stuck — DC 18 Athletics; it falls to sodden pieces.** **The west door has a one-foot barred window and opens normally.**
- **Sensory:** **a circular room, smooth walls painted light gray with no visible seams between the stone blocks.** **Iron stairs wind up the curved walls to a trapdoor in the ceiling nearly 100 ft above.** **A red bloodstain glistens at the center as if a human-sized creature bled to death very recently — with no body.**
- **History:** **the [[Roseguard]] finally defeated [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] here ~500 years ago.** **As she died her spirit was drawn into the Vaults through the [[Gauntlight]], but the bloodstain stays as fresh as the day she died.** **By day the "fresh blood" occasionally ripples like a deep pool — harmless.** **The iron stairs creak and sway alarmingly but are sound — they're part of the Gauntlight and can't be damaged while Belcorra's ghost is active.**
- **Stairs:** **90 ft up to a locked bronze trapdoor into A25.** **Pick it with four successes at DC 25 Thievery, or use the key from A13.**
- **Hazard — Blood of Belcorra (level 3, complex haunt; night only):** **a bloody image of Belcorra arises and emits a soul-draining light that draws blood from living creatures in the room.** **A hero who has seen a depiction of Belcorra elsewhere in the Vaults recognizes her; otherwise DC 20 Society confirms her identity.**

### A12. Belcorra's Office · no encounter
▶ [40:16](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=2416)

- **The ceiling has collapsed in the SW corner; shelves line the walls, but the books and papers and the wooden desk and chair (NE alcove) have rotted away.**
- **Secret door (east wall):** **DC 20 Perception — a rotating shelf is the handle.** **A flight of stairs beyond leads down to [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B6. Abandoned Guard Post · no encounter|B6]].**
- **Treasure:** **searching the desk automatically notes a hidden, partly open drawer holding still-potent elixirs [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] stashed: a lesser comprehension elixir · a minor elixir of life · a lesser skeptic's elixir.**

### A13. Belcorra's Sanctum · no encounter
▶ [41:12](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=2472)

- **A triangular room that might once have been comfortable, now wrecked by rain and weather through two arrow slits in the NE wall; divan, armor and tapestries have decayed.** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] kept it as a private retreat and built a secret entrance to the shrine.**
- **The key ring:** **a rusted ring of keys under the divan — any searching hero finds it.** **Rust has ruined six iron keys; two bronze keys remain:** **one with a *lighthouse* unlocks the trapdoor to the A25 cupola; one with a *book* unlocks Belcorra's private reading room in [[floor-03-cult-of-the-canker#C27. Belcorra's Reading Room · Moderate 3|C27]]** *(→ [Floor 3](floor-03-cult-of-the-canker.md))*.

### A14. Scorpion Kennel · Moderate 1
▶ [42:32](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=2552)

- **Sensory:** **two ceiling collapses: a smaller one destroyed a stair to the ruined upper floor; a larger one mostly buried the south double door, leaving a roof hole partly blocked by cross timbers.** **A second double door to the north has a stone face carved with skulls and caked in moss.**
- **Purpose:** **the [[Mud Lickers]]' first big acquisition — a giant scorpion.** **They lured it in with minimal loss, then collapsed timbers into the rubble to pin it.** **A Medium or smaller creature can clamber through the tangle; unless badly tormented the scorpion won't think to smash out.**
- **The mossy skull:** **the moss on the skull carving looks like ordinary overgrowth, but it belongs to the original design — it's the symbol of *[[Nhimbaloth]]*, Outer God of ghosts, despair, swamps and will-o'-wisps, whom [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] reveres as patron.** **Her lore is rare and hidden: DC 25 Religion (Recall Knowledge) to identify the symbol.** **Fledgling heroes may not recognize it — but [[Wrin Sivinxi]] knows the sinister Outer God and can enlighten them.**
- **Creature — a giant scorpion:** **well fed and content, it attacks anyone else at once.** **It crosses the rubble without difficult terrain and *can* climb the south rubble and force a path (DC 18 Athletics) — but is unlikely to unless harried from the other side.** **A particularly dangerous foe for first-level characters:** ***encourage a retreat if they struggle, and have the scorpion fall back rather than chase.***

### A15. Nhimbaloth Shrine · Severe 1
▶ [44:51](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=2691)

- **Sensory:** **the air feels colder and damper than elsewhere; condensation beads and runs in rivulets across countless carved skulls.** **A moldering human corpse slumps against a wall, its hand wedged in a crack "as if he died trying to pry a brick loose."** **Three short flights of steps rise to a wider chamber north, where a semicircular dais holds an altar of white stone; beyond it loom four 20-ft stained glass windows showing ghosts rising from overgrown graveyards toward a four-pointed orange star.**
- **Purpose:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] made no secret of her faith — a shrine both for worship and as an intimidating setting to meet visitors.** **It feels as though something ancient and distant is watching through the glass.** **DC 25 Religion (Recall Knowledge) identifies a shrine to [[Nhimbaloth]].**
- **The thief:** **the corpse lies by the secret door to A13; he snuck in months ago, was outclassed by the corpse lights, and died just before reopening it.** **He left the door slightly ajar with the rotating skull still turned — easy to spot.**
- **Creatures — two long-dead acolytes of Nhimbaloth** (mossy skeletons sprawled along the east edge of the north chamber, out of sight from the south), **each harboring *corpse lights*.** **They rise as the heroes approach the north chamber; fight until destroyed but don't pursue.** **The first corpse light reduced to wisp form animates the dead thief at the secret door if he's still there — *or, in a darker turn, a dead hero.***
- **Treasure:** **the thief: a +1 rapier · four daggers · a silver ring (5 gp) · a minor healing potion · a lesser darkvision elixir.** **The acolytes: little, except one has a gold tooth (4 gp) and one wears a channel protection amulet.** **DC 18 Perception on the altar finds a secret panel in its north side — a shallow niche holding a scroll of *restore senses* and a 1st-level wand of *heal*.**

### A19. Spookywisp · Trivial 1
▶ [51:43](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=3103)

- **Door:** **the exterior door is stuck — DC 15 Athletics.** **A study with several bookshelves, a desk and two chairs; books and objects in disarray, torn pages everywhere.** **[[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]] studied and took lessons from [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] here.**
- **Creature — [[Tangletop]],** **a puckish brownie whose blonde hair is always frizzy** (**and who fancies himself "something of a spooky sort of fae"**). **When he notices the heroes (e.g. the door isn't opening right away) he hides under the desk and uses *ventriloquism* to boom that he's *"Spookywisp,"* a dangerous ghost; he uses *dancing lights* to fake a will-o'-wisp and throws his voice from the light.**
  - **Backstory:** **arrived a few days ago, tore through the room's contents (nothing he liked), then went to A23 — where the shiny spyglass entranced him until the soulbound doll chased him off.** **He came back here to plot how to get it.**
  - **If attacked:** **he shrieks and flees toward A23, hoping the heroes destroy the doll — then he tries to steal the spyglass and leave forever.**
- **Side quest — the shiny:**
  - **He wants "the shiny" in the SW room; warns that a "mean beak-faced doll" will stop them; promises *three secrets* if they bring it.** **Asked to describe it: *"it's shiny, you'll know when you see it"* — pressed, *"a sparkling glowing tube that I want."*** **It's the Bejeweled Spyglass in A23.**

    1. **This building belonged to someone named *Volluk Azrinae*.**
    2. **Volluk was apprentice to someone named *Belcorra* — and even though Volluk seemed mean and dangerous, Belcorra seemed much worse.**
    3. **The "spooky magic lighthouse" can do more than shine eerily — *it can shoot a beam full of ghosts.*** *(His reading of the A22 lighthouse painting — but "not far from the truth.")*

### A23. Lens Workshop · Moderate 1
▶ [57:56](https://youtu.be/irDpBXAPlMw?t=3476)

- **Sensory:** **a once elegant workshop in shambles — soggy strings of dripping moss hang from cracks in the wooden domed roof; below, a carpet of broken glass glitters dangerously.** **Benches, shelves, armatures and displays used to craft and repair immense lighthouse lenses lie in ruins; only a Bejeweled Spyglass on a display rack near the south wall survived.**
- **History:** **[[Volluk Azrinae|Volluk]] wrecked it after [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s death — he'd hidden in the swamp waiting for the [[Roseguard]] to leave; on returning he was so guilty over his cowardice that he destroyed the workshop in a fit of fury.**
- **Terrain:** **broken glass makes uneven ground — Acrobatics DC 11; a creature that falls prone takes 1 piercing damage.**
- **Creature — [[Mr. Beak]]** (raven-headed, raven-winged **soulbound doll**): **Volluk's favorite construct — not the most powerful or helpful, but it holds the soul of Volluk's devoted servant, the goblin *[[Borbo]]* (a fact that ensures its place at Volluk's side).** **It remains here, abandoned, "waiting forever for a creator who moved on … in an entirely new body."** **When the heroes enter it stands up from a slumped heap and asks in a warbling voice: *"Is my master Azrinae coming back?"*** **Whatever they answer, it realizes none is its creator, leaps into the air on flapping wings, and attacks in frustrated fury — fights until destroyed.** **A unique variant of soulbound doll with ungainly wings it can nonetheless fly with — easily avoiding the uneven ground.**
- **Treasure:** **the Bejeweled Spyglass — Volluk's finest creation apart from the [[Gauntlight]]'s lenses (even in self-loathing rage he couldn't smash it); it's the "shiny" [[Tangletop]] wants; worth 20 gp if kept.** **Mr. Beak's *soul focus gem* (in its back between the wings): 5 gp — but more valuable as a source of information if placed in the partly built doll in [[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B2. Volluk's Workshop · no encounter|B2]] ([Floor 2](floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon.md)).**
