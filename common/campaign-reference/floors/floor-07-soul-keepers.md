## Chapter 7 Background (GM-only)
▶ [2:55](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=175)

**The contract.** **Five centuries ago** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] summoned the **contract devil [[Urevian]]** to administer her Abomination Vaults. Always looking to sweeten a deal, Urevian **offered to train her armies and lead her invasion of [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] — for a price.**

- **The price:** he knew **[[Vol Rajani]]** — legendary [[Roseguard]] swordswoman, **exiled Nidal royalty** — lived in Absalom. The **Rajani family has, for reasons even Urevian doesn't know, special value to certain parties in Hell.** In exchange for his aid Belcorra **guaranteed him Vol's soul.**
- **What went wrong:** when the Roseguard attacked [[Gauntlight]] — **bringing Vol's soul right to Belcorra's doorstep** — Urevian was **in Hell, committing Vol's soul to a much more powerful entity he didn't yet possess.** By the time he returned, **Belcorra was dead** and couldn't deliver. Urevian was **trapped** preparing a surface invasion that would **gain him nothing.** Worse: he **didn't dare return to Hell without the soul**, and his agreement **forbids him from capturing it directly or through infernal proxies** — Belcorra was meant to deliver it.
- **Now:** Belcorra is back, so the agreement **can** be fulfilled. He plans to **train her armies and invade the surface, starting with [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]** — where his spying tells him **[[Carman Rajani]]** (whose soul also satisfies the contract) lives.

**Urevian's situation.**

- He has **fully mobilized his devils** to train the fleshwarps that came to his realm.
- **Belcorra put several horrible monsters in stasis (G17)**; he doesn't yet know how to free them reliably — **that's his next priority.**
- **Getting an army through:** devils can *dimension door* to the surface, but the rest of his troops need **physical access**, so he asked **[[Jafaki]]** to let them through the labs. Jafaki suspected a diabolic ruse (reasonable, given the history) and agreed on **one condition: the seugathi personally inspect all infernal forces and make "upgrades and improvements."** Urevian knew that was a ploy for **endless research subjects** and that Jafaki would **never sign off on any troops.**
- **He'd rather quit.** Urevian is **ready to wash his hands of the whole arrangement — but he has a contract.**
- **The heroes are his solution.** If they **slaughter their way through Jafaki and the seugathi, all the better.** He's ordered his devils to **kill all but one or two of them and bring him the survivors for negotiation** — he plans to **convince the survivors to deliver Carman Rajani.** Otherwise he'll **march through the dungeon and destroy Otari.**
- **The lower levels:** little contact even in better times; he **receives food from the farm levels** to feed his mortal soldiers but **cares nothing for the denizens or their machinations.** Since Belcorra's return he's seen **more urdefhan, drow and others parading through** and has **had enough** — he **blocked the stairs down with rubble (G1)** and **put a magical barrier across the big elevator opening (G3).** He knows **that gives him leverage over the heroes** — **he needs them as his hands on the surface.**
- **He's been spying on Carman**, so **he knows a little about the heroes** (see *Environmental Cues*).

- **Sensory:** the spiral staircase bottoms out on a **circular chamber with a red stone floor.** **Four wide passages** lead off in opposite directions — **three end at a door.** The **west passage has the most impressive door: 15 ft high, inscribed with a blazing pentagram** (→ **G10**).
- **The rubble:** the **southwest stairway** is **choked with boulders, rubble and dust** ⚠F4. It leads to **[[floor-08-decaying-gardens#H1. Overgrown Stairs · no encounter|H1]], 100 ft down.** **Clearing it takes at least a week of labor**, and the level's denizens **won't give the heroes that time** — the rubble **can't be effectively cleared while devils and other hostile creatures remain.** **[[Urevian]] will clear it if the heroes negotiate with him (G10).**
- **Doors:** the room gives **easy access to the cell blocks**; **most doors are unlocked.** **Exceptions:** the **secret door to G2** (hidden *and* locked) and the **pentagram doors to G10** (magically sealed).
- **Guards:** none here — but **the barbazu drilling grothluts in G3 (north) likely sees anyone entering**, and **exhorts his soldiers to attack.**
- **Connections:** up → **[[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F1. Central Shaft · Low 6|F1]]** · down → **H1** (rubble) · G3 (north).

### G2. Level 7 Portal Chamber · no encounter
▶ [9:18](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=558)

- **Secret door:** **DC 25 Perception.** **Pressing a particular stone above the door** rotates it inward **an inch or so**, revealing a **combination lock of metal cylinders.** The **combination is lost.**
  - **Open it:** **DC 25 Athletics** (force) **or four successes at DC 25 Thievery** (pick).
  - **A Master in Thievery** recognizes the **tumblers as the weak point** and opens it with **a single DC 20 Thievery.**
- **Sensory:** the **floor gleams under a thin layer of dust**; **runes filled with silver inlay** are carved into the walls.
- **Function:** an **inert teleportation circle** like the others in the Vaults (first met at **[[floor-02-the-forgotten-dungeon#B17. Level Two Portal Chamber (Teleport Circle Summary) · no encounter|B17]]**); it must be **reactivated with the *awaken portal* ritual.**
- **XP: 30** for activating it.

### G3. Drill Field · Moderate 7
▶ [10:33](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=633)

- **Sensory:** a **well-worn floor** (constant use); walls **angle up to a 20-ft ceiling.** The **north wall has a mine-shaft elevator, blocked by a wall of shimmering orange energy.** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] **greeted guests from the Darklands here** before leading them upstairs for entertainment and refreshments; its **opulence has faded** under **centuries of drills and sparring.** Walls are **splattered with blood and ichor.**
- **The barrier:** [[Urevian]] **called in favors from infernal contracts** to make a **permanent glowing wall of force** that seals the elevator shaft and sheds dim light. **He has no personal control over it — it's tied to the essence of his contract.** It **vanishes immediately only if his contract with Belcorra is fulfilled, or if he's slain** (details in **G10**).
- **The elevator:** descends **100 ft to [[floor-08-decaying-gardens#H17. Dock and Lift · Low 8|H17]]**; it's currently **in the lower position.**
- **Creatures — a barbazu drilling six grothluts:** [[Jafaki]] barely notices the grothluts, **assuming the devils killed them all.** In truth Urevian's forces **trained them to fight *against* their creators.** Grothluts are mindless, so training is **exceptionally slow — but the devils have time.** The barbazu **uses magical bracers to enforce compliance**, and the grothluts **obey.** The devil is **immune to the grothluts' moaning** ("pious moaning" ⚠F17). They **attack as soon as they see intruders** — **likely when the heroes arrive in G1.**

### G5. Hellforge · Moderate 7
▶ [14:05](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=845)

- **Sensory:** **charred black walls**; **chains** hang from the **20-ft ceiling**, suspending **shelves of tools and metal ingots.** The **forge fills an alcove in the north wall — its mouth a beast's, filled with jagged teeth, a fire roaring inside** — with a **smoke hole between its horns** and **smoke belching from its nostrils.** An **anvil** sits near it; a **trough of foul water** against the west wall; a **weapon rack** flanks the **south double doors.** The tiny vent shaft rises **hundreds of feet** into a **large empty chamber in the earth, where the smoke settles.**
- **History:** once a **torture chamber**; [[Urevian]] **repurposed it to make weapons for the assault.**
- **Creatures:** Urevian assigned **forge duty to an erinyes, Barkumuk** ⚠F1 — a **skilled smith who bristles at making mundane weapons.** He takes more pride in the **long, laborious craft of hellforged weapons**, so **output over the centuries has been modest.** Also **a barbazu.**
  - The devils know the heroes **must have beaten Azvalviganer and her bodyguards to get here** and are **openly grateful for the "exacting" zebub's demise** — **they attack anyway.**
  - **Barkumuk fights with fiery forge hammers**; he has the **Flames of Fury** ability common to erinyes, so **any weapon he holds gains a *flaming* rune.** The barbazu wields a **hellforged glaive** (**+1 flaming striking hellforged glaive**) — **damage well above a typical barbazu's.** **Both fight to the death.**
- **Destroying the hellforge:** the heroes **lack the skill to use it as anything but an ordinary forge**, but they can destroy it. **DC 22 Crafting or Religion (Recall Knowledge)** remembers that **a little holy water on the infernal forge destroys it**; **on a critical success** they also remember that **it releases a blast of energy.** Splashing it with holy water **cracks it down the middle with a mighty groan:** **4d6 sonic + 4d6 evil damage, DC 23 basic Fortitude** to **creatures in the room** ⚠F17.
- **Treasure:** special metals Barkumuk hasn't forged, **stacked by the anvil:** **5 cold iron chunks (10 gp each) · 2 silver chunks (10 gp each) · 1 adamantine chunk (500 gp).**
- **XP: 30** for destroying the hellforge.

### G6. Guard Chamber · Moderate 7
▶ [17:12](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=1032)

- **Sensory:** a **plain stone chamber**, **double doors north and south**, and **tiny carvings covering the east and west walls.** The guardian made them to **pass the time**; each is a **nonsense metaphysical infernal question**, such as ***"Who are you if you are no one?"*** and ***"When your soul is consumed, do you consume yourself?"*** — the creature poses these **during interrogations** so victims **puzzle over them in search of deeper meaning and feel foolish when they find none.**
- **Creature — Eagan** ⚠F2, a **bone devil**, stands watch **at [[Urevian]]'s command**, **unhappy about being a mere guard** but **bound by the contract terms** of their service.
  - Eagan takes their charge **very literally:** they **never warn the erinyes in the summoning chamber (G7)** of trouble and **won't leave the room no matter what they hear** — unless **personally attacked.**
  - **If given a chance to talk**, Eagan **freely explains their role and offers some "nonsense meditations."** They use the **time the heroes spend contemplating to ask questions with their "stigan" Inquisitor ability** ⚠F8. Eagan **quickly loses patience** and **insists the heroes leave or risk their wrath.** In combat: **fights to the death.**

### G7. Summoning Chamber · Severe 7
▶ [18:48](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=1128)

- **Sensory:** a **stairway at the south end leads north to a platform overlooking a cylindrical chamber.** **Half-melted candles** cover a **stone altar** at the platform's edge. **Water drips from a crack in the ceiling** onto the platform, **evaporating rapidly in the stifling heat.** **Six heavy chains** hold a **wide metal disc** over a **magma pool** that lights the chamber orange; a **fiery pentagram** is visible atop the disc.
- **History:** [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] had **geomancers divert a natural magma flow** to pool here, then commissioned **a ritual altar and the summoning circle atop the disc.** She **summoned several fiends here and bound each to her service — the greatest was [[Urevian]]**, whose contract gave her **access to every devil who serves him.**
- **Dimensions:** ceiling **20 ft at the doors**, **25 ft above the platform.** The **disc is level with the platform but a 15-ft gap over the magma** makes it daunting to reach.
- **Hazards:**
  - **Heat:** the air is **magically cooled only to "severe heat"** — **1d6 fire damage every round.**
  - **The magma pool (10 ft deep):** immersion deals **20d6 fire damage per round.**
- **Knowledge:**
  - **DC 23 Crafting or Religion:** the **magma's heat powers the summoning circle.**
  - **DC 23 Nature** (or a related Lore such as mining lore): the **ceiling crack lets water seep from the underground lake directly above ([[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F4. Deep Pool · Moderate 6|F4]]).**
  - **Widening the crack** (proper tools, a way to reach the ceiling, **several hours of chipping**) turns the **drip into a trickle, then a flood** — **steam fills the room and the magma cools to stone over a few hours.** The heroes also know **cooling the magma destroys the summoning circle.**
- **Creatures — twin erinyes sisters** ("gate forgers"), **trying to convert the summoning circle into a permanent gate to Hell.** They're **more concerned with the theory of extraplanar portals than the application** — lots of discussion, little action. **Furious at interruption**, they most likely **attack.** **But** if they think a hero **shares their interest** — e.g. **they see a hero summon a creature into a fight** — they **offer to stop fighting and discuss the finer points of extraplanar law.** **When they've learned all they think they can, they try to murder the rival scholar.** *(One holds a **pendant fragment** — G10.)*

- **The door:** a **note over a small sliding grate at eye level**, written by **the imp [[Szek]]** in **Common, Undercommon and Infernal:** ***"Do not disturb if you value your limbs."***

  - **If disturbed**, she **mistakes the heroes for more lackeys** and reacts with **sudden, startling violence.** She **lets foes who flee retreat** but otherwise **fights to the death.**
  - *(G9: this gug **wrecked the high catwalk** (see G9) when Urevian **sent a barbazu with a summary report instead of meeting her.**)*

### G10. Urevian's Domain · Severe 7
▶ [27:58](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=1678)

- **The doors:** **huge, magically sealed, emblazoned with an enormous glowing pentagram.** The center has **a circular, hand-sized indentation with five deeper indentations** — the heroes can tell **an amulet-like device fits here.**
- **The pendant:** **[[Urevian]]'s pendant opens the door**, but it's **split into five fragments held by his lieutenants.**
  - **A devil** who casts *dimension door* **while touching even one fragment** can **teleport to the other side.**
  - **Anyone else** must **reassemble all five fragments and touch the pendant to the indentation.** **Once done, the door effectively stays open for good.**
  - Urevian could **enact a year-long ritual to recreate the door and pendant** — but **likely won't.**
  - **The loophole (Urevian doesn't know):** a clever user can open the door with **only four fragments** by touching them to the door and **succeeding at DC 25 Religion** to **mimic the unholy energies of the missing fragment.** **Failure:** **4d12 fire damage** and **stupefied 1 for 1 hour.** **Critical failure:** **8d12 fire damage** and **stupefied 3 for 1 hour.**
  - **The five fragments are in: [[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F5. Deep End · Moderate 6|F5]] · G4 · G5 · G7 · G18.** *(F5 = slag gagon; G4 = Azvalviganer; G5 = Barkumuk; G7 = the erinyes sisters; G18 = [[Ysondkhelir]].)*
- **Sensory:** a **large natural-stone chamber running north–south**, **an enormous stone double door as its only exit.** **Lit braziers** throw **strange shadows** and bathe the room in a **sinister light.** **Bookshelves, a sturdy desk, neat rolled scrolls and stacks of parchment** fill an **alcove to the north.**
- **History:** Urevian made this **his home, sealed off from the rest of the dungeon so he can work in peace.** He **fashioned the door for himself**, but **made [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] a magical pendant so she could bypass it.** **Shortly before her death he had the imp [[Szek]] steal the pendant back**, then **broke it into five and gave one to each lieutenant.** His trusted devils **come and go freely** but **know not to bother him except on official business.**
- **The desk:** **reports, inventories and maps** of **the surface world, the [[Gauntlight]], and the surrounding Darklands.** Of particular note: **complete maps of Levels 5, 6 and 7 — including all secret doors.** Also **copies of several infernal contracts** (originals are in **Hell's vaults**). A hero who investigates and **succeeds at DC 23 Society or Legal Lore** realizes **Urevian has kept tabs on [[Vol Rajani]]'s line for generations.**
- **Creatures:** **Urevian**, who **spends most of his time revising attack plans against every contingency**, keeps **two barbazu bodyguards** nearby **to intercept lieutenants' reports and keep him undisturbed.** When the heroes open the doors **the barbazus spring forward — but Urevian stops them and warmly greets the heroes.**

#### Urevian's Offer
▶ [31:44](https://youtu.be/xEAvSfoq-D4?t=1904)

*A ruggedly handsome devil steps forward; a long roll of parchment hanging from his horns curls up, then unrolls again.*

> *"Friends! Friends — weapons away! Truly, I am happy to see you."* (to his bodyguards, glaives raised:) *"Stand down, you two. I doubt they are arrogant enough to fight us."* *"I am Urevian, the ⚠F11 contract agent of Belcorra — but I assure you I am no enemy of yours. You seek to vanquish her, to put an end to her murderous spirit, the threat she poses to all mortal life, et cetera, et cetera. Then perhaps we can broker a deal — one that greatly benefits us both, with a minimal number of precious lives lost."*

*If the heroes already met Szek and struck a deal with her, Urevian doesn't mind:* ***"You've already struck a bargain with Szek, so I know it is not a matter of if but of what price. Trust me when I say I'm offering you an excellent deal."***

**His answers to the likely questions (cleaned):**

| The heroes ask… | Urevian answers… |
|---|---|
| **Who are you?** | "I am Urevian, a contract devil of some renown, if I do say so myself." |
| **What do you want from us?** | "Simply put, you are the **third-party agents** I need to get out of this dreadful place and return home. There is something I want, but **I'm not allowed to go and get it myself, nor can I employ any infernal agents to retrieve it.** The hands of **a mortal foe of Belcorra** must deliver me my prize. My devils are valueless in this regard — as are my fleshwarped soldiers." |
| **Why a mortal foe of Belcorra?** | "Ah — that is the **delight of legalistic contract interpretation.** It cannot be me, any of my minions, or any **ally** of Belcorra.
