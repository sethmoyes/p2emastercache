## Background (GM-only)
▶ [2:26](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=146)

**The Hunting Grounds.** *"The farther you descend, the wilder and stranger things become."* As [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] dug deeper she found **an extensive system of caverns — truly wild, unlike the self-contained farm level.** She **fought the fierce denizens and tried to seal the passages to deeper places, but it was futile.** She named it **the Hunting Grounds** and **left it mostly alone, save for building alliances.** She built **only a few strongholds: a lodge, a vault, and the entrance to the Gate of [[Nhimbaloth]]** (the subterranean temple that holds the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]] and the source of the [[Gauntlight]]'s power).

### The Urdefhan Cult
▶ [3:37](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=217)

**Urdefhan are vampire-like humanoids who largely pursue violence and slaughter** — brutal killers **obsessed with ending life and existence itself.** Those who regularly **raid the caverns and lakes from their caves** were **exactly the soldiers [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] needed.** She made **a calculated, overwhelming attack on all of them**, and **when the sorcerer called for a parley, they listened.** She **swore the urdefhan would be the vanguard of her surface assault** and **gave the Emerald [[Fulcrum Lenses|fulcrum lens]] to the war champion as a token of favor.** **Generations of war champions have passed it down as their symbol of leadership.**

### Drow Allegiance
▶ [4:29](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=269)

With the deep gnomes cowed and the urdefhan awed, [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] turned to **the drow of the Hunting Grounds.** From their **secure stronghold, [[Yldaris]]**, they **ranged the caverns "like deadly apparitions," retreating in the face of significant danger.** She **captured and imprisoned more and more of their hunters until Yldaris's leader agreed to meet her.** **She traded the captives for a promise:** the drow would **keep the Ochre [[Fulcrum Lenses|fulcrum lens]] safe and aid her attack on the surface.**

### Belcorra's Return
▶ [5:09](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=309)

When she arose as a ghost, **she visited the Hunting Grounds first.** She **checked her treasure vault**, then **sent its grave-knight guardian ([[Caliddo Haruvex|Caliddo]]) to fetch a valuable book she'd left on an upper level.** She then **visited the urdefhan, who still told vague legends of the quasi-mythical sorcerer.** Their **new war champion, [[Khurfel]], carries the [[Fulcrum Lenses|Emerald lens]] and agreed to follow their ghost queen to raze the surface world.** **The urdefhan have stepped up their summoning of daemon allies for the coming assault.** **Her visit to [[Yldaris]] went less well:** **[[Quara Orshendiel]]** — **granddaughter of the leader who promised service** — **had little idea who [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] was.** Belcorra accepted **Quara's hastily given, not entirely genuine renewal of her grandfather's promise.**

### Hunting Grounds Features
▶ [6:11](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=371)

- **Light:** the caverns are **dark**, though **glowing fungus lights enough areas to convey their great size.**
- **Ceilings:** **large caverns 40 ft · smaller caverns 20 ft · worked humanoid areas ([[Yldaris]], the caligni lodge, the Gate of [[Nhimbaloth]]) 12 ft.**
- **Doors:** mostly **stone or sturdy fungal wood**; **entrances to inhabited areas are often thicker or reinforced.**

## The Yldaris Outpost (I1–I20)

### I1. Guarded Intersection · Trivial 9
▶ [6:50](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=410)

- **Setup:** the passage **up to the farm level (from [[floor-08-decaying-gardens#H41. Descent to Yldaris · Severe 8|H41]])** is fairly secure, but the cautious drow **keep a guard anyway.**
- **Creature — a surly drow hunter, Rikizlia** (captions "Rekislia"; repo: female, *Rikizlia Azrinae*), **sitting on a stool whittling fungus wood.** **If the heroes arrive without an escort she races to I2 for help.** **If drow accompany them she watches with open curiosity and keeps whittling.**

### I2. Caravan Staging · Moderate 9
▶ [7:28](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=448)

- **Sensory:** **bags, barrels and crates line the walls between the cavern's three exits.** **Caravans between [[Yldaris]] and Lozardyn load and unload here** (erratic schedule; usually a few drow linger). The **east passage leads to twisting Darklands caverns.**
- **Creatures:** **six drow unloading a large harnessed pack lizard.** **If the heroes are under escort or have Yldaris's trust, the drow neither confront nor question them; otherwise they mobilize.** **A warden needs 2 Interact actions to unhitch a riding lizard and fight beside it.** **When combat starts a hunter runs to I3 to rally that room.** **Badly outnumbered or injured drow flee east into the caverns and stay away for days.**
- **Treasure:** **trade goods — spices, dried fungus, cloth (all uncommon on the surface): 18 Bulk, 100 gp total.**

### I3. Stable · Moderate 9
▶ [8:51](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=531)

- **Pens for half a dozen giant lizards**; a **jumbled pile of harnesses** by the corrals. **Three drow wardens** guard and feed [[Yldaris]]'s riding lizards. **The lizards can climb out and along the walls but don't unless directed to fight.**

### I4. Secret Tunnel · no encounter
▶ [9:16](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=556)

- **Sensory:** the tunnel **seems to end in rock to the south**, but the **rockfall hides a secret door the hunters use to come and go.**
- **Finding it:** **DC 26 Perception** — **or automatically if detecting magic or an *expert in Survival*** (the door is magical and **subtle tracks lead right to it**).
- **Opening it:** the rubble **swings aside when someone speaking *any words in Elven* touches an invisible magical wall panel.** **Magically reinforced — can't be broken through.**

### I5. Yldaris Entry · Trivial 9
▶ [10:02](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=602)

- **The west door:** **iron sheets outside**, **a slot at drow eye level covered by a sliding plate inside**, **no keyhole or handle**, **heavy bars.** **Get in by bursting through (hardness 15, 60 HP) or giving the secret knock all outpost drow know** (it changes occasionally) **and waiting for a guard.** The **east door (into [[Yldaris]])** is built the same way but **normally open and unbarred.**
- **Creature — a drow warden, Taklitur** (captions "Taklitar"), **stands guard inside, ready to admit anyone giving the right knock.** **A wrong knock:** he first **ignores it, assuming another drow misremembers.** **If it persists or he realizes someone is breaking in, he opens the slot, shoots at whoever's outside, shuts it, and raises the alarm.**

### I6. Watch Chamber · Moderate 9
▶ [11:10](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=670)

- **Sensory:** **table and chairs in the NE corner**; **walls carved in a flowing script underscored with harsh marks** — **drow aphorisms in Elven about staying alert and protecting one's home; the marks record each time the drow repelled invaders.** **Four doors; the west door looks sturdier.**
- **Creatures:** **a drow shootist, Yrorix** (captions "Yorix"), **and two hunters, playing cards with a drow Harrow deck.** In an assault they **knock on the three inner doors to alert defenders but make their stand here.**
  - **If the heroes are [[Yldaris]]'s allies** the drow **cheerfully invite them to play** (low stakes; **the drow cheat relentlessly**).
  - **If the heroes rescued [[Falxi Orshendiel]] ([[floor-06-experiments-in-flesh#F12. Kitchen · no encounter|F12]]), he's here gambling** — **well-regarded as a distant cousin of Yldaris's leader, but fundamentally lazy**; he **greets them warmly and vouches for them: a peaceful meeting.**
  - **Weapons rule:** if drow led the heroes to meet [[Quara Orshendiel|Quara]], they stop here and **ask the heroes to leave their weapons for the meeting** — **Falxi (or Yrorix) promises to guard the gear**; **armed strangers may not see the leader.** **They don't negotiate**; if the heroes balk, **the drow usher them out and invite them back.**
- **Treasure:** **well-worn Harrow deck with unusual drow iconography (15 gp)** · **wagers worth 24 sp.**

### Befriending the Drow
▶ [22:25](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=1345)

*If the heroes dealt peacefully with the drow and earned an audience with [[Quara Orshendiel|Quara]]:*

- **The wait:** wary wardens **escort them to I17 and ask them to wait.** Staff **bring chairs from I15 and food and fungus wine from I16.** The wardens are **curious and talkative** (little experience with surface dwellers) but **avoid anything that sounds like an interrogation — that's Quara's prerogative.** **If the heroes are reticent or rude, the drow wait in silence; if they attack, the wardens call for backup.**
- **Quara arrives** *"radiating calm command and curiosity,"* with **a stern Salaisa Malthulas (commander of the wardens)** and **the strange twin drow seers, Bhazrade and Klathor** (captions "Bazrad and Calthor"; called "cavaliers" once ⚠F10), who **hang back quietly.** **Quara does the talking:**

> *"You have the look of surface dwellers. I've never met any of your kind, but I've seen artwork. Strange — you are not nearly as ugly as our art makes you out to be. I am Quara Orshendiel, leader of this outpost. Let's figure out whether or not we can help one another."*

- **If they rescued [[Falxi Orshendiel|Falxi]]**, Quara **thanks them for rescuing her cousin "from one of the scrapes he keeps getting himself into."** **She's sincere** — **the seer foretold that surface dwellers would come as potential allies, and she wants to believe it.**
- **The conversation:** she **invites their story**, **sympathizes with hardships, laughs at the funny parts, asks questions.** **If they mention or show a [[Fulcrum Lenses|fulcrum lens]] she takes obvious interest.** **She overlooks petty slights but calmly chastises overt aggression *once*; overly obnoxious heroes are asked to leave and not return.** She ends: *"You've given me something to think about. Thank you."*
- **She invites them to the overflow lodgings (I13)** while she decides — **a few hours** — then **gambles on an alliance** and **calls them back to I17 (a few hours later, or the next time they return to [[Yldaris]]).** The twins **lurk at the edges as before.**

> *"I believe you've been truthful with me, so I'm going to be truthful with you. **Yldaris only exists at [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s pleasure.** It was here when she first created her vast dungeons. At the time, my grandfather was its leader. Belcorra made allies of several groups — **drow, urdefhan to the west, and deep gnomes above who have embraced undeath.** To each she gave **a large magical lens with strange powers** for safekeeping, I believe. **Ours was the ochre fulcrum lens, and it could manipulate emotions.** But there was more to the lens. My grandfather told my father, **Larathol**, who became Yldaris's leader after him — but my father never told me."*
>
> *(Quara sighs.)* *"I was never meant to lead. **My older sister was to succeed my father.** They both embarked on a **secret expedition a few years ago on an island in the great lake. The urdefhan warlord [[Khurfel]] caught and killed them.** The expedition had something to do with the lens, I believe, and I suspect my father had it with him. Perhaps he hid it someplace. Perhaps Khurfel took it. I don't know. **Belcorra came to me as a ghost shortly thereafter.** She insisted that I swear Yldaris's support and aid in a surface invasion that drives her even in death. For the good of Yldaris, **I renewed the vows my grandfather made — but I've never felt bound by mere words. I won't help her. I'll help you.** You're welcome in Yldaris as long as you'd like."*

*The heroes can ask more, but she knows little else. Before it winds down, **the twins speak in unison** — hard to tell which is speaking:*

> **First twin:** *"There is more."* **Second:** *"You should know."* *(Quara motions them on.)* **First:** *"The ochre lens lies in a dark and wet place. Khurfel didn't claim it. **Larathol dropped it in the water to deny it to his enemy.** He died attempting to learn more of its purpose — more than the partial truth we know."* **Other:** *"We have seen in a vision that **the stones both empower the ghost queen and hold the key to her demise.** They are slivers of something ancient, alien, evil, and hungry."* **Both:** *"We saw **eyes of [[Nhimbaloth|Empty Death]].**"* *"She separated the lenses because together they draw the baleful attention of the Empty Death. **Even the ghost queen cannot withstand the Empty Death when their powers are joined within her.** **The secrets to the lenses are in the old treasure vault which overlooks the lake.** Find the lenses — and the answers Larathol sought."*

*Quara:* *"I would say you get used to Bhazrade and Klathor, but one never does. Let me assure you that when they do speak, it's important. This is all news to me, but their words benefit Yldaris — and you as well."*

#### Consulting *The Whispering Reeds*
▶ [28:58](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=1738)

If the heroes recovered **the book of Nhimbaloth's lore from higher in the Vaults**, a hero who consults it finds **references to the fulcrum lenses — no Recall Knowledge check, and no suffering from the book's curse.** It says: **slivers of Nhimbaloth's essence exist in the lenses, perhaps as a way to contact the Outer God**; **the lenses have different and powerful (unspecified) abilities**; **they're dangerous in close proximity — even Nhimbaloth's worshipers fear bringing more than two or three together**; and **using their powers draws Nhimbaloth's scrutiny and invites destruction.**

▶ [29:44](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=1784)

- **XP: 120** for **allying with Quara and the Yldaris drow.**

- **History:** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] built this secure vault to hold the treasure she planned to fund her assault on [[locations/absalom|Absalom]].** **The door looks formidable but is unlocked** — **the vault's only intelligent occupant, the grave knight [[Caliddo Haruvex|Caliddo]], went to the Vaults' third level for books Belcorra wanted and forgot to lock it on his return.**

### I56. Caliddo's Vigil · Low 9
▶ [1:17:47](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=4667)

- **A small bare room with more vault doors (I57, I58)** — **[[Caliddo Haruvex|Caliddo]] carries these two keys too.** **A remarkably well-hidden secret door: DC 30 Perception finds the hidden switch.**
- **Creature — Caliddo Haruvex** (captions "Kalido Haravex"), **a grave knight in archaic armor clutching a greatsword.** **A skilled mercenary who came to serve [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] on hearing how powerful his distant relative had become — she accepted his offer, then promptly killed him and raised him as a grave knight eternally bound to her.** **He proved loyal many times, so she made him vault guardian; he kept a silent vigil for centuries.** **When Belcorra returned she sent him to fetch books from others who might use them against her** — **his first departure in centuries. He recovered the texts from the upper level and locked them in I58.** **He remains at his post and assaults anyone who enters** (**if the heroes didn't meet him with the golem in I53, he's here**). **Fights until destroyed; won't leave the vault unless Belcorra expressly orders it.**
- **Caliddo's Return:** **destroying him for good is hard — his armor rebuilds him.** **If he rejuvenates after the heroes plunder the vault he pursues them to recover the items** — **though he has no special tie to the treasure, so he may search a long time.** **He won't negotiate, but most wants his keys and the books back (the last things Belcorra showed interest in — he assumes they're the most valuable).** **Heroes who return the keys and books might avert his retribution and keep the rest.**

▶ [1:20:18](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=4818)

- **Treasure:** **a standard-grade adamantine warhammer · a +2 striking battle axe · a +2 resilient chain shirt** (tangled with the fallen armor stand that once displayed it).

### I58. Vault of Secrets · no encounter
▶ [1:20:59](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=4859)

- **Sensory:** **shelves of old books and odds and ends.** **In the center, a dusty mechanism of copper and silver — missing its lenses, but clearly the framework of a lantern for the top of a lighthouse.** **The dust has been recently disturbed.** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]]'s most protected vault — her treasures and the secrets of her past.**
- **The journals** — **research journals Belcorra wrote in life** (**centuries old, kept intact by careful magical preservation**), **most recently shelved here (trails in the dust):**
  - They describe **the four [[fulcrum lenses]] — crimson, ebony, emerald and ochre — which focus [[Nhimbaloth]]'s soul-extinguishing gaze across space and time.**
  - **How she built the [[Gauntlight]] and made a special framework — the *fulcrum lattice* — to hold the four lenses and harness their power.** **A diagram shows the device here is that lattice.**
  - **Her trepidation:** **each lens holds a sliver of Nhimbaloth's essence; close proximity is dangerous.** **The lenses impart Nhimbaloth's essence to those who use or are touched by them; having all four essences at once is sure to bring Nhimbaloth's dreadful scrutiny.**
  - **Her apprentice [[Volluk Azrinae]]** ("Valuk Azdayne") **made a mundane lens so the Gauntlight could work at diminished capacity until the lattice and true lenses were installed.**
  - **On the eve of the assault** the lattice would let one person **carry all four large, heavy lenses with ease.**
  - **Distribution:** **she uses only the Ebon lens, in the [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare|Empty Vault]] at the Gauntlight's base (one lens = minimal risk).** **She gave the others to trusted groups: Emerald → the urdefhan; Ochre → the drow; Crimson → hidden where her undead gnome minions could guard it.** **She trusts the groups won't cooperate — much less learn to combine the lenses to imbue one creature with all four splinters, which would "impart total annihilation."**
- **Other books:** **aging occult tomes long in the Haruvex family — lore on ghosts, Nhimbaloth and the Haruvexes.** **A hero who peruses them for at least an hour gets a +2 item bonus on Recall Knowledge checks on those subjects when using *Investigate* here.**
- **Treasure:** **a skull-shaped key** (value cut off in the captions ⚠F9) **that unlocks the Gate of Nhimbaloth (I60)** · **two signet rings (a tentacle wrapped around a skull with a moss-filled mouth): one is a ring of lies, the other a greater pendant of the occult** · **the fulcrum lattice** — **surprisingly light for its size; any lenses the heroes have found fit neatly and add no weight.**
- **XP: 80** for **uncovering the lens lore and learning how to destroy Belcorra.**

## The Gate of Nhimbaloth (I59–I64)

### I59. Level 9 Portal Chamber · no encounter
▶ [1:25:09](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=5109)

- **Spiraling silver-filled runes cover the curved walls; the same silvery metal covers floor and ceiling.** **Another inactive teleportation circle** — connect it to the network with ***awaken portal*.**
- **XP: 30** for reactivating it.

### I60. Ebon Doors · no encounter
▶ [1:25:41](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=5141)

- **Sensory:** **massive double doors of black and green stone; an oxidized bronze lock with a keyhole but no handles.** They lead to **the Gate of [[Nhimbaloth]] — an ancient temple of black stone streaked with a nauseating greenish hue.** **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] didn't build it — she found it and folded it into her dungeon.** **Whether it always lay underground or was cast down from the surface is a mystery, as are the cultists who once lived there.** **Its entry is now seamless with the Vaults' stone.** **The Gate includes rooms beyond the door *and most of the lower level (Level 10).***
- **The lock:** **incredibly complex — six successes at DC 40 Thievery; the door can't be forced.** **The skull-shaped key from I58 opens it;** **when it does, the key's carving changes to show moss sprouting from the skull's mouth.**

### I61. Hall of Priests · Moderate 9
▶ [1:27:00](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=5220)

- **Sensory:** **a large chamber of the same black stone as the ebon doors.** **Eight alcoves (four per side) along the east and west walls hold the remains of jade-colored statues — all defiled: none has a head, most are missing hands, and one is just a pair of bare feet on a pedestal.** **An enormous stone throne carved from heavy blocks stands in a dome chamber opposite the black doors.**
- **History:** **long before [[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] it was a semi-public place of worship for a [[Nhimbaloth]] cult.** **Those seeking vengeance, an enemy's obliteration, or assurance that lost loved ones would return as ghosts left offerings and sacrifices around the throne — and the creature on it, the *[[Lady's Whisper]]*.** **Aspirants hoped the shrouded skeleton would rise and lead them through one of three doorways to a test for initiation.** **It sometimes sat still for years, or ushered in new cultists for days on end.** **Even senior cultists never understood its link to Nhimbaloth, but none questioned its role as gateway.** **What the cultists didn't know: only those it ushered through the *eastern* door joined the cult; the *western* doors led to a grim fate for those unworthy even of testing.**
- **Creature — the Lady's Whisper:** **a unique undead, distantly related to the lesser deaths that serve the Grim Reaper; for reasons long lost it's irrevocably tied to Nhimbaloth.** **It knows the heroes are here to face Belcorra and are ill-suited to join the (now nonexistent) cult — yet decides to put them through an initiation trial anyway, hoping to weaken and kill them.** **It commands many dread wisps within the Gate, but none guard this room when the heroes arrive, so it proceeds cautiously:** **it rises and holds out both hands, gesturing for peace; points at the eastern doors and whispers each hero's name in turn; steps toward the eastern door, gesturing them to follow.**
  - **If the heroes attack:** it **lashes out with its claws to teleport them away — preferably into adjacent chambers with dangerous wisps and traps.**
  - **It flees if it takes more than 50 damage**, **luring the heroes deeper into the Gate as it goes.**
- *(This is the Lady's Whisper that sent [[Sacuisha]] to [[floor-07-soul-keepers|Floor 7]] — [[floor-07-soul-keepers#G21. The Secret Void · Moderate 7|G21]].)*

### I62. Oblivion Fall · hazard
▶ [1:30:15](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=5415)

- **A dark-stone passage opens into a rectangular chamber.** **Hazard: the room has no floor — a bottomless pit** that, **unlike the Core Rulebook hazard, has no trapdoor** — **yet, set in reflective black stone, it's still hard to notice.** **The [[Lady's Whisper]] led aspirants it judged useless to [[Nhimbaloth]] into this pit — by force when necessary.**

### I63. Garden of Failures · Trivial 9
▶ [1:31:00](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=5460)

- **A black-stone passage opens into an octagonal chamber — a riot of foul-smelling swamp weeds.** **Creatures who reached the Gate but were too afraid in their hearts to attempt the initiation tests below were brought here.** **The [[Lady's Whisper]] comforted them with words about simply resting and departing in peace — a lie:** **they were killed here, their blood and essence feeding the cavern's sunless garden.**
- **Creature:** **a single dread wisp floats here, desperate to feed for the first time in many, many years.**

### I64. Initiates' Chamber · Severe 9
▶ [1:31:44](https://youtu.be/itVWlIpgP_0?t=5504)

- **Sensory:** **black-stone walls with four large alcoves (two east, two west).** **Three hold large stone basins of reeds, moss and other swamp plants; the fourth basin is empty.** **Stairs lead down to the north — to [[floor-10-to-draw-the-baleful-glare#J1. First Challenge · no encounter — Challenge of Souls begins|J1]] (Level 10).**
- **Creatures — three dread wisps** hover over the three basins (**the fourth was slain long ago; its basin holds only desiccated soil**).
  - **If the heroes are with the [[Lady's Whisper]]:** **two of the wisps follow them at a distance, ready to intervene if the heroes turn violent.**
  - **If not: the dread wisps attack.**
- *(Each occupied basin is a wisp-to-Belcorra link — see [[floor-08-decaying-gardens|Floor 8]]'s **[[Belcorra Haruvex|Belcorra]] Attacks!**.)*
