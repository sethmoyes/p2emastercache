## Services

- Local delivery (within [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]): 1 cp per Bulk, generally delivered within 24 hours
- Deliveries to [[locations/absalom|Absalom]], Diobel, or points beyond: 5 cp per Bulk, delivered weekly
- Expedited delivery: 1 gp per Bulk, arrives in two days
- Stabling and transportation for caravans or carriages
- Special orders: Oloria can probably acquire anything not sold in Otari, though the price may be steep
