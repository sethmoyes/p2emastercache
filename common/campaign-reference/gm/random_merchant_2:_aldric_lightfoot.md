# Random Merchant 2: Aldric Lightfoot

<div align="center">

*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This secretive gnome merchant lost their parents at a young age. In their youth, they was imprisoned for a crime. They had a wise teacher who greatly influenced them. At one point, they caused suffering through inaction, motivated by jealousy. Now they are on a pilgrimage.*

**Proprietor:** Aldric Lightfoot (Gnome Merchant)

**Specialties:** General goods and supplies
