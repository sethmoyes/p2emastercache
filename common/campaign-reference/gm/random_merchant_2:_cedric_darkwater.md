*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This friendly halfling priest grew up among peasants, working the land. Their parents were serfs/peasants. In their youth, they was mentored by a skilled teacher. They befriended a traveling merchant or minstrel who greatly influenced them. At one point, they stole something valuable, motivated by love. Now they are on a pilgrimage.*
