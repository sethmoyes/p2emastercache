# Random Merchant 2: Hilda Proudfoot

<div align="center">

<img src="https://2e.aonprd.com/Images/Monsters/Merchant.webp" alt="Proprietor" width="250">

</div>

*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This proud dwarf craftsperson was born to privilege among the nobility. Their parents were serfs/peasants. In their youth, they was mentored by a skilled teacher. They was close to a family member who greatly influenced them. At one point, they lied to further their goals, motivated by religion. Now they are on a pilgrimage.*

**Proprietor:** Hilda Proudfoot (Dwarf Craftsperson)

**Specialties:** General goods and supplies
