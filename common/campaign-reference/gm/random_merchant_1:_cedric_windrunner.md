# Random Merchant 1: Cedric Windrunner

<div align="center">

<img src="https://i.redd.it/mr6j0g84qplc1.jpeg" alt="Proprietor" width="250">

</div>

*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This curious dwarf craftsperson was born to privilege among the nobility. Their parents were yeomen. In their youth, they was betrayed by a trusted friend or family member. They was close to a family member who greatly influenced them. At one point, they publicly embarrassed someone, motivated by justice. Now they are searching for a lost relative.*

**Proprietor:** Cedric Windrunner (Dwarf Craftsperson)

**Specialties:** Rare and exotic goods
