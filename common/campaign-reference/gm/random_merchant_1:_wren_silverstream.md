# Random Merchant 1: Wren Silverstream

<div align="center">

<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/dc/Wren.jpg" alt="Proprietor" width="250">

</div>

*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This secretive half-elf thief grew up among peasants, working the land. Their parents were merchants. In their youth, they lost someone close. They was influenced by a holy person who greatly influenced them. At one point, they lied to further their goals, motivated by religion. Now they are looking for employment.*

**Proprietor:** Wren Silverstream (Half-Elf Thief)

**Specialties:** Food and provisions
