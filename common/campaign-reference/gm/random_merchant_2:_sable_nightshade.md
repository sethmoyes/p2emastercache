*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This friendly gnome thief was born to privilege among the nobility. Their parents were soldiers. In their youth, they was imprisoned for a crime. They befriended a traveling merchant or minstrel who greatly influenced them. At one point, they betrayed someone's trust, motivated by money. Now they are seeking adventure.*
