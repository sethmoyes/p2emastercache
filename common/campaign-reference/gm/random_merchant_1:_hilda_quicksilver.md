*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This friendly halfling guard raised by another family. Their parents were entertainers. In their youth, they was imprisoned for a crime. They was close to a family member who greatly influenced them. At one point, they failed someone who depended on them, motivated by family. Now they are on a pilgrimage.*

**Proprietor:** Hilda Quicksilver (Halfling Guard)

**Specialties:** Rare and exotic goods
