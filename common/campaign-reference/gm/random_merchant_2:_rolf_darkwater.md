# Random Merchant 2: Rolf Darkwater

<div align="center">

<img src="https://2e.aonprd.com/Images/Monsters/Merchant.webp" alt="Proprietor" width="250">

</div>

*A traveling merchant encountered during an encounter. This talkative dwarf scholar grew up among peasants, working the land. Their parents were sailors. In their youth, they was kidnapped and later escaped. They had a significant romantic relationship who greatly influenced them. At one point, they failed someone who depended on them, motivated by love. Now they are searching for a lost relative.*

**Proprietor:** Rolf Darkwater (Dwarf Scholar)

**Specialties:** Tools and equipment
