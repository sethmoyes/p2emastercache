# Absalom — Master Gazetteer & Visceral Compendium

"The City at the Center of the World." Everything the party needs for the
road there and the city itself lives in this one file — reference, ambience,
and art all in one place, no cross-referencing required.

**Every district header below (Outer Districts and City Districts, both
sections) is a direct link to that district's chapter** in Mythkeeper's
["Pathfinder Regional Deepdive: Absalom P2 — The City Gazetteer"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck),
pulled straight from the video's own published chapter list, right down to
individual landmarks within each district — click any linked name, land
right where it's covered. A few landmarks near the end of the transcript
I had access to are marked *(timestamp not located)* — for those, or for
anything that drifts if YouTube re-cuts the video, open the video, click
"Show transcript," and search the name; YouTube jumps you straight there.

Every district also carries a **🎧 Immersion** line right under its title —
one or two ambience tracks to start playing the moment the party arrives,
picked for that district's specific mood. Click and go.

**Naming note:** one table's recap called the destination "the Arcanium,"
but the encounter content and gazetteer below both consistently name it the
**Arcanamirium** (the arcane university founded by Nex, in the Wise Quarter).
Treat these as the same place — "Arcanium" was almost certainly recap
shorthand/drift, not a second institution.

## Running This at the Table

**Ambience:** one track per scene, started a beat before you begin
describing it, low enough to talk over. Don't reset it between beats in the
same location — let it run under the whole scene. Every ambience link
below was actually looked up, not guessed. YouTube churns, though — if one
goes dead, the mood label next to it will get you back to something close
in ten seconds.

**Channels worth bookmarking directly** (huge, well-produced back catalogs
— faster than re-searching every session):
- [Michael Ghelfi Studios](https://www.youtube.com/@MichaelGhelfiStudios/videos) — the biggest TTRPG audio library out there; seamless 5–8 min loops for basically every scene type in this doc.
- [Tabletop Audio](https://tabletopaudio.com/) — not YouTube, but built exactly for this: ad-free, loopable, browsable by scene.

**The source video:** this gazetteer was built by analyzing Mythkeeper's
"Pathfinder Regional Deepdive: Absalom" series:
- [Part 1 — History & Politics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TJ82Ail_l6g)

- [Part 3 — The Starstone Isles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bl4C5qNt6PQ)

**Visuals:** for a still image, pause the video live and let the TV display
the frame while you talk over it — that's just watching a video you have

- [PathfinderWiki: Artwork from Absalom, City of Lost Omens](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Category:Artwork_from_Absalom,_City_of_Lost_Omens) — official Paizo art from the Absalom sourcebook. **Start here.**

## Quick Reference

- **What the party wants here:** the **[[Roseguard Relics#Thresholds of Truth|Thresholds of Truth]]**, the last of
  the four [[Roseguard Relics]], believed to be at the **[[Arcanamirium]]** (Wise
  Quarter). Entry secured via [[Morlibint]]'s letter of recommendation.
- **Getting in:** the main road from [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]] brings the party through the
  **Cairnlands** to **Gate 210, the Sally Port** — the western gatehouse
  (also listed under Westgate below).
- **Tone:** hyper-legalistic, money-driven, monumentally huge. Everything
  about Absalom should feel like it dwarfs Otari by orders of magnitude —
  scale is the point.

## The Road to Absalom (Encounters)

Three days of travel, one encounter per day, building toward the city.

### Day 1: The Caravan of Currency (Abadar & Commerce)

**🎧 Immersion:** [Fantasy Marketplace Ambience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yK92fY33FJ4) · [Medieval City Sounds | Market Day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-b5nMCj5hI)

**Theme:** [[Abadar]] (God of Cities, Wealth, and Law) & the scale of Absalom's economy.
**Purpose:** introduce Abadar as the divine engine behind the largest
trading hub on the planet — a social/roleplay intro to Absalom's pure
capitalism.

**Encounter — The Broken Scale:** The party finds a beautifully constructed,
gold-trimmed luxury wagon broken down on the road — not a normal merchant,
but a mobile, fortified vault belonging to the Church of Abadar. A
lower-ranking cleric, **[[Tax-Collector Justin]]**, and four heavily armored
guards on armored horses are trying to secure it. Justin is having a mild
panic attack: the wagon carries official tax revenue and certified
weights/measures from the logging camps, bound for the Bank of Abadar.

- **The interaction:** Justin won't let anyone touch the wagon (paranoia),
  but badly needs a mending spell or a physical escort while it's fixed. He
  speaks entirely in economic metaphors ("By the Golden Vault!", "Time is
  copper, friends").
- **The payoff:** if helped, Justin rewards the party with a Sticker of
  Balanced Weight or freshly minted Absalom coins, plus a tip: *"You're from
  [[00-campaign-overview-and-otari#The Town of Otari|Otari]]? Sweet simple folk. Listen to me: in Absalom, Abadar's laws dictate
  everything. If you trade, you pay the tithe. If you break a contract, the
  First Guard will throw you so deep in the Brine Grotto you'll forget what
  the sun looks like. Keep your ledgers clean and your coins untampered."*
- **The takeaway:** Absalom isn't just big — it's a hyper-legalistic
  powerhouse run entirely on money and divine bureaucracy.

### Day 2: The Traveling Apprentice (The Arcanamirium Focus)

**🎧 Immersion:** [1 Hour Fantasy Adventure Music | Kingdoms](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sm1Cd0ts-tE)

**Theme:** the [[Arcanamirium]] (the wizard college) & practical magic.
**Purpose:** directly sets up the party's actual destination — a contact
from the exact academy they're heading to, and a preview of how its wizards
operate.

**Encounter — The Floating Bookworm:** A stressed teenager in fine lavender
robes is walking backward down the middle of the road, six massive
leather-bound spellbooks floating around him, frantically scribbling on a
piece of hovering parchment. This is **Apprentice Elian**, an Arcanamirium
student sent on a mandatory "Practical Field Study" to catalog regional
flora — completely lost, totally overwhelmed.

- **The interaction:** Elian walks straight into a party member or a muddy
  ditch, not looking where he's going. Frantic about his "Maven"
  (upperclassman supervisor) and getting flunked if his notes don't make it
  back to the Wise Quarter by tomorrow.
- **The payoff:** Elian is a fountain of exposition: *"The Arcanamirium
  doesn't care about your theoretical nonsense — they want practical
  results. If you're going there to borrow a book, you better have a good
  reason or a heavy purse. The Headmasters don't just hand over manuscripts
  to anyone off the street. They treat knowledge like gold. But hey... if
  you guys protect me for the next few miles, I can tell you which side-gate
  at the academy is easiest to slip into!"*
- **The takeaway:** a direct preview of the Arcanamirium (values "practical
  application" over theory), plus a minor student contact who can help
  navigate the campus on arrival.

### Day 3: The Cairnlands Checkpoint (The Combat Encounter)

**🎧 Immersion:** [Battlefield Ghosts | Fights, Undead, Memories, Unrest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AeyuFHjRgGA) · [Wasteland | Desolate Fantasy Ambient Music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GHkPqkxy5vY)

**Theme:** the Cairnlands — the wasteland graveyard of failed sieges
surrounding the city.
**Purpose:** a punchy combat encounter set against Absalom's terrifying
military history.

**Encounter — The Scavengers of the Siege-Tower:** The highway enters the
Cairnlands — a vast, ash-choked plain ringing Absalom, littered with the
ruins of broken siege engines and battlefields from thousands of years of
armies that tried and failed to take the city. The road runs alongside the
massive, half-buried wooden skull of an ancient colossal siege tower. A band
of **Cairnland Scavengers** (aggressive mutant gnolls, or battlefield-fed
ghouls both work) erupts from its rotted framework to strip the party of
their gear.

- **Combat twist:** terrain choked with rusty spikes, broken catapult
  stones, and trenches — the monsters use the collapsing siege machinery's
  verticality to rain down attacks or drag players into the ruins.
- **The payoff:** after the fight, from this high point in the Cairnlands,
  the party finally sees it — Absalom's massive, sprawling, impossibly high
  outer walls. Every ruin in this wasteland was an army that thought it
  could take the city and failed. Frames Absalom as an impenetrable,
  legendary bastion right before the party reaches the gates.

## Arrival: First Sight of the City (read-aloud sequence)

**🎧 Immersion:** [Large City | D&D/TTRPG Music | 1 Hour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ddMSMwKQkKI) · [RPG | D&D Ambience - City Gates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u6q2mjSNuU0) · [Medieval City | Realistic Ambience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_52K0E_gNY0) — start with the first as they crest into view, switch to the City Gates track once they're in the crowd at the Sally Port.

Five beats, meant to be read/paraphrased aloud as the party approaches and
enters. Each ends with a GM pause prompting a reaction — don't rush past them.

**1. The Graveyards of Ambition**

> The treeline finally breaks, revealing a monument to utter ruin. To your
> right, the coastline chokes under the Flotsam Graveyard, where hundreds of
> thousands of shattered galleons groan in the surf like dead leviathans. To
> your left lie the Cairnlands, a scarred wasteland of rusted, tavern-sized
> siege towers leaning at terrifying angles. The earth beneath your boots
> isn't dirt; it is a continuous, jagged sea of iron splinters and millions
> of bleached bones crushing into dust.

*GM Pause: "The wind is whistling through the ribcages of forgotten armies.
How are your characters reacting to walking through this miles-wide field of
death?"*

**2. The Horizon of the Gods**

> As you push through the whistling bone-dust, the road begins to choke with
> swarms of trade caravans, shouting merchants, and armored guards. Before
> you even see the city gates, your eyes are dragged upward to impossible,
> god-built titans anchoring the skyline. Miles out in the northern wastes to
> your far left, a flawless, mile-high needle of gray stone bends the very
> clouds around it. On the far right, deep within the city walls, a massive
> monolith of pale blue coral pulses with an ancient, rhythmic arcane hum.

*GM Pause: "These are structures built by legendary archmages. Who is
stopping to stare, and does anyone want to roll a Recall Knowledge to
identify these world wonders?"*

**3. The Sentinels**

> Right next to the blue coral monolith, a brutal stone defensive keep
> crowns the eastern skyline, its peak ablaze with an eternal fire watch.
> Out at the far edge of the sea, a staggering 655-foot lighthouse pierces
> the mist, its blinding magical beacon cutting dozens of miles into the
> water. They are man-made mountains whose cold shadows stretch for literal
> miles, completely swallowing the road, the shantytowns, and the travelers
> walking beneath them.

*GM Pause: "You've gone from fighting in muddy swamps to standing in the
shadows of architecture that defies scale. Do you keep your head down and
push through the gloom, or are you taking a moment to take it in?"*

**4. The Jaws of "Best Gate"**

> The packed highway funnels you through a sprawling shantytown of makeshift
> tents and wooden shacks pinned directly against the city's flank. You
> stand before Gate 210 — the Sally Port — an oppressive wall of ancient
> stone utterly devoid of greenery. With a deafening, bone-rattling grind of
> massive chains, four colossal, sixty-foot drawbridges slam down across the
> ditch into the earth. Crossing the threshold, the air shifts instantly to
> the roaring, claustrophobic chaos of absolute urbanization.

*GM Pause: "The noise hits you like a physical punch. Do you cover your
ears, keep your hands on your weapons, or push forward into the crowd?"*

**5. The Lady of the Gate**

> Inside the vaulted gatehouse, heavily armored cavalry maneuver warhorses
> down wide stone ramps at terrifying, breakneck speeds. Far above this
> deafening military chaos, framed by a sumptuously decorated balcony
> window, sits the Lady of the Gate. She is lazily sipping dark wine from a
> jeweled chalice, looking down at the endless, desperate tide of humanity
> pouring into her city. To her, your party is just another drop in the
> current.

*GM Pause: "You are officially inside the City at the Center of the World.
Where is the party heading first?"*

## I. The Outer Districts & Suburbs

### 1. The Suburbs (Shoreline, Westerhold, Copperwood, & Dawnfoot)
▶ [Watch this section](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=0s)

**🎧 Immersion:** [Fantasy Marketplace Ambience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yK92fY33FJ4) · [Medieval City Sounds | Market Day](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o-b5nMCj5hI)

> Read aloud on arrival: *The road here doesn't feel like a city yet — more
> like every logging town you've ever passed through, just endless. Sawdust
> hangs in the air thick enough to taste, hammers ring from a dozen
> smithies at once, and every building you pass sits in the permanent
> shadow of a wall you still can't see the top of.*

**The Visceral Feel:** Heavy with woodsmoke, fresh sawdust, tanneries, and
water-mill pulp. Rhythmic pounding of smithies and axes; dust kicked up by
endless trade wagons. Grounded, industrious, and perpetually living in the
literal shadow of the outer walls.

**Locations & Landmarks:**
- **[Shoreline](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=59s):** Industrial town outside the walls with paper mills,
  tanneries, and breweries.
  - **[Pyramid of the Dog](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=104s):** Dilapidated siege castle serving as HQ for the
    Warhounders gang, led by Nessian.
- **[Westerhold](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=111s):** 2,000-year-old Dwarven-founded roadside settlement.
  - **[Crystal Creations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=142s):** Glassblowing shop hiding the secret surface
    entrance to Cowsworn Thieves Guild tunnels.
- **[Copperwood](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=158s):** Ancient laborer encampment turned trades town; residents
  ("Copperheads") work as guides/porters.
- **[Dawnfoot](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=204s):** Military family town built around Starwatch Keep.
  - **[Dawn Castle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=227s):** Converted 1298 AR siege castle used as town hall and
    military graduation hall.

**Key NPCs:** Nessian (Warhounder boss); Deepkeg Family descendants.

**Local Drama:** Smuggling through subterranean Dwarven tunnels beneath
Crystal Creations; Warhounder banditry creeping in from the Puddles.

### 2. Starwatch Keep & Pilot Island
▶ [Watch this section](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=0s)

**🎧 Immersion:** [RPG Ambience - Pirate Harbour](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h8dL9Igch60) · [Harbor Sounds | Seaside Market Ambience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=frEJTGfLOhM)

> Read aloud on arrival: *The wind turns sharp and briny the moment you
> crest the ridge, salt spray stinging your eyes. Somewhere above,
> something with wings that shouldn't fly that well screeches past — and
> out past the breakers, a beam of light wide enough to swallow a ship
> sweeps slowly across the water, like it's looking for something.*

**The Visceral Feel:** Freezing salt spray, ozone, and roaring waves.
Shouted naval commands and screeching military griffons overhead. Warmth
from a blinding 655-foot beam sweeping across the harbor.

**Locations & Landmarks:**
- **[Pilot Island & Absalom Lighthouse](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=293s):** Tallest structure in Absalom
  (655 ft), forged by [[Aroden]].
- **[Starwatch Keep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=244s):** Massive naval base and investigation hub, isolated
  during peacetime.
- **[Walled Docks & Aviaries](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=276s):** Houses Wave-Riders (hippocampus cavalry) and
  military griffon squadrons.

**Key NPCs:** First Harbormaster **[[Goodman Hugen|Scion Lord Goodman Hugen]]** of [[House Candren]] (High Councilor; owns the Sea King Shipyards); in canon his ship sinks in 4719 AR and his protégé **Adrielle Neprathep** succeeds him.

**Local Drama:** Anti-piracy operations, harbor pilot escort laws, and naval
defense during sieges.

### 3. The Flotsam Graveyard
▶ [Watch this section](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=0s)

**🎧 Immersion:** [Harbor Sounds | Seaside Market Ambience](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=frEJTGfLOhM) — or, for the wrecks themselves, [Battlefield Ghosts](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AeyuFHjRgGA) leans into the graveyard-of-ships eeriness.

> Read aloud on arrival: *The smell reaches you before the sight does —
> kelp and rot and something wetter, worse. Then the water clears enough
> to show you why: a forest of broken masts breaking the surface for
> miles, thousands of ships that never made it home, still groaning
> against each other in the swell.*

**The Visceral Feel:** Gag-inducing rot of kelp, waterlogged oak, and
stagnant brine. Groaning timbers and black water slapping against thousands
of sunken warships.

**Locations & Landmarks:**
- **[The Shifting Labyrinth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=357s):** Shifting underwater maze of 4,000 years of
  sunken siege fleets.
- **[Azarketi Enclaves & Reefs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=407s):** Coral-covered wrecks inhabited by Azarketi,
  grindylows, and sea monsters.

**Key NPCs:** Salvagers Guild divers, Harbor Pilots.

**Local Drama:** Illegal crossings carry total forfeiture of ship and cargo
to the Primarch. Salvagers Guild constantly clears pathways while scuttling
confiscated ships.

### 4. Fort Tempest & Azlanti Keep
▶ [Watch this section](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=0s)

**🎧 Immersion:** [Large City | D&D/TTRPG Music](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ddMSMwKQkKI) · [RPG Ambience - City Gates](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u6q2mjSNuU0)

> Read aloud on arrival: *The walls here don't look built so much as
> grown — smooth white stone with no seams, no mortar, nothing your hands
> can find purchase on. The air inside tastes like a held breath, ozone
> and old blood and something that's been waiting patiently for five
> thousand years.*

**The Visceral Feel:** Dry ozone, stone dust, and old blood. Cavernous white
stone walls forged by [[Aroden]] that swallow sound and intimidate mortals.

**Locations & Landmarks:**
- **[Fort Tempest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=415s):** Converted siege castle of Carass the Angel Binder, with
  reinforced Dwarven cliff-foundations.
- **[Azlanti Keep](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=482s):** Ancient fortress with radiant crystal Arches built by
  Aroden; features 12 legendary magical siege defenses.

**Key NPCs:** Sergeant Salif Deepkeg (Fort Tempest); First Gear Simo
(Azlanti Keep); the Varlock (magical defenders).

**Local Drama:** Unlocking forgotten Arodenite siege defenses, like the
Crucible Directive.

## II. The City Districts

### 1. The Wise Quarter (Academic & Arcane Hub)

- **[Palace of 13 Spires](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1027s):** Foreign dignitaries' luxury rental estate —
  Chelish, Taldan, Osiriani, and Minkai wings.
- **[Silk Castle](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1155s):** Ancient kite shop run by Vitar Kusek; hosts the Festival
  of Ribbons.
- **[Learned Pig](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=986s):** Taproom stuffed with thousands of student books and
  scrolls.
- **[Scrivener's Square & The Inkwell](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1125s):** Print industry hub around a public
  fountain.
- **[Temple of Sivanah](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1211s):** Illusionist temple whose colors and architecture
  subtly shift weekly.
- **[Salhar Estate](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1312s):** Osiriani ambassador estate.
- **[Withun House](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1387s):** Drumac Kalistrade business academy — secretly an
  indoctrination center.
- **Other institutions:** [Absalom Academy of Law](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1242s), [Athanaeum Museum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1267s), [Dotus
  Academy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1281s), [Gallery Rousta](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck&t=1297s).

**Key NPCs:** Nomarch Dawan Tour; Archmage [[Darchana Madinani|Lady Darchana of House Madinani]] ([[Arcanamirium]] archdean, Second Spell Lord); Nigel Aldain; Guard
Captain Menier "The Colossus"; Lord Kirkus of House Deak (Exchequer); Drem
Salhar.

**Local Drama:** High taxes on students; druid protests at the Menagerie;
