# Absalom — Immersion Kit

The "vibe doc." Ambience/music to play through a speaker and art to put on a
TV or pass around on a tablet while running [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] scenes, so the table
*feels* the scale of the place instead of just hearing it described.
Companion to [`absalom.md`](./absalom.md) — same folder, same city.

Every link below was actually looked up, not guessed — real videos that
exist right now. YouTube churns, though, so if one goes dead, the search
term next to it will get you back to something close in ten seconds.

## How to use this

- One ambience track per scene, started a beat before you start describing
  it, low enough to talk over.
- Don't reset it between beats in the same location — let it run under the
  whole scene.
- The district table below is a lookup, not homework: glance, click, go.

## The source video (play this, don't screenshot it)

This district gazetteer was originally built by analyzing **Mythkeeper's
"Pathfinder Regional Deepdive: [[locations/absalom|Absalom]]"** series — it's the same
district-by-district structure for a reason. Rather than static screenshots
(which I can't ethically pull frames from and hand you as image files —
that's redistributing someone else's copyrighted video/art), the better move
for tomorrow is to just cue the video up live on the TV and scrub to the
right chapter when the party reaches that district. Motion + his narration
beats a still image anyway.

- [Part 1 — History & Politics](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TJ82Ail_l6g)
- [Part 2 — The City Gazetteer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QCMQsNJ-mck) — **this is the one that matches the districts below.** Every district heading in [`absalom.md`](./absalom.md) is now a direct link to its chapter, pulled from the video's own published chapter list — click the title, land at the start of that district's coverage. No timestamp data exists for individual landmarks within a chapter; use the video's own "Show transcript" search for that.
- [Part 3 — The Starstone Isles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bl4C5qNt6PQ)

If you want still images specifically, the honest options are: (a) pause the
video live and let the TV display the frame while you talk over it — totally
fine, you're just watching a video you have every right to watch — or (b)
use the official art galleries linked below, which you're loading in a
browser tab yourself rather than me redistributing copies of.

## Visuals — official & source art

- [PathfinderWiki: Artwork from Absalom, City of Lost Omens](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Category:Artwork_from_Absalom,_City_of_Lost_Omens) — a real gallery of official Paizo art from the [[locations/absalom|Absalom]] sourcebook. **Start here** — this is the single best "put it on the TV" resource, and it's organized close enough to the districts above to just scroll to the right chapter.
- [Pathfinder Fandom Wiki: Artwork from Guide to Absalom](https://pathfinder.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Artwork_from_Guide_to_Absalom) — same idea, older sourcebook, more skyline/establishing shots.
- [Paizo store: Absalom, City of Lost Omens](https://store.paizo.com/pathfinder-lost-omens-absalom/) — the sourcebook itself; cover and preview art here.
- [Ekaterina Burmak's cover art for the Absalom sourcebook, on ArtStation](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/DAyBVR) — good single hero image for "you have arrived" moments.

For a specific district or building not well covered by the galleries
above, image-search `"<name>" Pathfinder Absalom art` (Google Images or
Pinterest) — most named locations here (Forae Logos, the Irorium, the
Starstone Cathedral, etc.) have official or fan art findable that way.
